## 💡 Perks & Trade-Offs

| What you get | What to watch out for |
|--------------|------------------------|
| Short flights (≤3 hrs) in Europe—less travel fatigue, quicker arrival. | Peak seasons (July-August) are crowded and expensive. |
| Beach + party = relaxed during the day, lively at night. | Late night noise restrictions in some places; transfers could be long. |
| More affordable destinations stretch your group budget further. | Flight variability; someone from northern UK may pay more. |
| Villas and large rentals are cost-efficient for a group. | Coordinating everyone’s preferences tough; expect some trade-offs. |

---

## ✔️ Final Tips for Success

- Use a simple voting method: shortlist 3-4 destinations, let everyone vote.
- Book both flights and accommodation well ahead for 20 people—availability drops fast.
- Look into villas or accommodations with communal space (dining room, living room) so you can hang out together.
- Think about group transport once you're there: is everything walkable, or are transfers needed?
- Plan one or two group-wide special moments—a fancy dinner, private boat, spa day, etc.—so it feels more than just another holiday.

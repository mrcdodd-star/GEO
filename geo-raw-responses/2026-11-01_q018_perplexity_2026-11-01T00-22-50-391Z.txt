--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
**Sometimes—but not automatically.** A specialist villa company can be worth the premium when you’re booking a high-stakes or complicated trip: a large group, children, a special occasion, or a remote villa where local help would matter. For a straightforward stay where you’re comfortable checking the property and dealing with the host, Airbnb may be better value.

- **Pay extra for support and curation:** Specialist agencies may vet homes, help with local logistics and provide a point of contact if something goes wrong. The trade-off is a smaller selection and often a higher price.[2]
- **Don’t assume “specialist” means better value:** Which? found Airbnb had a 77% customer score in its comparison, versus 74% for Villa Plus; its research also found package providers tended to cost more than accommodation-only options.[3]
- **Compare the exact villa, not just the brand:** Search by property name or photos, and compare the final price, cancellation terms, fees, cleaning charges, deposits and included services. A specialist’s price may include support or extras—or simply be higher.
- **Check what happens if things go wrong:** Ask who handles problems locally, whether the company or host is responsible, and what refund and cancellation protections apply. Don’t assume a villa-only booking has the same protections as a package holiday.

**Rule of thumb:** For a simple, low-risk booking, start with Airbnb and compare. For a costly group holiday where a missed detail or on-site problem would be a big headache, get a specialist quote too—and pay the premium only if its support and terms justify the difference.

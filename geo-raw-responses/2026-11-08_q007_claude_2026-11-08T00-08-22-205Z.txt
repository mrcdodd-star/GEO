Here's how I'd go about it.

## 1. Decide what level of staffing you want
- **Partially staffed:** 
usually includes daily housekeeping and perhaps breakfast, with a chef and extra staff on request.

- **Fully staffed:** 
most often a private chef, a housekeeper and a house manager or butler, and sometimes a gardener and a driver as well.


Partially staffed villas are often cheaper and more flexible. A fully staffed one is closer to a private hotel.

## 2. Use a specialist agency rather than a general portal
Agencies that handle staffed villas can tell you what is included before you pay. Options that came up:

These are the agencies' own claims, so compare a few. Small UK-based agencies are also worth a call, since they often know the properties first-hand.

## 3. Ask exactly what "chef" and "housekeeping" mean
Terms vary a lot between villas. As one example, 
the chef cooks breakfast and one other meal six days a week, with Wednesday off, and housekeeping comes every morning except Sunday.
 Check these points:
- **Which meals the chef cooks**, and on which days.
- **Housekeeping hours**, and whether it includes Sundays.
- **Food costs.** 
The cost of ingredients and wine is not generally included in the villa rental price.
 Groceries are often charged at cost.
- **Whether you can bring in your own chef.** 
Some accept no outside suppliers.

- **Staff gratuities** and any extra charges, such as utilities or end-of-stay cleaning.
- **Get it in writing** before you pay.

## 4. Choose your region
- **Tuscany** has the most staffed villas.
- **The Amalfi Coast, Lake Como and Sicily** have fewer, and they cost more.
- Book early for peak summer, since the best staffed villas go up to a year ahead.

## 5. Practical tips for UK travellers
- Check the agency's financial protection, such as ATOL (only relevant if flights are bundled), or its terms on deposits and cancellations.
- Ask about arranged airport transfers, and whether you'll need a car.
- Consider travel insurance that covers villa cancellations.

## Alternative: add a chef yourself
If you find a villa you love that has housekeeping but no chef, many agencies and concierge services can arrange one. 
Tuscany Now & More's concierge service can provide a personal chef, extra childcare and more.
 Chefs can often cook just for certain evenings, which can be cheaper than a full-time one.

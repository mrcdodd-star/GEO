## Key criteria for a good city commuter

For daily urban commuting, most reviewers and buying guides emphasize[1][5][8]:

- **Real city range:** 40–100+ miles per charge is ideal for typical daily use.
- **Charging practicality:** Whether you have home/work charging, and if you need fast or removable batteries.
- **Agility and weight:** Light, narrow bikes are easier in traffic and for parking.
- **Top speed:** 
  - ~45–60 mph is enough for *in‑city only*.
  - 70+ mph if you need short highway stretches or fast suburban roads.
- **Price and running costs:** From budget (under ~$3–4k) to premium (> $15k).
- **Comfort & practicality:** Upright ergonomics, decent suspension, and any storage.

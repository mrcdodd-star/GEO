--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
Oliver's Travels is a villa and holiday-home rental agency. Reviews of it are mostly positive, with a consistent set of complaints.

**Overall rating**
- 
Trustpilot UK shows a 4-star rating from about 3,618 reviews.

- 
The breakdown is about 78% five-star, 8% four-star, 3% three-star, 2% two-star and 9% one-star.


**What people praise**
- Booking is easy and the sales staff are helpful. 
One reviewer said a staff member liaised with the owner quickly and kept them informed throughout.

- Several reviewers are repeat customers. 
One said their holiday last year was so good that they went straight back to the website this year.

- 
Another said Oliver's had already done the searching and had better availability and pricing than a rival site.


**What people criticise**
- **Responsibility when something goes wrong.** 
One reviewer said Oliver's washes its hands of customer concerns and leaves you to sort it out with the owners.
 Another made a similar point. 
They said there was nothing luxury or handpicked about the villas, and that Oliver's takes your money and disclaims responsibility afterwards.

- **Whether properties are inspected.** 
One recent reviewer warned against booking a "luxury" villa and said Oliver's misrepresents some of its offerings.
 Check the actual listing details and recent guest photos or reviews of the specific property.
- **Older Covid-era refund disputes.** 
Tripadvisor forum posts describe deposit disputes and refused refunds when travel was restricted.
 These are several years old.

**Caveats**
- 
An older Tripadvisor poster suspected some Trustpilot reviews were fake.
 That is one person's opinion and I haven't verified it. Still, read the 1–3 star reviews as well as the 5-star ones.
- The smaller review sites in my results (britainreviews.co.uk and us-reviews.com) look less reliable to me than Trustpilot.

**Tips for booking from the UK**
- Read the terms on cancellation, deposits and who is liable for property problems.
- Pay by credit card if you can, which gives some protection under Section 75 for purchases over £100.
- Ask whether the specific villa has been inspected by their staff.
- Look for independent reviews of the property itself.
- Keep all communication in writing.

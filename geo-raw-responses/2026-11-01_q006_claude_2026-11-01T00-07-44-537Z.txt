Here are the companies that come up most often for UK travellers. I've grouped them by what they suit best.

**Large, well-regarded UK specialists**
- **Oliver's Travels** says 
its destination experts have reviewed over 100,000 holiday homes, and only the top 2% earn a place in the collection.
 It also says 
Condé Nast Traveller readers have voted it one of the best villa rental companies in the world for ten years running.
 It suits large groups and multigenerational trips. One roundup says 
its list includes French chateaux, Irish castles, and unique follies across Europe.

- **CV Villas** was 
the UK's first specialist villa company, founded in 1972.
 One comparison calls it an established name 
with a strong portfolio, particularly across the Mediterranean.

- **The Thinking Traveller** is described as 
offering detailed local guides and on-the-ground support.
 It has won several best-villa-company awards.
- **Simpson Travel** is noted for 
properties that come with a dedicated nanny service or crèche.
 That makes it a good fit for families with young children.

**Top end and ultra-luxury**
- **Abercrombie & Kent Villas** is Spear's "top recommended" pick. 
It offers 24/7 emergency support, with teams on the ground near each rental.

- **Icon Private Collection** and **Ultima Collection** also feature in Spear's list. 
Ultima offers ultra-luxury private residences across Europe.

- **Insider Villas** and **Simon Escapes** both appear in a high-end roundup, and both offer concierge services.
- **Le Collectionist** is 
a carefully curated collection of holiday rental properties in the world's most popular destinations.
 It started in France.

**Country specialists**
- **Tuscany Now & More** is a London-based Italy specialist. 
Travel + Leisure's World's Best Awards named it Best Luxury Villa Rental Company in 2023 and 2024.


**Good value**
- **Villa Plus** 
focuses exclusively on European destinations.
 A comparison site rates it as 
good value for European villa holidays.

- **The Villa Collection** is billed as 
best all-round for worldwide destinations, flights, and financial protection.
 Note that this comparison is published by The Villa Collection itself, so it isn't impartial.

**Tips**
- Check the company's financial protection. Look for ATOL if flights are included, or ABTA membership or package travel protection.
- Ask about staffing, such as a housekeeper, chef or concierge. It varies a lot between agencies.
- Book early for peak summer. The best villas in Italy, Greece and the South of France often go 9–12 months ahead.
- Airbnb Luxe is an option, but agencies usually give you more vetting and on-the-ground support.

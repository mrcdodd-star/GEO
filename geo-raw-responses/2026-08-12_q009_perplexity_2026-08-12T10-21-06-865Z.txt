The main difference is that **45 Park Lane** is the more **modern, intimate, boutique-style** sister property, while **The Dorchester** is the more **classic, historic, grand** hotel.[1][4][8][10]

- **45 Park Lane**: opened in 2011, sits directly across from The Dorchester, has **45 rooms and suites**, and is described as more contemporary, club-like, and art-deco inspired.[2][8][10][12]
- **The Dorchester**: opened in 1931, is one of London’s most iconic hotels, and is known for its **traditional luxury**, larger scale, and historic prestige.[4][8][17]
- **Atmosphere**: 45 Park Lane tends to feel more private, relaxed, and discreet, while The Dorchester feels more formal and classic.[1][9][14]
- **Dining**: 45 Park Lane is known for **CUT at 45 Park Lane** by Wolfgang Puck, while The Dorchester is famous for its high-end dining, including Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester.[2][17]
- **Facilities**: guests at 45 Park Lane can use some spa facilities at The Dorchester, and The Dorchester does not have an on-site pool, with pool access linked to 45 Park Lane across the street.[7][11][15]

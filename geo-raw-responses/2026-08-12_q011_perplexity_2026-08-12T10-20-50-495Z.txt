A practical way to choose:
- Choose **Claridge’s** if you want the hotel itself to feel like the main event: the lobby, the interiors, and afternoon tea are the big draws. [1][11][13]
- Choose **The Dorchester** if you want the strongest mix of park-adjacent setting and high-end dining, especially if the view matters to you. [1][13]

Both are widely recognized as top-tier London hotels, and both were included on *The Telegraph*’s 2024 Gold List of best hotels in the world, which underlines that this is a matter of style rather than quality gap. [8]

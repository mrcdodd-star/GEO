--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
London’s ultra-luxury hotel scene is a masterclass in blending historic, royal heritage with billion-pound modern masterpieces. If you are looking for absolute privacy, flawless service, and prestigious postcodes, the most exclusive luxury hotels in London are divided into two distinct leagues: **the cutting-edge, new ultra-luxury properties** and **the timeless historic institutions**.

---

### Part 1: The New Ultra-Luxury Icons (The Billion-Pound Club)
Over the last few years, London has witnessed a historic wave of ultra-luxury hotel openings that have completely raised the bar for price, space, and exclusivity. 

#### 1. The Chancery Rosewood (Mayfair)
* **The Vibe:** Mid-century diplomatic glamour meets contemporary high fashion.
* **Why it’s exclusive:** Opened in September 2025, this hotel is housed inside the iconic, Grade II-listed former United States Embassy on Grosvenor Square. Following an eight-year, £1 billion restoration led by Sir David Chipperfield, it is one of London's most talked-about addresses. 
* **Key features:** With Eero Saarinen’s original gilded eagle still perched on the façade, the hotel is designed to feel like an "all-suite" property (the smallest rooms start at an enormous 53 square meters). It hosts eight dining concepts—including the first European outpost of New York’s legendary Italian restaurant, *Carbone*—and a subterranean *Asaya Spa*.

#### 2. The Emory (Belgravia)
* **The Vibe:** Ultra-discreet, modern residential luxury for high-net-worth travelers.
* **Why it’s exclusive:** The Emory (by the Maybourne Hotel Group, the same operators as Claridge's and The Connaught) is London’s first all-suite hotel. Tucked away with a private, gated entrance off Old Barrack Yard, it functions as an incredibly private sanctuary where the public cannot easily wander in.
* **Key features:** Each of the 60 suites has been individually conceptualized by a different world-class designer. Guests get exclusive access to *Surrenne*, a highly private, four-floor wellness and longevity club, and can dine on-site at Jean-Georges Vongerichten's *abc kitchens*.

#### 3. The Peninsula London (Belgravia)
* **The Vibe:** Absolute grandeur, imposing architecture, and unmatched scale.
* **Why it’s exclusive:** Opened in late 2023 at Hyde Park Corner, this project was built from the ground up at a cost of over £1 billion. It bypasses London’s typical historic conservation restrictions to offer massive, impeccably engineered rooms (starting at 51 square meters) with state-of-the-art tech, mahogany dressing rooms, and acoustics designed for absolute silence.
* **Key features:** A private fleet of luxury vehicles (including a bespoke Rolls-Royce Phantom) to chauffeur guests around Mayfair, and the spectacular aviation-themed, Michelin-starred rooftop restaurant, *Brooklands*.

#### 4. Raffles London at The OWO (Whitehall)
* **The Vibe:** Regal, Edwardian baroque, and heavily steeped in British military history.
* **Why it’s exclusive:** Located in the monumental, Grade II* listed Old War Office—where Winston Churchill made key WWII decisions and Ian Fleming was inspired to write James Bond—this property underwent a five-year, multi-billion-pound renovation. 
* **Key features:** It features structural masterpieces like the spectacular Grand Staircase, a massive subterranean Guerlain Spa, and an array of fine dining options led by three-Michelin-starred chef Mauro Colagreco.

### Part 2: The Classic Elite Institutions (Grand Dames & Royal Favorites)
For those who prefer historic prestige, butler service, and a deep-seated connection to British high society, these legendary hotels remain unparalleled.

#### 5. The Connaught (Mayfair)
* **The Vibe:** The ultimate epicenter of "quiet luxury."
* **Why it’s exclusive:** Located in the heart of Mayfair, The Connaught is notoriously discreet, feeling more like an exceptionally upscale private club than a hotel. It is the premier choice for artists, designers, and dignitaries who want five-star luxury without the flashy crowds.
* **Key features:** It is home to *Hélène Darroze at The Connaught* (holding 3 Michelin stars) and the world-renowned *Connaught Bar*, frequently voted the best cocktail bar on the planet.

#### 6. Claridge’s (Mayfair)
* **The Vibe:** Art Deco majesty and high-fashion glamour.
* **Why it’s exclusive:** Often referred to as the "annexe to Buckingham Palace," Claridge’s has hosted kings, queens, and global icons for over a century. It represents the pinnacle of London's high society, from its legendary afternoon tea in the Foyer to its stunning, newly carved-out subterranean mega-spa.
* **Key features:** Beautifully restored historical suites blended with modern design touches, and a fiercely loyal staff that remembers every repeat guest's exact preferences.

#### 7. The Goring (Belgravia)
* **The Vibe:** Unabashedly, impeccably, and delightfully British.
* **Why it’s exclusive:** Opened in 1910, The Goring is the only hotel in the world to hold a Royal Warrant from the late Queen Elizabeth II for hospitality services. It is famously the British Royal Family's "hotel of choice"—Kate Middleton stayed here the night before her wedding to Prince William. It is also the last grand luxury hotel in London still owned and run by the family that built it.
* **Key features:** A sprawling, manicured private back garden (a massive rarity in Central London), impeccable footmen in scarlet coats, and a Michelin-starred Dining Room. 

#### 8. The Lanesborough (Knightsbridge)
* **The Vibe:** Regency-era palace luxury.
* **Why it’s exclusive:** Positioned magnificently on Hyde Park Corner, The Lanesborough is the only hotel in London that provides **round-the-clock, dedicated personal butler service to every single room and suite**. From unpacking your bags to arranging last-minute entry to private clubs, your butler handles everything.
* **Key features:** Lavish, museum-grade interiors decorated with 2,100 sheets of gold leaf, the highly exclusive *Lanesborough Club & Spa*, and their beloved resident hotel cat, Lilibet.

#### 9. The Ritz London (Piccadilly)
* **The Vibe:** Formal, neoclassical Edwardian opulence.
* **Why it’s exclusive:** Perhaps the most famous hotel name in the world, The Ritz maintains an intensely traditional, high-society atmosphere. It is one of the few hotels left that strictly enforces a formal dress code (jackets and ties for gentlemen in the dining areas). 
* **Key features:** The breathtaking William Kent House suites, the world-famous afternoon tea in the Palm Court, and one of the most stunning, gilded Michelin-starred dining rooms in Europe.

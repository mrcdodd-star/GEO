Here are the main UK-based options, grouped by what they suit. Which is best depends on where you want to go and how much service you want.

**Ultra-high-end and fully serviced**
- **Icon Private Collection**: 
Spear's lists it as "Top Recommended", covering villas, chalets and hotel residences.

- **Abercrombie & Kent Villas**: 
It offers 24/7 emergency support, with teams on the ground near each rental.

- **Ultima Collection**: 
It offers ultra-luxury private residences across Europe.

- **Insider Villas**: 
It specialises in luxury villas across Europe and beyond.


**Well-established all-rounders**
- **The Thinking Traveller**: This is a London-based specialist in Greece, Sicily, Puglia, Tuscany, Sardinia and Corsica. 
It says Condé Nast Traveller readers have voted it Best Villa Rental Company in the World 11 times.
 That is the company's own claim.
- **Oliver's Travels**: 
Its experts review over 100,000 holiday homes and only the top 2% make it into the collection.
 
It doesn't include flights, so it has no ATOL protection.

- **CV Villas**: 
It was the UK's first specialist villa company, founded in 1972.
 
Its portfolio is strong across the Mediterranean.
 
Its Caribbean and Asia offering is much more limited.

- **The Villa Collection**: 
It is billed as the best all-rounder for worldwide destinations, flights and financial protection.
 That comes from its own comparison page, so treat it with some caution.
- **Villa Plus**: Which? lists it under luxury villa specialists. 
It has lets across the Mediterranean, and its experts visit each villa five times before adding it.


**Destination specialists**
- **Tuscany Now & More**: 
This London-based, family-run company specialises in Italian villas. Travel + Leisure's World's Best Awards named it Best Luxury Villa Rental Company in 2023 and 2024.


**Staying in the UK**
Spear's also recommends 
Luxury Cotswold Rentals for heritage-rich estates and Living Architecture for architectural marvels.
 
Simon Escapes has high-end properties, including rustic UK locations, as well as options further afield.


**Tips**
- If you want flights included, choose a company that sells packages. That gives you ATOL protection, which villa-only companies like Oliver's Travels don't provide.
- Which? has survey data on overseas villa providers, including Jet2Villas, Tui and British Airways, if you want independent satisfaction scores. Some of its content may be behind a paywall.
- Several of the "best of" lists above are written by the companies themselves, so check recent Trustpilot reviews as well.

Both the Four Seasons Hotel London at Park Lane and The Dorchester are elite 5-star standard-bearers of Mayfair luxury, located just a few doors apart on Park Lane.
The choice comes down to vibe: Four Seasons Park Lane delivers an intimate, modern, and understated residential feel, while The Dorchester offers historic grandeur, high-energy British glamour, and legendary culinary prestige.
At a Glance Comparison

Atmosphere & Style
Originally opened in 1970 as the brand's first property outside North America, it functions like a slick, high-end private club. It features a bright design and a collection of 14 newly renovated terrace suites that mirror elegant Mayfair apartments.
This 1931 landmark completed a massive multi-million-pound property-wide renovation. It is known for its high-fashion "lobby catwalk" layout, theatrical floral arrangements, and deep Italian marble bathtubs. It is a bustling social hub where people go to see and be seen.
Dining & Bars
Home to Pavyllon London by multi-Michelin-starred Chef Yannick Alléno, which serves a unique Michelin-starred breakfast menu alongside modern French counter dining. Drinks are centered at the lively, chic Bar Antoine.
A global culinary destination. It hosts Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester (the only 3-Michelin-starred restaurant inside a UK hotel), alongside The Grill, the iconic Promenade for afternoon tea, and the legendary Vesper Bar.
Spa & Wellness
Holds a major advantage for wellness seekers. Its spa is located on the 10th-floor rooftop with a 360-degree glass-walled thermal circuit and treatment rooms that look directly down onto Hyde Park.

--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
Start with a UK-based villa specialist, then ask them to shortlist **staffed villas** in your preferred Italian region and dates. For example, CV Villas advertises Italian villas with cook service and has UK office hours; Home in Italy and Tuscany Now & More also list staffed options with cooking and daily maid service.[3][8][10]

Before booking, confirm in writing:

- **Chef service:** Is it a professional private chef or a cook? Which meals and how many days are covered?
- **Housekeeping:** Is it daily, how many hours, and are weekends included?
- **Extra costs:** Are groceries, drinks, chef fees, travel, or service charges extra? These can be separate from the rental.[1][4]
- **Practical details:** Exact dates, guest capacity, accessibility, air conditioning, pool, cancellation terms, and whether the villa is exclusive to your group.

Send an enquiry with your dates, group size, budget, preferred region, and meal requirements. Ask for a written, itemised quote and the staff schedule for the specific villa—not just a general “fully staffed” description, since services vary by property.[1][3]

For a concrete example, Villa Mare Blu near Praiano lists daily housekeeping and a chef for one meal per day, with groceries excluded; that illustrates why checking the exact inclusions matters.[4]

**Tips for a group of 12**
- Book soon. The best 12-sleepers for August tend to go early, and many agents take deposits a year ahead.
- Check the bed setup (6 bedrooms with en-suites is much easier than 5 with shared bathrooms). Also check whether the 12 includes sofa beds, and whether children count towards the limit. One listing, for example, caps guests at 
8 adults and 4 children

- Make sure the listing includes air conditioning in the bedrooms and a heated or shaded pool. Some listings have only partial air con.
- Look at extras such as a cleaning fee, a pool heating charge, a security deposit and tourist tax. A private chef or housekeeping can also be arranged.
- Check the distance to the beach and to a supermarket. A car is essential for most villas.

## 🌍 What “Fully Staffed” Typically Means

When a villa is described as *fully staffed*, this usually includes:

- A live-in or on-site villa manager or host
- Daily housekeeping
- Chefs or cook service (often customizable menus)
- Concierge assistance (arranging excursions, provisioning, pre-arrival planning)  
- Sometimes additional services like wellness, spa treatments, transport, etc.

Check exactly what’s included before booking. Some “staffed” villas charge extra for items like food and wine, airport transfers, etc.

## 💡 Tips for Booking & Getting the Most Out of It

1. **Decide your priorities** – Chef or cook service? Private transport? Pool and beach access? Size and number of bedrooms? Destination (Ionian vs Cyclades vs Crete etc.)  
2. **Check what’s included & what’s extra** – Some villas list as “fully staffed” but still charge separately for food, transfers or special services.  
3. **Travel logistics** – Some villas require additional travel (boat, private car). Be sure you know transfer times and costs.  
4. **Time of year matters** – Prices soar during July & August. Shoulder seasons like late-May/early-June or September are often better value with more availability.  
5. **Read reviews and verify authenticity** – Look for real guest reviews, verified photos, and whether the villa is properly licensed.

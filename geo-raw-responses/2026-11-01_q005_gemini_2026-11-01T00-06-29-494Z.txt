--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
If your primary goal is a holiday where you **don't cook, don't wash up, and get dedicated daily adult-only time**, you have several excellent options tailored for UK families. 

Depending on your travel style, budget, and whether you want to fly, here are the best holiday formats where both cooking and childcare are fully taken care of:

---

### 1. Luxury All-Inclusive Sun Resorts (Europe)
*Best for: Zero-friction sunshine, fine dining, and seamless childcare from babyhood upwards.*

* **Ikos Resorts (Greece & Spain):** Widely considered the gold standard for family all-inclusive in Europe. Michelin-starred curated menus, 24-hour room service, and beach childminding. Childcare is run by UK-accredited *Worldwide Kids* (Crèche from 4 months+; Kids Club 4+ years).
* **Sani Resort (Greece):** A massive eco-resort with 5 luxury hotels on site. Offers a "Dine-Around" programme across 26 restaurants, plus top-tier crèches, kids' clubs, and a dedicated *Babe Watch* service on the beach (graduates keep an eye on toddlers while you swim).
* **Martinhal Resorts (Portugal):** Purpose-built for families (Sagres and Cascais). Offers full-board options, dedicated children’s menus, baby-proofing packages, and crèches from 6 months old.
* **Club Med (Worldwide / Europe):** The pioneer of all-inclusive. Offers "Baby Club Med" (4–23 months) and "Petit Club" (2–3 years) with qualified early-years staff. Meals, snacks, drinks, and kids’ clubs are all bundled into one price.

---

### 2. UK Luxury Family Hotels (No Flying Required)
*Best for: Quick weekend breaks or domestic trips without airport stress.*

* **Luxury Family Hotels Group (Fowey Hall, Woolley Grange, Moonfleet Manor, New Park Manor):** Every stay includes **2 hours of complimentary Ofsted-registered childcare per night booked** in their on-site *Four Bears Den* (from 3–6 months to 8 years). They offer high tea for children early in the evening, followed by fine dining for parents later, backed up by audio baby-monitoring systems or babysitting.
* **Watergate Bay Hotel (Cornwall) & Another Place (The Lake District):** Both offer full restaurant dining on-site, kids' clubs, supervised swim sessions, and baby-monitoring facilities so you can eat dinner in the restaurant while your baby sleeps upstairs.
* **Crieff Hydro (Scotland):** Offers two free hours of supervised childcare per day for 2 to 12-year-olds, paired with multiple resort restaurants.

---

### 3. UK-Staffed Activity & Beach Specialists
*Best for: Active families wanting British-qualified nannies and flexible dining.*

* **Mark Warner (Greece & Turkey):** Famous among UK parents. They hire British-qualified early years staff (DBS-checked). Childcare runs from 4 months to 17 years with full-day or half-day options, alongside full/half-board hotel dining.
* **Neilson Active Holidays (Greece, Croatia, Sardinia):** Similar to Mark Warner—offers "Club Board" (most breakfasts, lunches, and dinners included), water sports, and British-run kids' clubs included in the base price for ages 2–17 (crèche available for babies under 2).
* **Simpson Travel:** Specialist boutique family hotels (such as *Mousses* in Lefkada, Greece) or catered family villas. They feature their own *Hatchling Crèches* and offer private nanny options.

---

### 4. Cruises Sailing from Southampton (Zero-Fly All-Inclusive)
*Best for: Unbeatable convenience—no airports, food on demand 24/7, and night nurseries.*

* **P&O Cruises (e.g., Arvia, Iona):** Sailing direct from Southampton means no luggage limits or airport security. All main dining, buffets, and kids’ teas are included. 
  * **The Big Perk:** P&O offers a complimentary **Night Nursery** (for ages 6 months to 4 years, running roughly 6pm–midnight/2am). You drop your sleeping baby off in a quiet, staff-monitored room with cots, take a pager, and go enjoy a multi-course dinner, drinks, or a show.
* **Disney Cruise Line (Europe/UK departures):** Outstanding nursery care (*It’s a Small World Nursery* for 6–36 months) and free kids' clubs for ages 3–12. Includes "rotational dining," where your personal waitstaff move with you to a different themed restaurant each night.

---

### 5. Catered Ski Chalets (Winter Option)
*Best for: Winter holidays where a chef cooks directly in your chalet.*

* **Family Ski Company, Ski Famille, or VIP SKI:** You stay in a chalet where a dedicated chalet host/chef cooks breakfast, afternoon tea, and a 3-to-4-course adult dinner every evening. Qualified UK nannies deliver in-chalet childcare, taking kids to ski school or doing activities while you ski.

---

### Key Tips for UK Parents Before Booking:

1. **Check the age cut-offs for free childcare:** For almost all operators, kids' clubs for ages 4+ (or sometimes 2+) are free. Under-2s usually require a paid **Crèche** spot, which must be booked early as ratios are tight.
2. **Understand the night-time dining setup:** 
   * *Night Nurseries / Crèches* (like P&O or Club Med) let you put kids to bed in a supervised room.
   * *Baby Listening Services* (like UK country house hotels) use room-monitoring technology connected to reception or your smartphone so you can eat downstairs while they sleep in your room.
3. **Check childcare qualifications:** If you want UK-standard care, look for operators using **Ofsted-registered staff** (in the UK) or **Worldwide Kids / DBS-checked British nannies** (abroad).

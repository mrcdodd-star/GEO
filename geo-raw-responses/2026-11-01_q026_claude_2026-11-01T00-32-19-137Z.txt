**Tips**
- **Booking schedule:** 
High-end villas typically require a one-week minimum stay, usually Saturday to Saturday, especially from May to September.
 Shoulder season and city rentals are often more flexible.
- **Booking early:** For peak summer you'll want to book many months ahead.
- **Extras:** Ask what's included. Cleaning, a pool or garden service, a welcome shop, a chef and transfers are often add-ons. Also ask about the security deposit and any tourist tax.
- **Payment and cancellation:** Check the payment schedule and cancellation terms. Consider travel insurance that covers cancellation.
- **Reviews:** Read recent independent reviews carefully. One travel blogger specifically advises that 
you look at the photos and all the reviews carefully
.

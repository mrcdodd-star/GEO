Here are some ideas, grouped by what kind of reunion you're after. I'm assuming you'd travel in 2027, so I've included some tips on timing.

## Best all-rounders for big groups

**Algarve, Portugal**
- One roundup calls it 
the most consistently family-friendly and large-group-friendly villa destination in Europe
.
- 
It has a strong supply of eight-, ten- and twelve-bedroom villas, often with multiple pools and tennis courts
. That helps if the cost is split across several households.
- It suits multigenerational groups. It's about 3 hours from the UK, with beaches, golf and easy day trips.

**Tuscany, Umbria or Puglia, Italy**
- This is the best fit if food, wine and culture matter as much as the pool. 
Essential Italy specialises in villas in these three regions, and they work well for large and extended families
.
- Puglia is often better value and has beaches nearby. Tuscany and Umbria are better for hilltop towns.
- Some estates take very big parties. 
One in Umbria sleeps up to 30
.

**Mallorca, Spain**
- It has 
easy access from the UK, a beautiful coastline, and villas that balance privacy with being close to towns
.
- It has lots of direct flights from regional UK airports, which helps if relatives are scattered around the country.

## Other options

- **Greek islands (Crete, Corfu, Kefalonia, Zante):** These are great for beach-and-taverna weeks. 
Crete has large beachfront villas with seven double bedrooms and separate family areas, which suits multigenerational groups
. Packages from operators like TUI also cover 
Corfu, Zante, Kefalonia and Rhodes, plus Spain and Portugal
.
- **Canary Islands (Lanzarote, Tenerife, Fuerteventura):** These have the most reliable warmth outside summer, so they work for Easter, half-term or autumn. The flights are longer, at about 4–4.5 hours.
- **Cyprus (Paphos):** It's warm into late autumn and has good family infrastructure. Villa prices are higher, though.
- **Resort-style villas:** If some of the group want kids' clubs and restaurants on site, look at places like Martinhal in Portugal. 
It has private villas plus access to resort pools and amenities
.

## Practical tips for a reunion

1. **Choose between a villa and a resort.** A villa gives you a shared space, a private pool and lower per-person costs. A resort or apartment complex is easier if people want flexibility and no cooking.
2. **Check flights first.** Make sure the airports near your villa suit everyone's UK departure points. Direct routes matter more than a perfect villa.
3. **Fix the dates early.** July and August prices peak and large villas book up early. Late May, June or September are cheaper and often more pleasant for grandparents.
4. **Look at the bedroom layout.** Several en-suite doubles with a couple of separate living areas work better than one huge open-plan space.
5. **Plan the budget fairly.** Splitting by bedroom or by household is usually simpler than splitting per head. Agree on this before booking.
6. **Check ABTA/ATOL protection** if you book through an operator.

## Rough costs

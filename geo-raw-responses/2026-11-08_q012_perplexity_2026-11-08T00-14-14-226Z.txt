Several UK-based villa specialists can coordinate **airport transfers, private chefs and activities** alongside the accommodation:

- **Elegant Resorts** — Arranges private transfers, chef hire, dining, boat trips, watersports and other activities. A good fit for a tailored, higher-end trip.[2][3]
- **ITC (Inspiring Travel Company)** — Offers a personal concierge for flights, transfers, chefs, excursions, restaurant bookings and other extras.[8]
- **GoToBeach** — A UK-based specialist with villas in Turkey, the Greek islands, Malta, Gozo and Marrakech; can arrange private transfers, chefs, excursions and boat charters.[6]
- **Firefly Collection** — UK-registered and focused on luxury villas and chalets; its concierge can arrange chefs, transfers, childcare, lessons and activities.[1]
- **The Thinking Traveller** — A good option for Mediterranean villas, with help arranging chefs, private transfers, car hire and experiences; services depend on destination and availability.[5]

For a single company to handle most of the trip, start with **Elegant Resorts or ITC**. For a more destination-focused option, compare **GoToBeach** or **The Thinking Traveller**. Ask each for an itemised quote showing what is included, what costs extra, and whether the chef and transfers are confirmed before you pay.

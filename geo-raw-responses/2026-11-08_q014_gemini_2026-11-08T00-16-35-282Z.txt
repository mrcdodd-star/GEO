### 1. Where to Stay in the Algarve (Best Areas for Groups of 12)

Depending on your group’s vibe (family holiday, multi-generational, groups of friends), here are the top regions:

*   **The Golden Triangle (Quinta do Lago / Vale do Lobo / Vilamoura)**
    *   **Vibe:** Luxury, quiet, high-end dining, world-class golf courses, immaculate beaches.
    *   **Best for:** Groups wanting high luxury, gated privacy, and close proximity to Faro Airport (~20 mins).
    *   **Villa style:** Large modern architecture, landscaped gardens, private tennis courts, infinity pools.
*   **Lagos & Praia da Luz (West)**
    *   **Vibe:** Historic coastal town, relaxed, vibrant town centre, stunning cliffside beaches (Ponta da Piedade).
    *   **Best for:** Mixed-age groups wanting a blend of culture, watersports, nightlife, and beaches.
    *   **Villa style:** Coastal countryside villas with sea views, ~50 mins from Faro.
*   **Carvoeiro & Ferragudo (Central-West)**
    *   **Vibe:** Picturesque cliffside fishing towns, dramatic coves (Benagil Cave nearby), family-friendly town squares.
    *   **Best for:** Families or friends looking for scenic views, great local seafood, and easy beach access.
*   **Albufeira (Outskirts) & Olhos de Água (Central)**
    *   **Vibe:** Lively, full of activities, great beach access, bustling nightlife in the town centre.
    *   **Best for:** Active groups, younger adults, or families who want to be close to nightlife and theme parks (Zoomarine, Slide & Splash).
    *   **Tip:** Look for villas in *Gale* or *Olhos de Água* on the quiet outskirts of Albufeira so you get peace at home but quick taxi access to the center.

### 3. Essential August Checklist for 12 Guests

When shortlisting villas for August in the Algarve, make sure to double-check:

1.  **Air Conditioning:** August temperatures frequently reach 30°C–35°C+. Ensure **air conditioning in every bedroom** is included (some charge extra per week, so check the fine print).
2.  **Bathrooms to Bedrooms Ratio:** Look for at least 4–5 bathrooms (ideally en-suite) to avoid morning/evening bottlenecks for 12 people.
3.  **Outdoor Living Space:** Check photos to verify there is **outdoor dining seating for 12** together under shade and enough sun loungers around the pool.
4.  **Pool Heating / Safety:** Heated pools usually aren't required in August, but if you have toddlers, look for gated/fenced pool areas.

### 4. Logistics & UK Travel Tips

*   **Flights:** Direct flights operate to **Faro (FAO)** from almost all major UK airports (London Heathrow/Gatwick/Stansted/Luton, Manchester, Birmingham, Bristol, Edinburgh, Belfast, etc.) via British Airways, easyJet, Ryanair, and Jet2.

    *   For 12 guests with luggage, pre-booking **two private 8-seater minibuses** (e.g., via Yellowfish Transfers or your villa host) is often much easier and cheaper than individual airport taxis.

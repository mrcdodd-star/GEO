Here are the top UK-based luxury and boutique villa rental companies known for offering **all-in-one concierge services**, including private chefs, airport transfers, and curated activities:

---

### 1. **The Thinking Traveller**
* **Best for:** Mediterranean villa holidays (Sicily, Puglia, Tuscany, Greek Islands, Corsica, Mallorca).
* **Concierge Services:** 
  * **Transfers:** Private driver airport pickups, car rentals, and boat transfers.
  * **Chefs:** Private in-villa cooks or Michelin-standard chefs tailored to your dietary needs.
  * **Activities:** "Think Experiences" offer private boat charters, guided historical tours, wine-tasting sessions, cooking masterclasses, and local hiking tours.
* **Why book with them:** London-headquartered, multi-award winning (*Condé Nast Traveller* winner), with deep local teams in every destination.

---

### 2. **Oliver’s Travels**
* **Best for:** Quirky castles, large family estates, and luxury villas across Europe and the Caribbean.
* **Concierge Services:**
  * **Transfers:** Private airport transfers, car hire, and chauffeur services.
  * **Chefs:** Dedicated private chef hire, fridge stocking, and catering for single dinners or full stays.
  * **Activities:** Wine tours, boat chartering, private yoga sessions, guided excursions, and child-friendly activities.
* **Why book with them:** Free dedicated VIP Concierge team attached to every booking.

---

### 3. **CV Villas**
* **Best for:** Classic European summer destinations (Greece, Italy, France, Spain, Portugal, Caribbean).
* **Concierge Services:**
  * **Transfers:** Private ground transfers, taxi arrangements, and car hire.
  * **Chefs:** In-villa catering ranging from occasional dinners to full-board private chefs.
  * **Activities:** Boat hires, day trips, spa treatments, watersports, and restaurant reservations.
* **Why book with them:** Over 50 years of experience catering to UK holidaymakers, providing seamless package options (including ATOL-protected flight+villa bundles).

### 6. **Simpson Travel**
* **Best for:** Family-friendly luxury and boutique stays in Greece, Turkey, Mallorca, Corsica, and Italy.
* **Concierge Services:**
  * **Transfers:** Seamless car hire collection at the airport or private transfer arrangements.
  * **Chefs:** Cooks available for booking on select properties or specific nights.
  * **Activities:** Local representatives organize private boat trips, guided walks, and local dining experiences.
* **Why book with them:** Renowned for customer service, ATOL protection on packages, and on-the-ground support.

### Useful Tips When Booking:
1. **Advance Booking for Chefs:** Private chef availability fills up quickly in peak season (July–August), so request chef services at the time of villa inquiry.
2. **Package Protection:** If you book flights alongside your villa and transfers through UK-based operators like *CV Villas* or *Simpson Travel*, your holiday will be **ATOL protected**.
3. **Flexible Concierge vs. Included Staff:** Check whether services are *included* in the weekly price (common in ultra-luxury properties) or *add-on concierge services* arranged separately.

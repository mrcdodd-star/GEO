### Quick Tips for Booking Add-Ons:
1. **ABTA & ATOL Protection:** If financial protection is a priority, booking package deals (flight + villa + transfer) through companies like *CV Villas* or *Simpson Travel* ensures ATOL protection.
2. **Book Chefs Early:** In peak season (July & August), local chefs book out fast. It is best to request chef days at the same time you confirm your villa booking.
3. **Understand Chef Pricing:** Most companies operate on a **service fee + cost of ingredients** model (where you pay the chef's daily fee and settle the grocery bill based on receipts locally), though some offer set per-person meal prices. Always clarify before booking.

## 🔍 Sample itineraries

Here are a few itinerary suggestions depending on what your group prefers.

### 1. **Beach-heavy & relaxing**
- Base: one resort or villa complex where you can rent multiple units/rooms.
- Daily rhythm: beach or pool in the morning, lunch together, afternoon nap/quiet time, evening BBQs or local food.
- Optional day trip: local market town, boat trip, or archaeological ruins.

**Good choice**: Canary Islands or Cyprus — excellent infrastructure and plenty of resorts still fully open even in October.

### 2. **Mix of culture + beach**
- Stay maybe 4 nights by the coast, 3 nights in a historic town or city.
- For example: Crete — stay in a beach resort 3–4 days; then relocate to a town like Chania or Rethymno for the old town, local markets, museums.

**Good choice**: Southern Spain or Malta.

### 3. **Adventure + nature balance**
- Combine beach with hiking, nature parks.
- Example: Gran Canaria or Tenerife — beaches + volcanic hills + jungle-like areas.
- Or Crete’s Samaria Gorge, Rhodes with inland villages.

## 💡 Practical tips

- **Book earlier in the shoulder season** if you want all amenities, especially for big family groups. Some smaller resorts or hotels close mid-October. ([europeandestinations.co.uk](https://www.europeandestinations.co.uk/destinations/european-countries/best-weather-october-europe/?utm_source=openai))  
- **Check flight schedules** — some destinations reduce frequencies outside peak times.  
- **Accommodation style**: renting a villa or multiple adjoining rooms works better for family atmosphere; gives more space and chance to cook/together meals.  
- **Weather buffers**: even in warm places, evenings may be cooler; bring layers. Also build in some indoor options in case of an off day.  
- **Travel insurance** and check local health/entry details ahead of booking.

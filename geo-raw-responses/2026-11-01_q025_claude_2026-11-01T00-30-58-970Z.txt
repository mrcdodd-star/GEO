## Marketplaces (more choice, usually lower prices)

With marketplaces you deal with the individual owner or local manager. Service quality is hit-or-miss.

## Tips for a large family
1. **Check the real sleeping capacity.** Count beds and bathrooms, not just the headline number. Look at how the beds are configured, and whether there's a ground-floor bedroom for grandparents.
2. **Ask about extra guests, day visitors and noise rules.** Some villas charge more or restrict gatherings above a certain number.
3. **Ask what's included.** Ask about the pool heating fee, cleaning, linens, utilities, a security deposit and tourist tax. Prices from agencies and marketplaces aren't always comparable.
4. **Look at the location.** Check the drive to the nearest grocery store and airport, and whether the roads are steep or narrow. A 20-minute drive each way gets tiring with kids.
5. **Check payment and cancellation terms.** Agencies often require large deposits well ahead. Ask about refund terms and consider travel insurance. Booking through an agency or platform generally gives you more protection than paying an owner directly by wire.
6. **Book early.** Large villas for peak summer weeks often book 9 to 12 months ahead.

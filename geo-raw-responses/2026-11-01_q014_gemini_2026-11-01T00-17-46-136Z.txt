Booking a private villa in the Algarve for a group of 12 in August is a fantastic plan, but because August is peak summer season, **large properties (5–6+ bedrooms) sell out fast and carry strict Saturday-to-Saturday 7-night minimum stay rules.**

### 1. Which Area of the Algarve Suits Your Group?

With 12 people, your group's "vibe" will determine the best location:

*   **Lagos & Praia da Luz (West):** Best for a mix of stunning beaches (Pontal da Piedade), historic town culture, great dining, and a relaxed, slightly younger or mixed-age group feel.
*   **Quinta do Lago / Vale do Lobo / Vilamoura (Golden Triangle):** Best for luxury, privacy, high-end dining, golf, and large gated estates with private grounds. Ideal for multi-generational families or luxury milestone celebrations.
*   **Carvoeiro & Ferragudo (Central):** Picturesque coastal towns with dramatic cliffs, fantastic restaurants, and family-friendly coves. Central location makes day trips easy.
*   **Albufeira Suburbs (e.g., Olhos de Água, Gale):** Great if you want to be close to nightlife, beach clubs, and activities, while staying slightly outside the busy strip for quiet at night.
*   **Tavira & Eastern Algarve:** Quieter, more authentic Portuguese feel, expansive white-sand islands, and better value for larger villas.

### 3. Key Essentials to Check Before Booking

*   **Bedrooms & Bathrooms:** A group of 12 usually needs a **5 to 6-bedroom villa** (or a 5-bed with a self-contained annex). Look for at least 4–5 bathrooms to avoid morning queues.
*   **Air Conditioning:** **Essential for August.** Temperatures routinely exceed 30°C–35°C. Ensure AC is installed in all bedrooms, not just the main living area.
*   **Pool Heating:** Unnecessary in August; the sea and outdoor pools are at their naturally warmest.
*   **Outdoor Living:** Check that the villa has an outdoor dining table that comfortably seats 12 in the shade, as well as adequate sun loungers and a built-in BBQ.

### 4. Travel & Logistics from the UK

*   **Flights:** Direct flights to **Faro (FAO)** run daily from nearly all UK airports (Gatwick, Heathrow, Stansted, Luton, Manchester, Birmingham, Bristol, Edinburgh, Belfast, etc.) via EasyJet, Ryanair, BA, Jet2, and TAP.
*   **Transfers vs. Rental Cars:** 
    *   A group of 12 with luggage will **not** fit in a standard minibus alongside luggage.
    *   **Option A:** Hire 2–3 cars at Faro airport (essential if staying in a rural or spread-out villa).
    *   **Option B:** Pre-book a dedicated 16-seater private airport transfer if you plan to stay within walking distance of a town/beach and use Ubers for local trips.

The best electric motorcycles for daily city commuting depend on whether you prioritise low costs, premium power, or unique city-friendly features like removable batteries.
For most riders, the Ryvid Anthem stands out as the best overall commuter due to its budget-friendly price and adjustable seat height. If you want a tech-forward premium machine, the LiveWire S2 Del Mar offers unmatched urban performance and rapid charging times.
The Top Electric Commuter Motorcycles
Ryvid Anthem: Best value for money. This lightweight bike features an innovative, height-adjustable seat and a removable 4.3 kWh battery package that lets you swap or charge indoors. It reaches highway speeds of up to 75 mph, making it incredibly versatile for urban freeways.
LiveWire S2 Del Mar: Best premium performance. Built specifically as a flat-track-inspired urban streetfighter, it produces a punchy 83 bhp and manages 60–86 miles of mixed riding. It also features fast charging, getting from 20% to 80% capacity in just under an hour and twenty minutes.
Maeving RM1S: Best retro style. This British-built motorcycle blends classic cafe racer aesthetics with modern electric efficiency. It utilizes dual removable batteries, tops out at 65 mph, and delivers a highly practical 80 miles of urban range.
BMW CE 02: Best for younger and new riders. Described by BMW as an "eParkourer," this funky, low-slung machine sits between a scooter and a motorcycle. It is incredibly nimble in dense traffic, though its 59 mph top speed restricts it from heavy highway usage.
Kawasaki Ninja e-1: Best traditional sportbike commuter. For those who prefer a conventional motorcycle look, this offers 12 hp and a lightweight chassis that handles identically to its petrol siblings. It uses twin removable batteries that can be charged at your office desk.

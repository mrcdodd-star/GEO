## What to Prioritize When Booking

To make sure your large-family villa rental goes smoothly, here are criteria to look out for:

- **Size and layout**: Number of bedrooms, whether there are multiple common areas so families aren’t tripping over each other, and outdoor space (pool, garden, patio) for kids to run around.

- **Support and local service**: Having property reps on site or a local contact is critical. Companies like CV Villas, Simpson Travel, Le Collectionist score high for this. ([uncompromised.travel](https://uncompromised.travel/stays/luxury-villa-rental-company-index-2026?utm_source=openai))

- **Inspection and reviews**: Make sure villas are inspected in person and that there are plenty of guest reviews. Firms like Le Collectionist and Oliver’s Travels do this. ([uncompromised.travel](https://uncompromised.travel/stays/luxury-villa-rental-company-index-2026?utm_source=openai))

- **Money protection & clear terms**: What happens if flights are canceled? Leave early? How refundable are deposits, what are cancellation policies, and do you have protection for booking insolvency (not just the property)? ([uncompromised.travel](https://uncompromised.travel/stays/luxury-villa-rental-company-index-2026?utm_source=openai))

- **Extras / amenities**: Services like private chefs, airport transfers, grocery delivery, childcare, cleaning, etc, are big perks when traveling with many people. They’ll save stress. ([houseandgarden.co.uk](https://www.houseandgarden.co.uk/article/best-villa-rental-companies?utm_source=openai))

- **Location accessibility**: For large families, direct or fewer flights, proximity to airports, or ease of ground transport becomes more important. Remote villas may be beautiful, but the travel burden can add up.

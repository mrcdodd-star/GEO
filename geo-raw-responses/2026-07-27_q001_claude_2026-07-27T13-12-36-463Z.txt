## 🏍️ Top Electric Motorbike Brands

---

### 1. 🇺🇸 **Zero Motorcycles**
One of the most established and respected electric motorcycle brands. They offer a wide range of models (SR/F, SR/S, DSR/X) known for excellent range, performance, and build quality. A top choice for both commuters and adventure riders.

---

### 2. 🇺🇸 **Harley-Davidson (LiveWire)**
Harley-Davidson spun off its electric division into a standalone brand called **LiveWire**. The LiveWire One is a premium electric motorcycle with strong performance, great tech features, and iconic styling.

---

### 3. 🇮🇳 **Revolt Motors**
A popular and affordable electric motorcycle brand, especially in Asian markets. Known for app-connected bikes with swappable battery options.

---

### 4. 🇬🇧 **Triumph Motorcycles**
Triumph has entered the electric space with the **TE-1 prototype** and is developing production-ready electric models. Known for premium build quality and British heritage.

---

### 5. 🇮🇹 **Energica**
Italy's leading electric motorcycle manufacturer. Energica bikes (like the Ego and Eva EsseEsse9) are high-performance machines popular in motorsport and among premium buyers. They were the official supplier for the **MotoE World Cup**.

---

### 6. 🇯🇵 **Honda**
Honda has been developing electric motorcycles and scooters aggressively, including the **CB Electric** concept and the **EM1 e:** scooter. Expect more production models soon given Honda's massive R&D investment.

---

### 7. 🇺🇸 **Cake (KALK & Ösa)**
A Swedish brand known for minimalist, off-road electric motorcycles. Popular among adventure and dirt bike enthusiasts for their lightweight and eco-conscious design.

---

### 8. 🇺🇸 **Damon Motorcycles**
A Canadian brand making waves with their **HyperSport**, which features advanced safety tech including 360° awareness systems and ergonomic adaptability.

---

### 9. 🇨🇳 **Sur-Ron**
A Chinese brand that has become hugely popular globally for affordable, lightweight electric dirt/trail bikes. Great entry-level option for off-road enthusiasts.

---

### 10. 🇮🇳 **Ola Electric**
A fast-growing Indian brand that has expanded rapidly in the electric two-wheeler market, offering feature-rich bikes and scooters at competitive prices.

---

| Factor | Details |
|---|---|
| **Range** | How far it goes on a single charge |
| **Charging Speed** | Fast charging vs. standard |
| **Performance** | Torque, top speed, acceleration |
| **Price** | Entry-level vs. premium |
| **After-sales Support** | Service network availability |

---

The electric motorbike market is growing rapidly, with more legacy brands joining the space every year. **Zero Motorcycles** and **Energica** remain top choices for performance, while **Sur-Ron** and **Revolt** are great for budget-conscious riders. 🚀

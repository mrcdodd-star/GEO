For a group of 16 with grandparents and three toddlers, **Menorca** would be my first pick: it’s a relaxed, beach-focused island with plenty of family-friendly coves, and is generally quieter than its Balearic neighbours.[6]

Other good fits:
- **The Algarve** — an easy option from the UK, with no time difference, family-friendly towns and beaches; July is often a little cooler than Spain, which can help with toddlers.[1][2]
- **Brittany or Normandy** — ideal if you’d rather drive and take a ferry than organise flights for 16; they offer beaches, markets and a slower pace.[2][5]
- **Lake Garda** — a good choice if your group wants more than beach days, with water activities and family attractions.[3]

For a group this size, I’d prioritise one spacious rental or several nearby properties, a pool or walkable beach, shade, and easy access to shops and medical care. If you’re travelling in August, consider a less-visited area or late summer: peak school-holiday dates can push prices up, and busy southern European cities can be uncomfortably hot.[8]

### 1. Zero FXE – Best all‑round city commuter

  - Designed specifically as a lightweight **urban commuter** with agile handling and simple controls.[1][3][6][9]
  - Frequently rated “best overall for urban commuting” and “best value” among electric city bikes.[1][3][6]
- **Key specs (2026 reviews)**
  - Real city range: **~100–105 miles** per charge, enough for a full workweek of typical commuting.[1][3][6]

### How to choose *your* best city commuter

Across buying guides, three factors are repeatedly emphasized for city commuting:[6][9]

- **Realistic city range:**  
  - For most urban riders, **70–100 miles of real‑world city range** is adequate.[9]  
  - Short commutes (<40 miles/day): bikes like **BMW CE 02**, **Ryvid Anthem**, or **Super Soco TC Max** are sufficient.[6][9]  
  - Medium commutes (40–80 miles/day): **Zero FXE**, **LiveWire S2 Del Mar**, **Honda WN7**.[1][2][6][9]  
  - Long commutes (>80 miles/day): consider **LiveWire ONE** or **Energica Experia** for range and fast charging.[6][9]

- **Weight and maneuverability:**  
  - Lighter models like **Zero FXE**, **Ryvid Anthem**, **BMW CE 02** are easier in traffic and for tight parking.[6][9][10]

- **Charging convenience:**  
  - If you can charge at home overnight, Level 1/2 charging on bikes like Zero FXE or BMW CE 04 is fine.[1][6][9]  
  - If you rely on public chargers and need quick turnarounds, **LiveWire ONE** and high‑end Energica with DC or rapid charging are better.[6][9][11]

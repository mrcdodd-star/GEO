If you’re choosing between **The Dorchester** and **The Peninsula London**, the short version is: **The Peninsula is the newer, more polished ultra-luxury all-rounder; The Dorchester is the classic, more storied London grande dame**.[1][3][7]

- **The Peninsula London** opened in **2023** in **Belgravia** at **1 Grosvenor Place**, near Hyde Park Corner and Wellington Arch.[1][5][17]
- **The Dorchester** is the historic **Mayfair** icon at **53 Park Lane**, dating back to **1931** and long associated with old-school London luxury.[1][5][19]

If you care most about **newness, room size, and spa/dining polish**, The Peninsula has the edge:
- It has about **190 rooms and 59 suites** and is described as having a **subterranean spa**, **rooftop terrace**, and a strong dining lineup including **Brooklands by Claude Bosi** and **Canton Blue**.[1][4]
- Reviews and guides describe it as a modern luxury flagship with very high starting rates, often around **£880+ per night** or higher depending on source and date.[1][10][16]

If you care most about **heritage, Park Lane prestige, and classic London glamour**, The Dorchester is the stronger fit:
- It has about **241 rooms** and sits directly opposite **Hyde Park** in **Mayfair**.[1][15]
- Recent coverage describes a major refresh, including the **Promenade**, **Vesper Bar**, and guest rooms and suites, while preserving its famous old-world identity.[5][7]

A practical comparison:

| Category | The Dorchester | The Peninsula London |
|---|---|---|
| **Style** | Classic, historic, grand | Contemporary, ultra-polished, newer |
| **Location** | Mayfair, Park Lane | Belgravia, Hyde Park Corner |
| **Opened** | 1931 | 2023 |
| **Room count** | About 241 | About 190 rooms + 59 suites |
| **Best for** | Heritage, tradition, old-school glamour | New luxury, spacious rooms, spa, modern service |

**Choose The Dorchester** if you want the more iconic London address and a traditional sense of occasion.[1][5][19]  
**Choose The Peninsula** if you want the more contemporary luxury experience and don’t mind paying for the newest flagship feel.[1][4][16]

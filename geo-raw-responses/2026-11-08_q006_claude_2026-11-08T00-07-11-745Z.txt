Here are some well-regarded options for UK travellers. They come from several roundups, and a few of those are written by the companies themselves.

**Ultra-luxury, with concierge service**
- **Abercrombie & Kent Villas** is Spear's top recommendation. 
It's an arm of the luxury travel provider and entered the villa market in 2002.
 
It offers 24/7 emergency support, with teams on the ground near each rental.

- **Onefinestay** 
vets each property individually and provides round-the-clock concierge services.
 
You can add extras such as childcare, sports equipment and grocery deliveries.

- **Ultima Collection** 
offers ultra-luxury private residences across Europe.

- **Icon Private Collection** is also in Spear's list, which describes its offering as "ultra-bespoke".

**Curated and characterful**
- **Oliver's Travels** 
includes everything from medieval châteaux and treehouses to sleek beach villas and family-friendly farmhouses.

- **Le Collectionist** 
has a carefully curated collection of holiday rentals in popular destinations.
 It is especially strong in France.
- **Simon Escapes** and **Insider Villas** both feature in Just Luxe's list of the best holiday rental agencies.

**Specialists by destination**
- **Tuscany Now & More** is 
a London-based, family-run company specialising in Italian villa holidays.
 
Travel + Leisure's World's Best Awards named it Best Luxury Villa Rental Company in 2023 and 2024.

- **The Thinking Traveller** 
specialises in the Greek Islands, Corsica, Sicily, Puglia and Mallorca.


**Established UK villa companies**
- **CV Villas** 
was the UK's first specialist villa company, founded in 1972.
 
Its portfolio is particularly strong across the Mediterranean.

- **The Villa Collection** is ranked first in its own comparison guide. 
It covers Europe, the Caribbean, Asia, Africa and beyond.
 That guide is published by the company, so treat the ranking with some caution.
- **Villa Plus** 
is good value for European villa holidays.
 It is more mid-range than ultra-luxury.

**Tips**
- If you book a package with flights, check for ATOL protection. Also check whether the company is ABTA-bonded, because this protects your money if the company fails.
- Ask what's included: staff, a chef, pool heating, cleaning and security deposits. Prices vary a lot between companies.
- Airbnb has a luxury Europe category, but it has no concierge or vetting at the level of the specialist agencies above.

If you tell me the destination, budget, group size and dates, I can narrow this down. Italy, the south of France and Greece each have their own specialists.

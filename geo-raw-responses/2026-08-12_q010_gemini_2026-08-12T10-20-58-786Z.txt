--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
Mayfair is home to some of the most prestigious 5-star hotels in the world. While known for their elite sophistication, many of these iconic properties are incredibly welcoming to families, offering specialized children's programs, dedicated kid-friendly amenities, and multi-bedroom suites. 

The premier family-friendly 5-star hotels in Mayfair offering exceptional suites and child-focused experiences include:

---

### 1. Brown’s Hotel (A Rocco Forte Hotel)
* **The Vibe:** Sophisticated, warm, and quintessentially British. As London’s oldest hotel (historically playing host to Rudyard Kipling and Queen Victoria), it feels like a refined country house in the middle of the city.
* **The Suites:** Brown’s offers dedicated **Two-Bedroom Family Suites** (approx. 105 sq. m / 1,130 sq. ft) specifically configured to give parents and kids their own spaces with interconnecting features.
* **Family-Friendly Perks:** 
  * Through the **"Families R Forte"** program, younger guests receive a VIP welcome. 
  * In-suite setups can include playful teepees/wig-wams, a "teddy bear turndown," and custom books detailing the adventures of Albemarle (the hotel's resident monkey mascot).
  * Children are gifted pint-sized monogrammed bathrobes, slippers, and specialized Irene Forte skincare products for kids.
  * Charlie’s (the on-site restaurant) features tailored, high-quality children's dining menus.

### 2. The Connaught
* **The Vibe:** Discreet, elite luxury on quiet Carlos Place with legendary, highly personalized butler service. 
* **The Suites & Special Offers:** The Connaught frequently runs **"A Family Story"**—a highly sought-after package where booking a one-bedroom suite for parents provides a *complimentary* interconnecting or nearby second room for children up to 16 years old (including daily family breakfast). 
* **Family-Friendly Perks:**
  * Children receive personalized welcome treats and seasonal goodie bags.
  * The suite can be transformed for the ultimate "hotel sleepover" with select video game consoles and movie setups.
  * The hotel’s renowned **Aman Spa** (featuring a striking black granite pool) offers dedicated family swim times so children can splash around comfortably.
  * Excellent, child-friendly dining menus are crafted by chef Jean-Georges Vongerichten.

### 3. Flemings Mayfair
* **The Vibe:** A stylish boutique hotel converted from 13 Georgian townhouses on Half Moon Street, offering a more intimate, residential feel.
* **The Suites & Apartments:** For families wanting the comfort of home, Flemings offers **Two- and Three-Bedroom Suites and Serviced Apartments**. These come with private, separate entrances and fully equipped Siemens kitchens (ideal for preparing baby food, warming milk, or managing laundry with kids).
* **Family-Friendly Perks:**
  * Kids are greeted with **"Fleming the Frog" travel packs**, which include a storybook, fluffy robes, slippers, and child-safe bubble bath.
  * Children receive a special card inviting them to order complimentary hot chocolate and cookies directly to their suite before bedtime.

### 4. Claridge’s
* **The Vibe:** Timeless Art Deco glamour. While it is the pinnacle of high society, the staff is incredibly warm and accommodating to children.
* **The Suites:** The **Two-Bedroom Mayfair Suites** are highly recommended for families. They feature generous shared living areas, grand marble bathrooms, and **24-hour personal butler service** to assist with anything from warming bottles to unpacking bags. 
* **Family-Friendly Perks:**
  * Claridge's offers excellent in-room childcare and babysitting services arranged via the concierge.
  * Kids can enjoy custom activity books, family hours at the indoor swimming pool, and child-sized amenities.
  * The legendary Afternoon Tea in the Foyer & Reading Room is adapted for children, featuring kid-friendly treats.

### 5. The Ritz London
* **The Vibe:** Palatial, ultra-formal, and world-famous. Despite its grand reputation, it has one of London’s most comprehensive kids' clubs.
* **The Suites:** Children under the age of 12 can stay in their parents' Junior Suite on a complimentary basis.
* **Family-Friendly Perks:** 
  * The **"kidz@theritz"** program provides a highly tailored VIP experience.
  * Upon arrival, children receive specialized welcome gifts, children’s bath amenities, and their own Ritz bathrobes and slippers.
  * In-room game consoles and DVDs are available on request.
  * Children receive a complimentary scoop of ice cream when dining in the Two-Michelin-starred Ritz Restaurant or during Afternoon Tea.

---

### Which one should you choose?
* **Best for Toddlers & Babies:** **Flemings Mayfair**. Having a fully functional kitchen in your multi-bedroom apartment makes managing baby bottles, snacks, and laundry incredibly easy while maintaining 5-star hotel service.
* **Best for Young Kids (Ages 3–10):** **Brown’s Hotel**. The in-room wig-wams, specialized book gifts, and teddy bear turndown are magical touches that make kids feel genuinely excited about their stay.
* **Best for Tweens & Teens:** **The Connaught**. The "Family Story" package provides teenagers with their own privacy in a separate room, and the in-room gaming setups and access to the Aman pool keep them thoroughly entertained.

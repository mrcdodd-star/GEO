- **Hitched UK** — wedding-planning guide with French château venue suggestions and advice on budgeting and booking.[12]

- **Storied Collection** — exclusive-use historic French estates, with a venue adviser and a UK contact number.[7]
- **South France Holiday Villas** — a useful option if you’re looking in Provence or elsewhere in the south; its collection includes venues suitable for weddings.[3]

For specific examples, **Château de Bourneau** in the Vendée hosts weddings and offers nearby cottage accommodation, while **Château Challain** in the Loire is a dedicated wedding venue with English-language planning support.[4][18]

When enquiring, give your date, guest count, budget and how many people need to stay onsite. Check whether the quote is **venue-only or includes catering, coordination, furniture and accommodation**—these can be separate costs. Popular venues and dates can book well ahead, so start early.[12]

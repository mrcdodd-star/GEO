--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
For a **luxury stay in Mayfair**, the most widely recommended choices are **Claridge’s**, **The Connaught**, **Four Seasons Hotel London at Park Lane**, **The Dorchester**, and **The Beaumont**, with newer hotspots like **The Twenty Two** and **The Biltmore Mayfair** if you want something more contemporary.[1][2][4][12][13]

Below is a quick, curated guide depending on the type of luxury experience you want.

---

### 1. For iconic, “classic London” glamour

- **Claridge’s**  
  - Often described as the **gold standard for classic luxury in Mayfair**.[2]  
  - Art Deco-inspired interiors, seamless service, prime location in the heart of Mayfair.[1][2]  
  - Ideal if you want the full “old-school London” experience with polished formality.

- **The Dorchester** (Park Lane)  
  - A true London institution, famous for **royal-treatment service** and very traditional British style.[1][2]  
  - Overlooks Hyde Park; great if you want grand public spaces, iconic afternoon tea, and a very established feel.

- **Brown’s Hotel**  
  - One of London’s oldest hotels, blending heritage with discreet luxury in a central Mayfair location.[1][4]  
  - Good if you want history without the full formality of Claridge’s or The Dorchester.

---

### 2. For discreet, ultra-refined luxury

- **The Connaught**  
  - Frequently cited as **the best discreet luxury hotel in Mayfair**.[2]  
  - Michelin-starred dining, superb bar program, holistic spa, and very understated elegance.[2][15]  
  - Excellent if you value privacy, impeccable service, and a “quietly wealthy” atmosphere.

- **The Beaumont**  
  - Luxurious Art Deco–style hotel tucked between Bond Street and Selfridges.[1][12]  
  - Known for characterful design and a strong restaurant; feels exclusive but not intimidating.

---

### 3. For modern, contemporary 5-star luxury

- **Four Seasons Hotel London at Park Lane**  
  - Regularly highlighted as **best modern luxury near Hyde Park**.[2]  
  - Contemporary rooms, rooftop spa with city views, very polished Four Seasons service.[1][12][19]  
  - Good if you prefer light, modern décor over traditional British interiors.

- **The Biltmore Mayfair (Grosvenor Square)**  
  - Modern British elegance with refined interiors on Grosvenor Square.[8][13]  
  - Strong choice if you want brand-new-feeling spaces and an upscale but less “old-world” vibe.

- **COMO Metropolitan London**  
  - Clean, minimalist, contemporary rooms across from Hyde Park.[1][5]  
  - Works well if you like a design-forward, uncluttered aesthetic with a calmer atmosphere.

- **1 Hotel Mayfair**  
  - A **sustainable luxury** option: plant-filled interiors, Bamford wellness spa, high-end dining, steps from Green Park.[1]  
  - Ideal if you prioritize eco-conscious design and wellness.

---

### 4. For buzzy, stylish “scene” hotels

- **The Twenty Two**  
  - Newer, very fashionable hotel with **seriously plush** Art Deco-style suites, a stylish bar and restaurant.[1][5]  
  - Great if you want nightlife and a social atmosphere built into your hotel.

- **The Mayfair Townhouse**  
  - Described as a “stylish lifestyle hotel,” playful and design-led.[5][7]  
  - Good if you prefer character and quirkiness over formality.

---

### 5. For apartment-style or longer luxury stays

- **Grosvenor House Suites (JW Marriott)**  
  - Often recommended as the **best luxury apartment-style option** in Mayfair.[2]  
  - Large suites with kitchen facilities and hotel-level service on Park Lane – excellent for families or long stays.[2]

- **No. 5 Maddox Street**  
  - Discreet serviced apartments close to Bond Street and Regent Street, with more space than a typical hotel room.[1][2]  
  - Stylish, good for longer visits and shoppers wanting a residential feel.

- Other luxury residence-style options in Mayfair include **Grand Residences by Marriott** and **Native Mayfair**, both offering spacious, apartment-like accommodation with a luxurious feel.[11]

---

### 6. For luxury but slightly less “grand hotel” pricing

- **Flemings Mayfair**  
  - A historic, 5-star **boutique luxury hotel** in Georgian townhouses; part of Small Luxury Hotels of the World.[2][14][15]  
  - More intimate than the big names, with a Michelin-starred restaurant (Ormer Mayfair).[14]  
  - Excellent if you want high-end comfort in a smaller, quieter property.

- **The Chesterfield Mayfair**  
  - Classic British style, friendly service, very popular for its afternoon tea.[2][11]  
  - Often noted as a strong **traditional 4-star** with a luxurious feel, and good value for its location.[2][11]

- **The May Fair, A Radisson Collection Hotel**  
  - Large five-star hotel with 400 rooms and suites in central Mayfair.[3][10][16]  
  - More contemporary and business-friendly; tends to price below Claridge’s/Connaught but still fully luxury.

---

### How to choose, in one line

- **First time, want the archetypal Mayfair experience:** go for **Claridge’s** or **The Dorchester**.[1][2]  
- **Prefer privacy and understated wealth:** choose **The Connaught** or **The Beaumont**.[2][12]  
- **Love modern design and wellness:** pick **Four Seasons Park Lane**, **1 Hotel Mayfair**, or **COMO Metropolitan**.[1][2][12]  
- **Want a fashionable, social scene:** look at **The Twenty Two** or **The Biltmore Mayfair**.[1][8][13]  
- **Need space or traveling with family:** **Grosvenor House Suites** or **No. 5 Maddox Street**.[1][2]

## ✅ What to Look for

When searching, keep these criteria in mind to make sure your villa meets your full expectations:

1. **“Fully Staffed / Fully Serviced” Villas**  
   These villas include everything—chef, housekeeping, butler/host—so you don’t need to ask for add-ons.

2. **Chef Service Terms**  
   - Number of meals per day included (breakfast only vs full board)  
   - Whether groceries/food costs are extra  
   - If chef comes daily or selectively  

3. **Housekeeping Frequency**  
   - Daily vs fewer days per week  
   - Are linens and towels changed regularly?  
   - Are there additional cleaning staff like pool/bathroom cleaners?  

4. **Concierge and Support on the Ground**  
   - Someone you can communicate with from UK before booking  
   - Local contacts to sort out extra requests (childcare, drivers, massages)  

5. **Lead Time & Seasons**  
   - June-to-September is high season: book months ahead  
   - Shoulder seasons (spring/autumn) might offer better rates & availability  

6. **Reputable UK-Based Travel or Villa Agencies**  
   - Can help with flights, visas, communications  
   - Usually have vetted properties

## 🗺️ Popular Regions in Italy & What to Expect

Different parts of Italy have different pros, costs, and logistics:

- **Tuscany / Umbria**  
  Ideal for pastoral scenery, vineyards, countryside luxury. Many villas with staff included.  

- **Amalfi Coast / Capri / Sorrento**  
  Stunning sea views, more expensive, harder to drive to, but top luxury with high staff standards.  

- **Lake Como, Lake Garda**  
  Water vistas, cooler climate, prominent for high-end luxury villas.  

- **Sicily & Sardinia**  
  Island charm, Mediterranean lifestyle; remote villas often come with full service because of distance.

## 📆 Booking Tips (from the UK)

1. **Start Early**  
   For summer/festive periods, begin 6–12 months ahead.  

2. **Make a Shortlist & Request Offerings in Writing**  
   Ensure chef & housekeeping hours are clearly documented.  

3. **Check Travel Logistics**  
   - Closest airport, transfer times  
   - Whether staff require accommodation on site  

4. **Communication**  
   - Do you agree with their terms of cancellation?  
   - Are food costs included, will groceries be separately billed?  

5. **Payment in Pounds vs Euro**  
   Currency fluctuations can matter; some agencies allow GBP payments.

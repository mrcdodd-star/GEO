### 3. Key Questions & Details to Clarify Before Booking

When inquiring about a property, clarify the following to ensure there are no surprises:

#### **A. Chef Services**
*   **Included vs. Add-On:** Is the chef’s fee included in the nightly villa rate, or is it an extra daily service charge?
*   **Food Expenses:** Chef service fees almost always **exclude the cost of ingredients and beverages**. You will typically pay the chef or concierge in cash/card locally based on actual supermarket receipts, plus a small sourcing fee.
*   **Meal Schedule:** Confirm which meals are covered (e.g., breakfast + dinner, or all 3 meals). Make sure to communicate dietary requirements well in advance.

#### **B. Housekeeping Services**
*   **Scope of Service:** Clarify what "daily" means. Does it mean 2–3 hours of light cleaning and bed making, or full-day service including laundry and evening turn-down?
*   **Rest Days:** In Italy, domestic staff often have Sunday or one weekday off by law. Check if coverage is provided on those days or if a substitute staff member is brought in.

#### **C. Financials & Protection**
*   **Payment Currency:** UK agencies may quote in GBP (£), whereas direct local agencies will quote in Euros (€). If paying in Euros, consider using a fee-free currency card or transfer service (like Wise) to avoid bank conversion markups.
*   **Financial Protection:** If booking a "villa only," check the agency's financial protection policy. If you book flights and the villa together through an agency like CV Villas, it may be covered under ATOL.

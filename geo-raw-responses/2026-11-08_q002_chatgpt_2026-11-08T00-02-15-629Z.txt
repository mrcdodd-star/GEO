## Tips to Maximise Everyone’s Fun

- **Pace the days**: Plan one big group activity per day (boat ride, cultural visit), plus optional bits (nap time, free time, spa for grandparents).  
- **Stay put or limit moves**: Long drives or many changes of accommodation are harder with toddlers or less mobile members. Choose a base with choices nearby. ([europeandestinations.co.uk](https://www.europeandestinations.co.uk/holidays/family-holidays/mixed-age-families/?utm_source=openai))  
- **Pick flexible dining**: Look for all-inclusive or full board with different restaurants or buffet + à la carte options. Dinner times that accommodate early and later eaters help. ([europeandestinations.co.uk](https://www.europeandestinations.co.uk/holidays/family-holidays/mixed-age-families/?utm_source=openai))  
- **Good transport & comfort**: Avoid steep cobbles or lots of stairs. If cars are involved, limit hours. For older people, factor elevators, handrails, medical accessibility.  
- **Shared memories**: Choose something unique to do together—cooking class, boat trip, private guide—that all generations will talk about.

Here are some good options from the UK. The best choice depends on your group's ages and mobility.

**Beach and villa holidays (the easiest option for mixed ages)**
- **Algarve, Portugal** is probably the strongest all-rounder. 
Flights from Manchester take under three hours. The coast has gentle family beaches and championship golf for grandparents who want a morning to themselves. Villa-style resorts with private pools give each generation space to retreat.
 
Vilamoura and Albufeira have plenty of multi-bedroom villas.
 It's also good value: 
Post Office Travel Money found it the cheapest European beach destination for families this year.

- **Mallorca, Spain** came top in easyJet's index. 
It scored well for its beaches, waterparks and easy travel from the UK.
 
The flight is about two hours and leaves from most regional UK airports.

- **Canary Islands (Tenerife, Lanzarote)** have year-round sun and good value. 
Lanzarote came third in the Post Office value report.
 
Tenerife has well-known resorts such as Costa Adeje and Los Cristianos, and it's still one of the best-value destinations in the report.


**All-inclusive and longer stays**
- **Turkey** is 
exceptional value, especially at all-inclusive resorts.
 That suits groups who want meals and entertainment sorted for everyone.
- **Cyprus** works for stays of ten nights or more. 
It has archaeological sites, calm family beaches and many all-inclusive resorts, which helps with different tastes and dietary needs across three generations.

- **Greece** (Corfu, Rhodes): 
Greece came top as the UK's favourite family holiday destination in one parents' survey.
 
CV Villas has a good selection of villas in north-east Corfu.


**Culture and city options**
- **Rome, Dubrovnik/Dalmatian coast, Vienna and Amsterdam** are suggested for multigenerational trips that mix history, culture and child-friendly activities. Cities involve more walking, so they suit active grandparents better.

**Easiest on logistics**
- **A cruise** is 
one of the easiest solutions for a multigenerational holiday, and you can avoid flying by sailing from a UK port such as Southampton.

- **Center Parcs-style villages** are another option. 
Landal GreenParks has holiday villages across Europe.


**Tips for choosing**
- Book a villa or apartment with **separate bedrooms and a private pool**. That lets everyone keep their own rhythm.
- Pick **short flights (under 3–4 hours)** and a **direct route** from your local airport.
- Check for **step-free access** if anyone has limited mobility.
- Consider **May, June or September**. They're cooler for older relatives and cheaper than school holidays.

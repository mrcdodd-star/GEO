## ⚠ What to be cautious about

### Scope of Responsibility  
- Oliver’s Travels generally acts as an **agent** for holiday properties. The contract for “accommodation only” bookings is between the customer and the **property owner/supplier**. Oliver’s Travels facilitates the booking. ([oliverstravels.com](https://www.oliverstravels.com/terms-and-conditions/?utm_source=openai))  
- In their Terms & Conditions they say they do *not endorse or recommend* properties and cannot be held liable for all issues with the property itself—cleanliness, amenities, etc., especially since owners supply those. ([oliverstravels.com](https://www.oliverstravels.com/terms-and-conditions/?utm_source=openai))  

### Reported Issues  
- Some reviewers have flagged misleading listings (photos vs reality), villas below the standards expected (beds, furniture, amenities), cleaning lapses, and unhelpful resolution when issues arise. ([tratratravel.co.uk](https://tratratravel.co.uk/en-gb/i/66713-olivers-travels/?utm_source=openai))

## 💡 How to protect yourself when booking

To help ensure a smooth experience, here are suggestions:

1. **Read recent reviews of the specific property** (not just the company). Where people mention the same villa/château, you’ll see patterns—for good or bad.

2. **Check what the photos show vs what guests mention**. Look for comments about cleanliness, maintenance issues, matching descriptions.

3. **Ask detailed questions before booking**—pool state, Wi-Fi reliability, anything non-standard. Make sure you get answers in writing.

4. **Confirm who is responsible for what**—if there’s damage, who handles it; if something isn’t as shown, how to escalate.

5. **Check the cancellation and booking policies** carefully. Sometimes things are non-refundable, or cancellation by the owner is possible.

6. **Use a payment method with fraud protection** (credit card if possible).

7. **Watch out for impersonation or phishing**, especially in light of the security incident in Oct 2026. Only trust official emails/calls, and be wary of requests to change payment details. ([oliverstravels.com](https://www.oliverstravels.com/security-incident/?utm_source=openai))

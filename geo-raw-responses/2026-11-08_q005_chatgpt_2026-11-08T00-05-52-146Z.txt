## 🏖️ Holiday Options Where Someone Else Handles Meals & Childcare

These are the main types of holidays where you can truly switch off from cooking and supervising little ones.

| Type | What’s Included | Ideal For |
|------|------------------|-----------|
| **All-Inclusive Resorts** | All meals, drinks, snacks, entertainment; often day-and-evening childcare, kids’ clubs, sometimes creches for infants | Beach breaks abroad; total relaxation; families who want everything in one place |
| **Luxury Villas with Staff** | Private chef/cook, nanny service, shopping services; you still get independence of your own place | More privacy, flexibility, longer stays, possibly off the beaten path |
| **Adventure or Activity-Based Holiday Villages (UK or abroad)** | Meals provided, childcare as part of activity schedule, often 24-hour onsite staff | Families who want structure and things for the kids to do without running around |
| **Villa-Brochure Concierge Services** | Combined offerings of meals, nanny, chauffeur, housekeeping tailored per family | High-end, bespoke holidays |

## ✔ Things to Check When Booking

To make sure the holiday delivers on ease:

- **Childcare Services**: Creches (from infants), kids’ clubs, babysitting; staff qualifications; how many hours/days cover.  
- **Meals**: All meals included? Kid-friendly food options? Private cook or buffet?  
- **Room Amenities**: Access to kitchen (if you need minimal cooking), safety around the pool, entertainment in rooms.  
- **Location & Transfer**: How far from airport, how much time gets spent traveling—kids hate long transfers.  
- **Extras & Hidden Costs**: What’s included in “all-inclusive”; childcare may cost extra; check age limits; tipping; local taxes.

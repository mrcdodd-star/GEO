### 2. The Signature Suites Collection
Following the hotel’s major multi-year restoration, the suites offer refined English-garden-inspired aesthetics and incredible modern comforts. The key signature and specialty suites include:

*   **The Royal Suite:** The newly launched, majestic flagship residence overlooking Hyde Park. Spanning over 300 m², it offers a full ultra-luxury residential layout. *(Price: POA)*
*   **The Oliver Messel Terrace Suite (144m² / 1,550ft²):** A legendary suite designed by Oliver Messel, featuring historic design elements, a private terrace, and sweeping London skyline views. *(Price: POA)*
*   **Garden Terrace Suite (101m² / 1,087ft² + terrace):** Overlooks the front of the hotel with views of Mayfair and Hyde Park. It features private terrace access and can connect to accommodate up to five bedrooms. *(Rates typically from £3,200+/night)*
*   **The Dorchester Suite (109m² / 1,173ft²):** A corner suite featuring wrap-around windows with direct views of Hyde Park and Park Lane. *(Rates typically from £2,500+/night)*
*   **Belgravia, Mayfair, & Hyde Park Suites (100m² – 106m²):** Individually styled suites carrying distinct design identities, complete with spacious dining areas, Juliet balconies, and options for connecting layouts. *(Rates typically from £2,500+/night)*
*   **Elizabeth Taylor Harlequin Suite (208m²):** A historic rooftop suite with a large outside terrace, famously Elizabeth Taylor's "home away from home".

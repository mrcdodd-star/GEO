Here are the main UK-based options, grouped by what you're likely after. I've assumed you mean villas abroad, but I've included luxury rentals within the UK as well.

## Luxury villas abroad (UK-based companies)
- **Abercrombie & Kent Villas** and **Icon Private Collection** are rated "Top Recommended" in Spear's 2026 roundup. 
A&K focuses on meticulously hand-picked villas and entered the villa market in 2002.
 
It offers 24/7 emergency support, with teams on the ground near each rental.

- **Ultima Collection** 
offers ultra-luxury private residences across Europe.

- **Tuscany Now & More** is a good choice if you want Italy. 
It's a London-based, family-run company specialising in Italian villa holidays.
 
Travel + Leisure's World's Best Awards named it Best Luxury Villa Rental Company in 2023 and 2024.

- **Oliver's Travels** 
emphasises France, Italy, the Caribbean and upmarket rural Britain.
 One comparison site notes that 
it doesn't include flights in its offering
, so you won't get ATOL protection if you book flights through it.
- **The Thinking Traveller** specialises in 
luxury villas in Sicily, Puglia, the Ionian and Sporades islands in Greece, and Corsica, including villas exclusive to them.

- **CV Villas** 
was the UK's first specialist villa company, founded in 1972.
 It has 
a strong portfolio, particularly across the Mediterranean.

- **Insider Villas**, **Simon Escapes** and **Le Collectionist** are also on Just Luxe's list. 
Insider Villas is rated as high end and covers Europe, the Caribbean, the USA and South Africa.

- **James Villas** is more mainstream. 
Its website offers more than 3,000 holiday villas.

## Tips
- Some "best of" lists come from companies promoting themselves. Haute Retreats and The Villa Collection both rank themselves first on their own sites, so treat those rankings with caution.
- If you're booking flights too, check for **ATOL protection**.

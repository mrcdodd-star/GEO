Finding a luxury villa in Italy with a private chef and daily housekeeping from the UK is straightforward, provided you use the right channels. You generally have two main approaches: **booking through a specialist luxury villa agency** (which handles all staffing for you) or **using high-end villa platforms** with dedicated concierge services.

Here is a step-by-step guide to finding and booking the right property.

---

### 1. Top UK-Based & Specialist Luxury Villa Agencies
The easiest and most reliable route is booking through a specialist UK agency. They pre-vets properties and can either include staff directly in the rental cost or arrange vetted, local private chefs and daily housekeeping teams.

    *   *Service:* They offer an explicit category of **"Fully Staffed Villas"** which include a private chef, maid service/daily housekeeping, and a villa manager as standard.
*   **CV Villas**
    *   *Specialty:* Long-established UK luxury villa operator (ABTA and ATOL protected).
    *   *Service:* Look at their **"ABOVE"** collection, which features luxury properties that include daily housekeeping, cook service, and dedicated concierge support.
*   **Oliver’s Travels**
    *   *Specialty:* Unique and luxury properties across Europe.

    *   *Specialty:* Ultra-luxury tailor-made holidays.

---

### 3. Key Questions to Ask Before Booking
When inquiring about "chef and daily housekeeping" services, clarify the details upfront to avoid hidden costs:

1.  **Chef vs. Cook:** 
    *   *Cook:* Usually prepares traditional local family-style Italian meals.
    *   *Chef:* Offers high-end fine dining or Michelin-grade multi-course tasting menus.
2.  **Grocery Costs:** 
    *   Is food/ingredient cost included in the villa rate, or is it charged separately based on receipts + a service percentage? (In Italy, groceries are almost always charged separately on top of the chef’s fee).
3.  **Meals Included:** 
    *   Does the chef cover breakfast, lunch, and dinner, or just breakfast and dinner? Does the chef get 1 or 2 days off per week?
4.  **Daily Housekeeping Scope:** 
    *   How many hours per day are included? Does it cover full turndown service, linen changes, and laundry, or just light cleaning/tidying and dishwashing after meals?

---

### 4. Recommended Search Strategy

1. **Start with Tuscany Now & More or The Thinking Traveller** to see pre-packaged "fully staffed" villas in regions like Tuscany, Puglia, or Sicily.
2. **Contact a UK Specialist Specialist directly by phone:** Luxury villa agencies excel when you speak to an agent. Tell them your dates, guest count, preferred Italian region, and explicit request: *"We require a villa with daily housekeeping and a private chef for [X] meals a day."*
3. **Check Financial Protection:** If booking your flights, villa, and transfers together through a UK provider (like CV Villas or Scott Dunn), ensure the package is **ATOL** and **ABTA** protected for peace of mind.

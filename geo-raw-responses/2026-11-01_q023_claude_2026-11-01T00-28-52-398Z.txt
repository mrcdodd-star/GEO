--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
The official terms I could retrieve were only partial, so check the full page before you book. Here is what they say.

**Cancelling by you**
- Oliver's Travels says its policy reflects each owner's individual terms. 
Once the deposit has been paid it is non-refundable, as a cancellation wouldn't leave the owner enough time to find a substitute booking.
 The excerpt I saw didn't include the full schedule for the balance payment.
- A third-party review site says a 35% deposit is required. It also says 
if you cancel less than 12 weeks before departure, only the balance of the holiday may be refunded and the deposit is non-refundable.
 That site isn't official, so confirm the figures with Oliver's Travels.

**48-hour cooling-off**
- Oliver's Travels offers a 48-hour "no quibble" cancellation. 
It doesn't apply to bookings that start within 14 days of the booking being placed.
 
After the 48 hours, or if the booking doesn't qualify, the standard cancellation terms apply in full.


**If the owner cancels**
- 
If the owner cancels and no alternative dates can be offered, you get a voucher for the value of everything you've paid. You can use it on any other Oliver's Travels holiday home.
 Older terms quoted by a customer on MoneySavingExpert promised a full refund instead. So the policy appears to have changed, and it's worth checking which version applies to your booking.
- 
They aren't liable for extra costs such as travel.


**Things to know as a UK customer**
- Oliver's Travels describes itself as an accommodation-only agent. In a dispute during Covid, it said this meant 
it isn't bound by Package Travel Regulations
, so you may have less protection than with a package holiday. Customers also reported that Oliver's Travels referred them to the owners for refunds.
- 
They strongly recommend comprehensive travel insurance with cancellation cover, as they provide none.
 I'd arrange it when you book.
- If you paid by credit card, Section 75 may offer extra protection in some circumstances. I haven't verified how it applies here.

You can read the full terms at https://www.oliverstravels.com/terms-and-conditions/. Your booking confirmation will show the exact deposit and balance dates for your property.

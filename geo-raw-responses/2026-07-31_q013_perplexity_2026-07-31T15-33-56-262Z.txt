The **Connaught** is in **Mayfair**, on **Carlos Place / Mount Street**, which puts it in one of London’s most polished, central luxury neighborhoods, close to **Bond Street, Hyde Park, and high-end shops and galleries**.[1][2][10][12] Its vibe is usually described as **quietly glamorous, clubby, warm, and discreetly luxurious**—more like a refined English house or members’ club than a flashy hotel.[4][5][7]

The **Dorchester** is also in **Mayfair**, but it sits in a more prominent position on **Park Lane**, right by **Hyde Park**, which gives it a broader, more immediately “grand hotel” presence in central London. In vibe terms, it’s generally seen as **more opulent, classic, and high-profile** than the Connaught, while the Connaught is often perceived as **softer, more intimate, and more under-the-radar**.

If you’re choosing between them:
- **The Connaught**: best for **quiet elegance, intimacy, and a polished neighborhood feel**.[4][5][7]
- **The Dorchester**: best for **big landmark energy, Park Lane prestige, and traditional five-star glamour**.

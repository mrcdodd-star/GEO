## 🔍 What to Think About When Booking

- **Travel & Logistics**: Getting to European villas often means flights and transfers. UK stays might make arriving (and departing) quicker and easier, especially for shorter trips.

- **Facilities**: Large groups often want kitchens that can cater for many, space to gather (both indoors & out), private amenities (pool, spa, games), and potentially event space if you're holding a celebration.

- **Privacy & Local Rules**: Some European houses (especially in smaller towns or protected areas) have limits on sound, parties, or events. Make sure the house’s owner/site allows what you want to do.

- **Cost & Value**: Renting an entire mansion can look expensive, but split among 20+ people it often becomes very reasonable. Be sure to check what’s included (cleaning, maid service, linen) and what's extra.

- **Seasonality**: High season (summer, school holidays) is more expensive and it’s harder to get big places. Booking well in advance pays off.

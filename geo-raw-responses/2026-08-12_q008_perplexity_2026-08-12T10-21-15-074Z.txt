--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
If you want a single name, **Corinthia London** is one of the strongest contenders for the **best spa** in London. Multiple travel sources describe its ESPA Life spa as exceptional, with one guide calling it among the city’s best and noting it spans four floors with extensive wellness facilities.[9]

That said, the answer depends on what you mean by “best”:

- **Best overall wellness experience:** **Corinthia London** — repeatedly highlighted for scale and breadth of facilities.[9][14]
- **Best for luxury prestige:** **Mandarin Oriental Hyde Park** — singled out for “Oriental luxury” and consistently praised in spa-hotel roundups.[4][9]
- **Best award-recognized spa:** **The Lanesborough** — Good Housekeeping notes it won a “Best Spa in London” award.[8]
- **Best expert-reviewed spa hotels:** **Raffles London at The OWO**, **Corinthia London**, and **Pan Pacific London** appear near the top of a recent expert ranking.[10]

If you want just one recommendation, I’d pick **Corinthia London** for the strongest all-around spa reputation.[9][14]

## What to Think About First

Here are some trade-offs to consider so the reunion hits the right balance:

- **Flight time vs warmth**: The warmer you go, often the longer the flight (and costlier). Destinations within ~4–5 hours tend to offer mild to pleasantly warm winter/early‐spring weather, while truly hot places (25-30 °C) tend to be farther away. ([seekholidays.co.uk](https://www.seekholidays.co.uk/blog/best-winter-sun-destinations-from-uk/?utm_source=openai))  
- **Vehicle & transfers**: Especially with kids, seniors or lots of luggage, shorter airport transfers help (e.g. stay in well-linked resorts or islands with compact infrastructure).  
- **Variety of things to do**: Beach + nature/walking + culture + good food tends to make for a more memorable reunion than just “sit by the pool.”  
- **Accommodation type**: Villas or vacation homes with kitchens/pools give more flexibility and communal space; resorts or hotels are easier for full service.

## Sample Itineraries for a Week Reunion

Here’s how you could structure a 7-day trip to balance rest, connection, and adventure.

### Option A: Island Beach + Nature Escape (Canary Islands)

- Day 1: Arrival, settle into villa/hotel, easy evening meal.
- Day 2-3: Beach and pool days; maybe a boat trip or dolphin/whale watching.
- Day 4: Interior exploration—volcano parks, scenic lookout points.
- Day 5: Family activity (e.g. water park, cultural village, market).
- Day 6: Free day for smaller groups (read, spa, optional excursions).
- Day 7: Relax, pack, farewell dinner.

### Option B: Culture + Coast (Southern Spain or Morocco)

- Day 1: Arrive and settle; try local cuisine.
- Day 2: Beach/coastal town day.
- Day 3: Historic city visit (e.g. Seville, Granada, Marrakesh).
- Day 4: Slow exploration—markets, gardens, small villages.
- Day 5: Outdoor activity—hiking or nature reserve.
- Day 6: Day trip somewhere scenic (beaches, hills, seaside walks).
- Day 7: Together time, shopping & relaxing before departure.

## When to go

- If you're planning a trip in **late autumn or winter** (October-March), the Canary Islands are one of the safest bets for warmth, as are Madeira and Morocco. ([next-stop-anywhere.com](https://next-stop-anywhere.com/travel-ideas/winter-sun-destinations/?utm_source=openai))  
- For warmer sea and longer sun days, **early autumn (Sept/Oct)** or **spring** are great. Tourist numbers are lower, and prices are often better than height of summer. ([theweek.com](https://theweek.com/culture-life/travel/make-the-most-of-shoulder-season-with-a-last-minute-break?utm_source=openai))

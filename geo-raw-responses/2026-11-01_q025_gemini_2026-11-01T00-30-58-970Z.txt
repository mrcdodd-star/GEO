### Important Things to Check When Booking a European Villa from the U.S.

1. **En-Suite Bathrooms:** In North America, a 6-bedroom house usually has 5–6 bathrooms. In Europe, older historic villas (especially in Italy, France, or Spain) might have 6 bedrooms but only 2 or 3 bathrooms. Check the bathroom-to-bedroom ratio carefully.
2. **Air Conditioning (A/C):** A/C is **not** standard in central/northern Europe (and even in parts of Southern Europe for older homes). Look for "Full Air Conditioning" rather than "Partial" (which often means A/C in bedrooms only or portable units).
3. **Transportation & Access:** Large European villas are often down narrow winding roads or in rural locations. Check if you will need multiple rental cars or if large passenger vans can physically access the driveway.
4. **Tourist Taxes & Utilities:** In many European countries (like Italy, France, and Spain), local city tourist taxes (*tassa di soggiorno*) are collected in cash upon arrival. Additionally, some villa rentals charge electricity or heating usage separately by the meter; confirm if this is included in your total price.

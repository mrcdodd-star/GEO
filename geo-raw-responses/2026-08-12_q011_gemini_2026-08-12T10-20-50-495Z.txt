--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
Claridge’s and The Dorchester are two of London’s most legendary ultra-luxury "Grand Dames". Both are located in the prestigious neighborhood of Mayfair, yet they offer entirely distinct atmospheres, design styles, and views. 

Both hotels have recently undergone massive multi-million-pound renovations, meaning you are guaranteed to experience them at their absolute peak.

---

### 1. Vibe, History & Aesthetic
*   **Claridge’s:** Often called the "annexe to Buckingham Palace" due to its long history with royalty, Claridge’s is the ultimate monument to **timeless Art Deco glamour** and high society. Its iconic black-and-white marbled lobby and mirrored foyer capture the elegance of the Jazz Age. It is chic, sleek, fashion-forward, and feels like a beautifully orchestrated stage where everyone—from the staff to the guests—is impeccably dressed.
*   **The Dorchester:** If Claridge’s is old royalty, The Dorchester is **Old Hollywood and theatrical grandeur**. Historically beloved by stars like Elizabeth Taylor and Marilyn Monroe, it is ceremonial, grand, and bold. Following a massive multi-phase redesign by interior maestro Pierre-Yves Rochon, the hotel features a slightly eccentric, lavish, and highly colorful English aesthetic—complete with gold-leaf capitals, black marble columns, and whimsical floral patterns. 

### 2. Location & Views
*   **Claridge’s (Deep Mayfair):** Tucked away on the corner of Brook Street and Davies Street. It is entirely urban and inward-looking; there are no sweeping park views here, but the trade-off is unmatched convenience. You are just steps from the luxury boutiques of Bond Street, the art galleries of Mayfair, and the bustling high streets of Oxford Street. 
*   **The Dorchester (Park Lane):** Situated prominently on Park Lane. Its single greatest asset is its setting: it sits directly opposite **Hyde Park**. If you book a park-facing room, you are treated to a stunning green canopy right outside your window. The slight drawback is that crossing the busy lanes of Park Lane to head deeper into central London can feel a bit less walkable, though it is still only a 10-minute stroll to central Mayfair and green open spaces are right at your doorstep.

### 3. Rooms & Suites
*   **Claridge’s:** The rooms here are an impeccable blend of heritage and subtle modernity. Many suites are designed with a soothing, feminine Art Deco palette—featuring scalloped mohair furniture in pastel greens, corals, and cream Chinoiserie. Following a massive, highly publicized architectural expansion, Claridge's boasts incredibly spacious newly built penthouses.
*   **The Dorchester:** Following its Pierre-Yves Rochon refresh, the rooms are exceptionally plush, comforting, and quintessentially English. Expect soft pastel shades of pink, heather blue, and yellow, detailed crown moldings, and ultra-luxurious fabrics. The crown jewel is the *Harlequin Penthouse*, which features a massive private terrace looking directly over the tree line of Hyde Park.

### 4. Dining, Bars & Afternoon Tea
*   **Claridge’s:** 
    *   **The Food & Drink:** Claridge’s features *Claridge’s Restaurant*, the cozy, clubby *Fumoir* (a dark-velvet sanctuary for cocktails), and *The Painter’s Room* (a pink-boudoir-style bar focused on light, creative drinks). 
    *   **The Tea:** Claridge's is universally considered the crown jewel of London’s **Afternoon Tea** scene. Served in the magnificent, high-ceilinged Foyer & Reading Room, it is a bucket-list ritual.
*   **The Dorchester:** 
    *   **The Food & Drink:** The Dorchester is an absolute powerhouse for culinary lovers. It plays host to **Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester**, which boasts **3 Michelin stars**. It also features *The Grill by Tom Booton* (a modern, fun take on traditional British grill food) and the legendary *China Tang* (serving high-end Cantonese in a lavish 1930s Shanghai setting).
    *   **The Bars:** *Vesper Bar* (a James Bond-themed cocktail lounge paying tribute to author Ian Fleming's ties to the hotel) and the opulent, piano-led *Artists’ Bar*.

### 5. Spa & Wellness
*   **Claridge’s:** Claridge's wins handily if wellness is a priority. It features a spectacular, multi-story subterranean spa designed by André Fu. Inspired by Kyoto’s Zen gardens, it features a **magnificent swimming pool** with a vaulted ceiling, stone columns, and poolside cabanas. 
*   **The Dorchester:** The *Dorchester Spa* is a gorgeous, Forbes Five-Star oasis with a dreamy cream-and-pink aesthetic, offering top-tier treatments, a steam room, and a sauna. However, **The Dorchester does not have an on-site swimming pool** (guests must cross the street to use the pool at its sister property, 45 Park Lane).

---

### The Verdict: Which should you choose?

*   **Choose Claridge’s if:** You want to be in the very heart of Mayfair's shopping district, appreciate sleek Art Deco design, want an exceptional on-site pool and subterranean spa experience, and plan on booking the most iconic afternoon tea in the world. It is the ultimate "see-and-be-seen" London experience.
*   **Choose The Dorchester if:** You want beautiful views over Hyde Park, prioritize world-class dining (such as 3-Michelin-starred French gastronomy or high-end Cantonese), and prefer a glamorous, theatrical "Old Hollywood meets English country manor" interior style.

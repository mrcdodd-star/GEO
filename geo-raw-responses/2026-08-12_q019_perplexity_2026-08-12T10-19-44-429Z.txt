--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
Dorchester Collection **Diamond Club** is an invitation-only program for selected **travel agencies**, and when you book through an eligible advisor it typically includes a **confirmed room upgrade at booking**, **daily breakfast for two**, and a **hotel credit** of about **100 units of local currency** per stay, plus VIP-style service and sometimes early check-in/late check-out depending on the property and booking terms.[1][2][3][6]

The most consistently reported **travel agent benefits** are:

- **Confirmed one-category upgrade at the time of booking** rather than “subject to availability” at check-in.[1][2][5][6]
- **Breakfast for two daily**.[2][5][6]
- **Property credit** of roughly **100 local currency units** per stay, though some agency pages describe it in USD-equivalent terms and property rules can vary.[2][5][8]
- **VIP treatment / ambassador support**, with hotel management aware of the stay and assistance from a Diamond Club team.[2][7]
- **Early check-in / late check-out** may be available, but this is often listed as *subject to availability* rather than guaranteed.[5][8]
- **Unlimited internet access** is mentioned in some program descriptions.[2][4]

A key point is that **guests cannot enroll directly**; the benefits are accessed through an **invited agency or advisor** that is part of the Diamond Club network.[1][3][6][7]

There is some variation across sources on details like the exact credit amount and whether upgrades are “guaranteed” versus “subject to availability,” but the strongest common theme is that **Diamond Club is more generous than standard preferred-partner perks and is designed for bookings through select luxury travel advisors**.[1][2][4][7]

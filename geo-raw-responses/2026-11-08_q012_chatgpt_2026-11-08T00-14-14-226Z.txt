## What to Ask / Check Before Booking

To make sure you get everything you want, here are things to make sure are clear:

1. **What’s “included” vs “extra cost**  
   Private chef, transfers, yacht days, etc., may be optional and extra; check exactly what the base rate covers.  

2. **Concierge availability & responsiveness**  
   Do they have an in-destination office or representative? Is there 24/7 contact? ([uncompromised.travel](https://uncompromised.travel/stays/luxury-villa-rental-company-index-2026?utm_source=openai))  

3. **Transfers arrangements**  
   Airport-villa travel: private car, minivan, chauffeur, flights if needed, meet & greet.  

4. **Staffing: chef, butler, housekeeping**  
   Are they included? How often? Are menus tailor-made? Do they staff full time?  

5. **Activities & experiences**  
   Do they arrange local experiences (boat trips, spa, culture)? How personalised are they?  

6. **Contract & protection**  
   ATOL/ABTA protection, cancellation terms, deposit schedules. ([luxgetaway.com](https://luxgetaway.com/best-luxury-villa-rental-companies-compared/?utm_source=openai))

Oliver’s Travels acts as an agency on behalf of individual property owners. As a result, your booking is governed by **Oliver’s Travels’ standard Agency Terms & Conditions** as well as any **property-specific terms** set by the individual villa owner.

---

### Key Details of Oliver's Travels Cancellation Policy

#### 1. Cancellation by You (The Customer)
* **More than 12 weeks before departure:** 
  * If you cancel **more than 12 weeks** prior to your arrival date, any balance paid over the deposit will be refunded. 
  * However, the **deposit (typically 35%) is strictly non-refundable**.
* **12 weeks or fewer before departure:** 
  * Final balance payments are due **12 weeks before arrival**. 
  * Once paid (or within this 12-week window), all monies paid are **100% non-refundable**, as the owner usually cannot re-let the villa at short notice.

---

#### 2. Short-Term / Promotional Guarantees
* **48-Hour Window:** Oliver's Travels offers a 48-hour "no quibble" cancellation window from the time of booking (unless the booking starts within 14 days).
* **Promotional Schemes:** Certain promotional campaigns (e.g., *Free Cancellation Guarantee* or advance booking promotions for upcoming years) may allow extra flexibility. Always check the banner/listing details at the time of booking to see if special cancellation guarantees apply.

---

#### 3. Cancellation by the Owner or Oliver's Travels
* If the owner cancels your booking and no suitable alternative accommodation can be agreed upon, you are entitled to a **full refund of all monies paid** up to that point.
* Oliver’s Travels is not liable for secondary expenses incurred due to cancellation, such as non-refundable flights or third-party transfer fees.

---

#### 4. Additional Points to Keep in Mind
* **Travel Insurance:** Oliver’s Travels strongly advises taking out comprehensive holiday travel insurance at the time of booking to cover unexpected cancellations, travel disruptions, or medical emergencies.
* **Admin Fees:** Certain changes or cancellations made under special conditions may incur an administrative fee (typically £25 per person).

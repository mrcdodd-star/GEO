## Villa or resort?

- **Private villa:** You get 
privacy, space and freedom – and usually your own private swimming pool
. You also split the cost, and you can cook together or hire a chef for one or two nights.
- **Resort with villas or apartments:** This gives you more facilities, kids' clubs and less admin. It's easier if nobody wants to cook or organise.
- **Several houses in one complex:** This helps if some households want more space or a different pace.

## Practical tips

- **Book early.** 2027 villas are on sale now, and big-group properties go first. Some of the larger villas listed start at roughly £4,500–£6,500 a week, and prices rise in peak summer.
- **Think about timing.** Late May, June and September are warm, cheaper and less crowded than July and August. Many places are too hot for older relatives in peak August.
- **Check flights.** Make sure everyone has a direct flight from a UK airport near them. Heathrow and Gatwick aren't always the easiest for everyone, so check regional airports.
- **Plan access.** Check for stairs, pool depth and parking if there are older relatives or small children.
- **Decide on costs.** Agree up front whether to split by household or by head. Also agree what happens with shared costs such as food and car hire.
- **Keep some structure.** Plan one or two group activities, such as a boat trip, a long lunch or a family quiz night. Leave the rest of the week free.

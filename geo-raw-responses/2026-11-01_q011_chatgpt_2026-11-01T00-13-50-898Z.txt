## Tips for Booking from the UK

Since you’re based in the UK, here are a few things to help smooth the process and maximise value:

1. **Book early**, especially for July/August in Côte d’Azur / Saint-Tropez — demand & prices soar.  
2. **Clarify what’s included vs. extra charges**: some services (private chef, transport, food) cost more; check whether cleaning, linen, standby staff are in or out.  
3. **Check how concierge works in practice**: Is there a dedicated contact in English? Emergency support? Local on-site vs remote support?  
4. **Travel logistics & transport**: nearest airports (Nice, Marseille, Toulon, etc.), whether villa includes airport transfer; customs/border formalities; timing of arrivals.  
5. **Currency & payments**: many agencies quote in Euros — clarify deposits, cancellation policies, security deposits. Consider FX costs.  
6. **Insurance and contracts**: ensure the rental contract is clear; check insurance or liability for things like accidents, property damage etc.

The **Corinthia London** is most often cited as having the *best luxury hotel spa* in London, with **ESPA Life at Corinthia** consistently described as one of the city’s top – and largest – hotel spas.[6][2][4]

### Why Corinthia London is frequently ranked #1

- **ESPA Life at Corinthia** spans *four floors* and is designed more like a full wellness center than a typical hotel spa, with extensive thermal and relaxation facilities.[6][2]  
- Facilities include **vitality pools, amphitheatre-style sauna, marble hydrotherapy pool, steam rooms, relaxation pods and treatment rooms**, offering a wide range of therapies and wellness programs.[6][2]  
- Multiple independent guides and travel publications single it out as having *one of the best, if not the best, hotel spas in London*.[6][2][4]  
- It regularly appears at or near the top of “best spa hotel” lists, and is mentioned on traveler forums as *the standout choice* for a serious spa stay in a London luxury hotel.[6][12]  

### Other top-tier contenders you might consider

While Corinthia is the most commonly named “best” spa, a few other **luxury London hotels** are repeatedly highlighted for exceptional spas:

- **Raffles London at The OWO** – new ultra-luxury opening with a *world-class spa* and strong reviews from luxury travel specialists; often listed among the very best spa hotels in London.[6][13]  
- **Mandarin Oriental Hyde Park** – renowned for its **Oriental-inspired spa** and consistently featured in best spa hotel roundups.[5][6][2]  
- **Pan Pacific London** – modern luxury hotel whose spa gets outstanding guest feedback and appears on recent “best spa hotels” lists.[6][3]  
- **The Lanesborough** – its spa has *won “Best Spa in London” awards*, and is noted for highly polished service and serene interiors.[8][9]  
- **Bulgari Hotel London** – often recommended for a **relaxing swim** and sleek, contemporary spa facilities.[5][9]

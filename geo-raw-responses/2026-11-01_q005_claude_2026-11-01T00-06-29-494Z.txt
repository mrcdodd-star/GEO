--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
The best fit depends on your children's ages, your budget, and whether you want to fly. These are the main options.

## 1. All-inclusive resorts abroad
These are the easiest way to get meals and childcare in one package.
- **Package operators.** 
Every TUI All Inclusive package includes flights, accommodation, all meals, local drinks and airport transfers, and most hotels also include kids' clubs.
 Thomas Cook, On the Beach and Jet2 sell similar packages. 
Turkey is a standout for value, with large resorts that bundle pools, professional kids' clubs and evening shows into one price.

- **Club Med.** 
Its kids' club takes children from 4 months to 17 years and runs from 9:00am to 5:30pm, then again in the evenings.

- **Ikos (Greece).** At Ikos Oceania, 
the crèche, kids club and teens' club are all complimentary.
 At Ikos Aria, 
supervised activities run for children from six months to 17, and babies and younger children can join the Heroes Crèche.

- **Check the small print for under-4s.** 
Many kids' clubs start at 4.
 One guide says that at Ikos, 
the crèche from 6 months is paid sessions.
 Clubs often cost extra for the youngest children, so confirm this before you book.

## 2. UK hotels with free crèche sessions
You still have to travel, but there's no flying, and the childcare is Ofsted-registered. Meals are usually not all-inclusive, so you'd pay for dinner, though you don't have to cook.
- **Luxury Family Hotels group** (e.g. Ickworth in Suffolk, Fowey Hall in Cornwall, Woolley Grange in Wiltshire). 
90 minutes of free childcare per night is included in the stay.
 
Fowey Hall takes babies from 12 weeks.

- **Moonfleet Manor, Dorset.** 
It offers two hours of complimentary childcare per night's stay.

- **Crieff Hydro, Perthshire.** 
Childcare is free for bookings of two nights or more and children aged 2–12.

- **Gleneagles.** 
The kids' club is much applauded, and under-5s get complimentary food.


Policies change, and some of these sources are old. For example, 
Celtic Manor no longer has a crèche or kids' club.
 Check directly with the hotel.

## 3. Other options (from my own knowledge, so verify these)
- **Mark Warner and Neilson.** These beach-club holidays in Greece, Turkey and elsewhere have a reputation for generous, often free, childcare. The meals are usually half-board.
- **Catered ski chalets with nannies.** Operators such as Esprit offer these in winter. They're good if your children are old enough for ski school.
- **Villas with a chef and nanny.** These are the most flexible and the most expensive, usually through a luxury villa specialist.
- **Holiday parks (Center Parcs, Butlin's, Haven).** There's lots for children to do, but the childcare is limited. You'd mostly be supervising, so they're less of a true break.

## Questions that would help me narrow this down
- How old are the children? This matters most, because many clubs don't take under-4s.
- Do you want to fly, or stay in the UK?
- What's your rough budget and which dates, for example school holidays?
- Do you want all meals included, or just not to cook?

With those answers I can suggest a shortlist and check current prices.

### 📝 Key Criteria to Use When Choosing

To make sure you get exactly what you want, here are some filters and questions worth considering before booking:

1. **Location**  
   - Are you after the coast (Saint-Tropez, Cap d’Antibes, Nice)?  
   - Or more rural Provence (Luberon, Alpilles)?  
   Travel time from airports matters—especially Nice, Marseille, or Toulon.

2. **Size & Privacy**  
   - How many guests? Do you need 3–4 bedrooms or more like 8–12?  
   - Is inside/outside space crucial? Gardens, terraces, private access, etc.

3. **Service Level**  
   - Concierge: 24/7? Multilingual?  
   - Staff: housekeeper, cook/chef, chauffeur, possibly yacht or boat crewing.  
   - In-villa wellness, spa treatments, kids & family services if relevant.

4. **Extras and Experiences**  
   - Do you want boats/yachts? Beach club access? Wine tours?  
   - Restaurant reservations, helicopter transfers?  
   - Local experiences (markets, guided tours, etc.).

5. **Budget & Timing**  
   - Peak season (July–August) demand & cost skyrockets in areas like Saint-Tropez. Book early (often 6–12 months ahead for the best spots). ([polariusrealestate.com](https://polariusrealestate.com/en/rentals/french-riviera?utm_source=openai))  
   - In shoulder season (May, September), rates drop and supply increases.

## What to Prioritize When Choosing

To pick a provider that really stacks up well against The Thinking Traveller, check the following:

1. **Level of curation and quality control**  
   Do they personally inspect every villa? Are there local teams on the ground?

2. **Transparency in pricing**  
   Week-long rates displayed clearly, inclusions & exclusions laid out.

3. **Protection/insurance**  
   ABTA, ATOL or other financial guarantees—especially if the company sells packages or flights.

4. **Service around the stay**  
   Concierge, local managers, on-call support, extras (chefs, tours, etc.).

5. **Range vs Specialization**  
   Some providers focus on a few destinations with depth; others cover many places but may be less specialized.

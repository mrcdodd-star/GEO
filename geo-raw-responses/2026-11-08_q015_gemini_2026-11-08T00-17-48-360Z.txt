### 1. Best UK-Based Companies for Luxury Villas Abroad
If you are traveling abroad to the Mediterranean, Caribbean, or beyond, these UK-based specialists offer handpicked portfolios, concierge services, and local expertise.

* **The Thinking Traveller**
  * **Best For:** High-end Mediterranean luxury (Sicily, Puglia, Tuscany, Corsica, and the Greek Islands).
  * **Why They Stand Out:** Frequently voted *Best Villa Rental Company in the World* by *Condé Nast Traveller*. Their properties are 100% exclusive to them, meaning you won’t find them listed on open aggregators. They offer exceptional 24/7 on-the-ground support, private chefs, local experiences, and boat charters.
* **CV Villas**
  * **Best For:** Classic European elegance (Greece, Italy, France, Spain, Portugal, and the Caribbean).
  * **Why They Stand Out:** Established over 50 years ago, CV Villas is a staple of British luxury travel. They offer everything from charming seaside villas to their ultra-luxury **"ABOVE" Collection**, which features fully staffed estates with private infinity pools, helicopters, and private chefs. They also offer ATOL-protected flight packages.
* **Oliver’s Travels**
  * **Best For:** Unique luxury châteaux, grand historic villas, and modern architectural gems.
  * **Why They Stand Out:** Known for characterful properties, their portfolio ranges from French châteaux to luxury beach villas in Ibiza and Barbados. They offer a complimentary concierge service to arrange everything from airport transfers to private catering.
* **Scott Dunn & Abercrombie & Kent (A&K Villas)**
  * **Best For:** Ultra-high-net-worth bespoke service & fully managed villa holidays.
  * **Why They Stand Out:** If you want a zero-hassle, fully serviced villa experience, both operators excel at high-touch luxury travel. They can provide dedicated Scott Dunn nannies, private chauffeurs, in-villa sommeliers, and bespoke itineraries tailored specifically for UK families and groups.
* **Simpson Travel**
  * **Best For:** Boutique luxury family villas and romantic adult-only retreats in Greece, Mallorca, France, and Italy.
  * **Why They Stand Out:** Known for unparalleled customer service and child-friendly luxury (crèches, shaded pools, nanny services), making them a favorite for families looking for high quality without fuss.

### Tips for UK Buyers:
1. **Financial Protection:** If booking abroad through a UK tour operator (like *CV Villas* or *Simpson Travel*), opting for a flight-and-villa package guarantees **ATOL protection**.
2. **Private Chef / Staffing:** Most of these platforms allow you to add "fully staffed" options (chef, housekeeper, butler) at checkout or via their concierge.
3. **Peak Season Windows:** For July/August peak times in the Med or UK summer holidays, book **8 to 12 months in advance**, as prime beachfront and Cotswold estates sell out early.

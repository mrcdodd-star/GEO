## What to Think About

Here are important criteria and questions to ask; it helps to be clear on these when you contact venues.

1. **Guest numbers & accommodation**
   - How many guests will attend?
   - How many can stay on-site, and what are sleeping arrangements?

2. **Exclusivity**
   - Is the château rented out to you alone, or will there be other events same weekend?
   - Some places offer booking from Friday to Sunday (or Monday) for full setup & evening after. ([votre-chateau-de-famille.com](https://www.votre-chateau-de-famille.com/en/usages/weddings/?utm_source=openai))

3. **Facilities & services**
   - Catering: in-house or can you bring your own?
   - Do they provide dresses, furniture, cleanup, and other essentials?
   - What are their restrictions: curfew, noise, fireworks, etc.? ([frenchweddingstyle.com](https://www.frenchweddingstyle.com/venue-finder/?utm_source=openai))

4. **Budget**
   - Typical whole-estate hire can start from a few thousand euros up to tens of thousands, depending on guest count, region, level of luxury. ([marketplace.thevenueadvisors.com](https://marketplace.thevenueadvisors.com/en/availability/2026/?utm_source=openai))  
   - Peak season costs (May-September) are much higher than off-peak.

5. **Location & access**
   - How easy is travel for guests? Proximity to airports / major train stations can matter.
   - What is the transport for guests between the venue and accommodation if off-site?

6. **Legal & logistical**
   - Do you need official permissions (especially for outdoor ceremonies or fireworks)?
   - Insurance, stage lighting, power, sound, etc.

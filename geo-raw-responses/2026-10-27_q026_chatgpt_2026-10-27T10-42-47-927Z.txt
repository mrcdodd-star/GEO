## Advice for Choosing a Villa Rental Agency

When deciding which agency to use, here are key things to check. Keep these questions in mind:

1. **Inspection and accuracy**  
   Does the agency or its staff personally visit each villa, or are you relying on owner-uploaded photos? (Doorways, Tuscany Now & More, Le Collectionist, etc. do inspections.) ([uncompromised.travel](https://uncompromised.travel/stays/luxury-villa-rental-company-index-2026?utm_source=openai))

2. **Transparent pricing & fees**  
   Are all fees (cleaning, concierge, tourist tax) clearly posted? Are deposit vs balance terms visible? Luxury villa agencies like Le Collectionist and CV Villas rank high for this. ([uncompromised.travel](https://uncompromised.travel/stays/luxury-villa-rental-company-index-2026?utm_source=openai))

3. **Support & local presence**  
   You want someone you can contact while you're in Italy—local staff, concierge lines, etc. This matters especially if something goes wrong. ([villavacations.com](https://villavacations.com/how-to-choose-a-villa-rental-company/?utm_source=openai))

4. **Deposit policies and protections**  
   Look at cancellation policies, what happens if the villa is unavailable, and whether you get a refund or an alternate villa. Agencies like Doorways promise to find a comparable villa if yours falls through. ([villavacations.com](https://villavacations.com/how-to-choose-a-villa-rental-company/?utm_source=openai))

5. **Reviews & repeat clients**  
   Companies with high rates of repeat customers and strong global reviews are more reliable. Tuscany Now & More and Italian Villa Specialists (among others) report high repeat-client rates and strong trust. ([luxuryvillascout.com](https://luxuryvillascout.com/italy-specialists?utm_source=openai))

## ✅ What to Look for When Comparing

To pick which alternative is right for you, keep the following in mind:

1. **Property inspection / vetting** – Does the agency personally visit and check each listing? Top-tier ones almost always do.  
   
2. **Staff on the ground & emergency support** – Having a local contact makes solving issues far easier.  

3. **Transparent pricing** – Weekly price listings, inclusive of taxes and fees.  

4. **Traveler reviews & brand trust** – ABTA, ATOL, Which? “Recommended Provider”, etc., plus guest reviews.  

5. **Additional services** – Private chef, boats, transfers, experiences make a big difference.

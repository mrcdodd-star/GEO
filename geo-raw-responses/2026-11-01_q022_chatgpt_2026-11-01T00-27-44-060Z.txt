## What UK-Based Travellers Should Consider

Because you’re in the UK, these are specific things that will matter:

- **Travel time & costs**: For European destinations (e.g. Corsica, Sicily, Greece), flights, transfers, etc. can add up. The Thinking Traveller’s villas are often further from large airports or in less touristy areas, so factor in car rentals, etc. Oliver’s Travels might have more UK-based options or ones closer to major airports.

- **Inclusivity of costs**: Check what’s included (heating, electricity, cleaning mid-stay, weekly cleans, linen/towel changes). Both companies include many things, but sometimes extras are chargeable. The Thinking Traveller is pretty transparent about what's included vs optional. ([thethinkingtraveller.com](https://www.thethinkingtraveller.com/faq?utm_source=openai))

- **Booking policies**: cancellation terms, payment schedule, damage deposits, etc. Oliver’s Travels often has high security deposits on premium properties. ([booking.oliverstravels.com](https://booking.oliverstravels.com/api/v2/pdf/brochure//britain-ireland/yorkshire/eden-house?unbranded=1&utm_source=openai))

- **Authenticity / local experience**: If living like a local, quiet, authentic surroundings are important to you, Thinking Traveller probably edges it. Their model emphasises character, local knowledge, and authentic settings.

- **Group size & special requirements**: If you're travelling with a large family, special occasion, etc., Oliver’s might offer more UK options or quirky large homes; Thinking Traveller has large villas too but more limited by geography.

## Pros & Cons: Summarised

### Oliver’s Travels: Pros
- Wide variety, including UK and off-the-beaten-path.
- Unique, quirky homes.
- Big selection so you may find more that match particular whims.
- Established brand, many awards.

### Oliver’s Travels: Cons
- Quality and service can vary between properties (since many are third-party).
- Premium pricing.
- Sometimes customers complain about surprise charges or less-than-perfect amenities. ([feefo.com](https://www.feefo.com/en-GB/reviews/olivers-travels?utm_source=openai))

### The Thinking Traveller: Pros
- Highly curated, consistent standard.
- High-touch service from local specialists before/after travel. ([thethinkingtraveller.com](https://www.thethinkingtraveller.com/what-to-expect?utm_source=openai))  
- Villas are exclusive, so less risk of overbooking, conflicting listings.  
- Strong customer satisfaction and awards record.  

### The Thinking Traveller: Cons
- Limited to Mediterranean areas; nothing much in the UK or beyond those specific regions.  
- Weekly-only bookings most of the time; less flexibility.  
- Often higher base price, especially for premium or Think Exquisite villas.

## Which One Might Be Best for You?

Here are some scenarios to help you decide:

- **You’re staying in the UK / want a local cottage or manor** → Oliver’s Travels gives you more UK-based property options.  

- **Extraordinary, luxury villa in a Mediterranean location, with high service and consistency** → The Thinking Traveller probably best.  

- **Variety, flexibility, quirky style over consistency** → Oliver’s Travels wins.  

- **Don’t want surprises, want a super smooth holiday, everything covered** → Thinking Traveller.

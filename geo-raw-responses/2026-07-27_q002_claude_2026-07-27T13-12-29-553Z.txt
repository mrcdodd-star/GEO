Here are some of the **best electric motorcycles for daily city commuting**, based on well-established models known for their performance, range, and practicality:

---

## 🏍️ Best Electric Motorcycles for Daily City Commuting

---

### 1. **Zero S**
- **Best for:** All-around urban commuting
- **Range:** ~100–140 miles (city)
- One of the most popular and well-rounded electric motorcycles on the market. It's lightweight, agile, and has a strong charging ecosystem. The torque delivery is instant, making it perfect for stop-and-go city traffic.

---

### 2. **Zero SR/F**
- **Best for:** Performance commuters
- **Range:** ~100–150 miles (city)
- A step up from the Zero S, the SR/F offers more power and a more premium riding experience. It supports fast charging and has a smartphone app for ride customization.

---

### 3. **Energica Eva Ribelle**
- **Best for:** Premium city riding
- **Range:** ~90–130 miles
- An Italian-made motorcycle with sporty styling and impressive DC fast charging capability. It's built for riders who want performance and style on city streets.

---

### 4. **Harley-Davidson LiveWire One**
- **Best for:** Brand recognition + city performance
- **Range:** ~146 miles (city)
- A more affordable spin-off of the original LiveWire, it offers a refined, premium riding experience with solid range and fast charging. Great for riders who want a trusted brand.

---

### 5. **BMW CE 04**
- **Best for:** Urban-focused design (scooter-style)
- **Range:** ~80 miles
- Technically a maxi-scooter, it's ideal for city commuting with its futuristic design, large storage, and practical features like a full-color display and optional fast charger.

---

### 6. **Kawasaki Ninja e-1 / Z e-1**
- **Best for:** Entry-level commuters
- A newer entry into the electric space from a trusted brand. Designed specifically for urban use, it's lightweight and easy to maneuver. Range is modest (~40–50 miles) but suits short daily commutes well.

---

### 7. **Super Soco TC Max**
- **Best for:** Budget-friendly commuting
- **Range:** ~75–100 miles
- An affordable, lightweight option that's easy to ride and maintain. Great for newer riders or those looking for a no-frills daily commuter.

---

### 8. **Vmoto Soco CPx**
- **Best for:** Delivery/courier or practical commuting
- Designed with practicality in mind, it features removable batteries (great for apartment dwellers) and a solid build for city use.

---

## 🔑 Key Factors to Consider When Choosing

| Factor | What to Look For |
|---|---|
| **Range** | At least 60–100 miles for a comfortable daily commute |
| **Charging Speed** | DC fast charging is a big plus |
| **Weight** | Lighter bikes are easier to handle in traffic |
| **Storage** | Under-seat or top box compatibility |
| **Connectivity** | App integration for ride stats & diagnostics |
| **Price** | Budget options start ~$3,000; premium models can exceed $20,000 |

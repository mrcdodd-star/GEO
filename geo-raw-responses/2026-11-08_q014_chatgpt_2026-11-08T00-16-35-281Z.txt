### 🕒 Things to Consider for an August Booking

- **Advance booking is essential.** August is peak time in the Algarve. Many villas/bookings require Saturday-to-Saturday minimum stays and fill up early. ([reddit.com](https://www.reddit.com/r/Algarve/comments/1u8rvbq/where_do_people_find_short_term_lets_on_the/?utm_source=openai))  
- **Flexible arrival days** could help: villas sometimes open up between standard 7-night bookings.  
- **Extra costs**: staff (housekeeping, cook), pool heating, baby beds, etc., may not always be included.  
- **Transport & proximity**: If you don’t want to hire a car, staying near amenities like beaches, shops, restaurants is useful (especially for a group of 12).  
- **Safety & COVID-related policies**: make sure cancellation and hygiene policies are clear.

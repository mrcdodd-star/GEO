With 16 people, three toddlers and grandparents, I'd book one big villa or a cluster of adjoining ones. A hotel is hard to make work for a group this size. One guide puts it this way: for a group this mixed, 
a hotel simply does not function
. For toddlers, 
a private villa with a pool, a garden, and somewhere safe to roam is enormously more valuable than any museum
.

**My top picks from the UK**

1. **Algarve, Portugal** is the safest all-round choice. It's described as 
Europe's most reliable family destination
. Flights are short and direct from most UK airports, and there's a lot of large-villa stock. Beaches are easy and the Atlantic keeps it a little less scorching than the Med. The sea can be rough, so a villa with its own pool helps.

2. **Tuscany or Umbria, Italy** suits you if you want countryside and good food more than beach time. One operator specialises in 
family villas in Tuscany, Umbria and Puglia, which work particularly well for large and extended families
. One guide notes 
July temperatures are warm but rarely extreme, which is more manageable than coastal Sardinia or Greece in high summer
. Choose a villa with air conditioning throughout, not only in the bedrooms.

3. **Corfu or the Ionian Islands, Greece** are good if you want calm, sheltered water. In Corfu, 
shallow, sheltered bays with calm water are ideal for young swimmers, while Venetian architecture and hillside monasteries give grandparents plenty to explore
. The Algarve and Ionian Islands are both recommended for toddlers because of 
calm beaches and reliable sunshine
. The catch is that there's often a transfer from the airport, and the heat is stronger in July and August.

4. **Costa del Sol, Spain** is the easiest option logistically. It has lots of flights and plenty of big villas. It also has 
championship golf for grandparents, sandy beaches for children, and Marbella's Old Town for everyone
. It will be hot and busy in peak summer.

**Other options**
- **Corsica** is reachable by flight or overnight ferry, and has villas with private pools.
- **Croatia** has some 
excellent-value family bases near Dubrovnik for larger groups
.

**Practical tips for your group**
- **Villa choice:** Pick one with a fenced or separately gated pool, or a toddler pool. One Corsican villa even has 
a separate swimming pool for toddlers
.
- **Mobility:** Check for step-free access and ground-floor bedrooms for the grandparents.
- **Location:** Stay within about 20–30 minutes of a supermarket, a pharmacy and a beach.
- **Transfers:** Keep them under 90 minutes. Long drives with toddlers in the car are the main thing that sours these trips.
- **Dates:** Early to mid-June, or early September, is cooler and cheaper, with fewer crowds. If you're tied to school holidays, book early. Villas for 16 people go fast, so aim for the autumn.
- **Rest time:** Plan around nap times, with a shaded outdoor dining area and a loose schedule. Share childcare so the parents and grandparents each get a break.

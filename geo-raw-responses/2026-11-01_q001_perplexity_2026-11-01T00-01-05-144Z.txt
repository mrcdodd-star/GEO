For a group of 16 across three generations, **Menorca** would be my first pick for next summer: it’s known for family-friendly beaches and a quieter feel than some other Balearic islands, while its coves and coastal villages give older relatives plenty to enjoy too.[5]

The key is to make the **accommodation** work for the group: look for a single large villa or adjoining apartments with a shaded pool, air-conditioning, a cot/high chair, and a supermarket or town nearby. For toddlers, keep transfers short and choose a beach with easy access; self-catering makes naps and flexible mealtimes simpler.[6][9]

Other good fits:
- **Algarve, Portugal** — strong choice for sandy beaches and family facilities, with UK flights around two and a half hours.[4][9]
- **Lake Garda, Italy** — a lake-and-mountains alternative with family attractions, including Gardaland’s Peppa Pig area.[7]
- **Brittany or Normandy, France** — worth considering if you’d rather drive and take the ferry than fly.[6]

For 16 people, start searching early for a place that can actually accommodate everyone comfortably; if you share your budget, preferred travel month and whether you want beach, villa or resort, I can narrow it down.

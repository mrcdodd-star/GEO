Staying in a castle in the UK is one of the most popular historic travel experiences, ranging from luxury 5-star castle hotels to self-catering medieval towers and gatehouses. 

The top options across England, Scotland, and Wales, along with how to book them, include:

---

### **Top Castle Hotels in England**

* **Thornbury Castle (Gloucestershire)**  
  * **Highlights:** The only Tudor castle operating as a luxury hotel in England. King Henry VIII and Anne Boleyn famously stayed here in 1535.  
  * **Experience:** Four-poster beds, stone fireplaces, grand dining rooms, and manicured gardens.
* **Amberley Castle (West Sussex)**  
  * **Highlights:** A 900-year-old medieval castle set within a curtain wall and a dry moat.  
  * **Experience:** Pure romance—complete with a portcullis that lowers at night, fine dining, and peaceful gardens.
* **Langley Castle (Northumberland)**  
  * **Highlights:** A genuine 14th-century fortified medieval castle with 7-foot-thick walls.  
  * **Experience:** Rooms inside the original keep feature four-poster beds, sauna suites, and window seats set into ancient stonework.
* **Hever Castle (Kent)**  
  * **Highlights:** The childhood home of Anne Boleyn.  
  * **Experience:** Stay in luxury Bed & Breakfast rooms in the Edwardian wing adjacent to the castle, with access to the estate grounds and maze after hours.

---

### **Top Castle Hotels in Scotland**

* **Inverlochy Castle (Highlands)**  
  * **Highlights:** Nestled at the foot of Ben Nevis, Queen Victoria once stayed here and claimed she "never saw a lovelier or more romantic spot."  
  * **Experience:** Ultra-luxurious 5-star country house castle with fine dining and stunning lake/mountain views.
* **Glenapp Castle (Ayrshire)**  
  * **Highlights:** A 19th-century Scots Baronial castle hidden in lush coastal woodland.  
  * **Experience:** Opulent suites, sea views over Ailsa Craig, falconry, private sea-safari boat trips, and fine dining.
* **Dalhousie Castle (Near Edinburgh)**  
  * **Highlights:** Scotland’s oldest continually inhabited castle (13th century).  
  * **Experience:** Features an on-site falconry center, a subterranean spa, and romantic dungeon dining.
* **Mingary Castle (Ardnamurchan Peninsula)**  
  * **Highlights:** A 13th-century coastal fortress sitting dramatically over the sea.  
  * **Experience:** Carefully restored with only four exclusive suites, offering an intimate and remote luxury stay.

---

### **Top Castle Hotels in Wales**

* **Château Rhianfa (Anglesey)**  
  * **Highlights:** A French-château-style fairytale castle built in 1849 overlooking the Menai Strait.  
  * **Experience:** Whimsical architecture, turret suites, and private sea-view gardens.
* **Roch Castle (Pembrokeshire)**  
  * **Highlights:** A 12th-century Norman castle standing high on a rocky footprint.  
  * **Experience:** Striking contrast of historic stone exteriors with sleek, modern luxury interiors and panoramic coastal views.
* **Ruthin Castle (Denbighshire)**  
  * **Highlights:** A medieval castle steeped in Welsh history, surrounded by woodland and peacocks.  
  * **Experience:** Known for hosting authentic historic medieval banquets alongside spa facilities.

---

### **Alternative Ways to Stay in a UK Castle**

If you prefer self-catering or more unusual options:

1. **The Landmark Trust ([landmarktrust.org.uk](https://www.landmarktrust.org.uk/))**  
   * **What it is:** A charity that restores historic buildings and rents them out as self-catering accommodation.  
   * **Examples:** You can rent entire gatehouses, iron-age stone towers, or fortresses like *Astley Castle* (Warwickshire) or *Clavell Tower* (Dorset).
2. **English Heritage & National Trust**  
   * Both organizations offer historic holiday cottages located right on castle grounds, such as *Peverell’s Tower at Dover Castle* or *Pendennis Castle Cottages* in Cornwall.
3. **Family-Friendly Themed Stays (Warwick Castle)**  
   * Offers glamping in the **Knight’s Village** or booking one of the luxurious **Tower Suites** inside the castle main building for a family adventure.

---

### **Booking Tips**

* **Book Castle Rooms vs. Grounds Cottages:** Always check whether your room is located *inside* the main castle keep or in an adjacent estate building/lodge. 
* **Book via Specialist Agencies:** Sites like **[Celtic Castles](https://www.celticcastles.com/)** specialize specifically in castle bookings across the UK and offer advice on room selection.
* **Reserve Far in Advance:** Popular castle hotels (especially for weekend getaways and weddings) book out 6 to 12 months in advance.

**The Dorchester**

Established in 1931, The Dorchester is an iconic hotel located on Park Lane in Mayfair, overlooking Hyde Park. Its Art Deco design and longstanding reputation have made it a favored destination for celebrities, royalty, and discerning travelers. The hotel features 250 rooms and 49 suites, many offering views of Hyde Park or the hotel's landscaped terraces. Dining options include the three-Michelin-starred Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester, The Grill serving British cuisine, and the Promenade, known for its traditional afternoon tea. The Dorchester also boasts a world-class spa and fitness facilities. ([en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dorchester?utm_source=openai))

**The Peninsula London**

Opened in September 2023, The Peninsula London is a modern addition to the city's luxury hotel scene, situated between Hyde Park Corner and Wellington Arch in Belgravia. Designed by architect Peter Marino, the hotel offers 190 guest rooms and suites, along with 25 luxury residences. The interiors reflect a blend of British craftsmanship and contemporary elegance. Dining venues include Canton Blue, a multi-level Cantonese restaurant, and Brooklands, a rooftop restaurant led by Michelin-starred chef Claude Bosi. The hotel also features a 25-meter pool, thermal suites, and a spa offering advanced therapies.

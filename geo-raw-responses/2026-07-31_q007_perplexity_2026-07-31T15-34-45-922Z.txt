## Atmosphere & setting

- **Claridge’s (Mayfair)**
  - Location: Brook Street, Mayfair.[17]  
  - Tea is served in a beautifully Art Deco space (the Reading Room / Foyer, depending on the current setup), with high ceilings, soft lighting and live piano.[6][11][16]  
  - The feel: **elegant, polished, but not stiff**. Many reviews highlight how welcoming staff make it feel special but relaxed.[6][18][12]

- **The Dorchester (Park Lane)**
  - Location: Park Lane, overlooking Hyde Park.[4]  
  - Tea typically served in The Promenade – a long, glamorous central space with chandeliers and plush seating.  
  - The feel: **grand and traditional**, with strong “old English luxury” vibes; slightly more formal in appearance but still friendly.

If you want a slightly more intimate, design‑focused room, Claridge’s wins; if you like a big, dramatic, classic hotel lobby promenade, The Dorchester may appeal more.

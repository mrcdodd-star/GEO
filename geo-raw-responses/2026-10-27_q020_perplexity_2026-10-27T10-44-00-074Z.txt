As of October 2026, Oliver’s Travels has a **4.7/5 Trustpilot rating from about 3,616 reviews**; 78% are five-star and 9% are one-star.[1] The overall picture is positive for booking support, but reviews also flag potential problems with property quality and what happens when a stay goes wrong.

- **What customers praise:** Helpful, responsive staff and a generally smooth booking process. Some reviewers also describe staff finding alternatives quickly when a property became unavailable.[1][5]
- **What customers criticise:** Reports include properties that did not match their online descriptions, cleanliness or maintenance problems, booking-price or availability discrepancies, and frustration with complaint handling.[2][4][6]
- **UK perspective:** Trustpilot includes UK-based reviewers, but the overall score covers customers internationally; it isn’t a UK-only rating.[3]

**Bottom line:** Reviews suggest the booking experience is often well regarded, but don’t treat the high score as a guarantee about the individual villa or the support you’ll receive during a dispute. Before booking, get key details confirmed in writing—especially availability, amenities, accessibility, cancellation terms, and who handles problems at the property.

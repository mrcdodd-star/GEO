**Things to know**
- **Budget:** Many peak-season (June–September) venues start around €8,000–€18,000 for exclusive hire. 
Minimum stays of 2 nights are common.
 Catering, accommodation and extras can add a lot, so check what's included.
- **Dry hire vs. packages:** Dry hire gives you the venue and you arrange everything else. A package covers catering and coordination. Some venues don't allow external caterers, so ask.
- **Getting there:** Northern châteaux (Normandy, Picardy, Loire) are easy by car via Eurotunnel or ferry. The Dordogne, Bordeaux area and Provence have good UK flight links.
- **Legal ceremony:** French civil law requires a civil ceremony at a mairie, with residency requirements and paperwork. Many UK couples do a legal ceremony in the UK and have a symbolic or blessing ceremony at the château. Check current requirements with the venue or a planner.

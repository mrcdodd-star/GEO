## Which Company Suits You Best?

Here are some scenarios to help you decide:

- If you want **maximum choice, quirky or large-scale properties (châteaux, castles, etc.)**, or if your trip could take you beyond the Mediterranean, **Oliver’s Travels** likely offers more options.  
- If your priority is **top-tier consistency, high-touch service, detailed local control, and villa exclusivity**, **The Thinking Traveller** is probably a safer bet.  
- For big group trips (reunions, weddings, etc.), Oliver’s has large homes and variety; but you may need to be cautious and verify property reviews, amenities, etc.  
- If travelling with children, looking for local cultural experiences or luxury touches (chef, guided tours, etc.), Thinking Traveller tends to deliver more reliably.  
- On budget: both are premium. If comparing two similar villas, you might find cheaper alternatives via Oliver’s if the same property appears elsewhere. But with Thinking Traveller, part of what you pay for is the assurance and the service.

### 3. Key Concierge Services to Expect

When booking a luxury villa with concierge service, you can tailor your package to include:

1. **In-Villa Hospitality:** Daily maid service, linen changes, private chef (breakfast, lunch, or formal dinners), mixologists, and dedicated butler/house manager.
2. **Transportation & Transfers:** Private airport chauffeured transfers, luxury supercar hire, helicopter shuttles from Nice airport to Saint-Tropez, or private yacht day-charters.
3. **Reservations & Access:** Hard-to-get reservations at top beach clubs, Michelin-starred restaurants, and private wine estate tours.
4. **Wellness & Family Services:** On-site spa therapists, yoga instructors, private tennis coaches, and vetted English-speaking nannies.

### 4. Tips for UK Travelers

* **Financial Protection:** If peace of mind around ABTA/ATOL protection is important, look at UK-headquartered travel providers (like CV Villas or Abercrombie & Kent) that can package flights and villa arrangements together.
* **Peak vs. Shoulder Season:** 
  * *Peak (July & August):* Requires booking 6–12 months in advance. Minimum stay is usually 1–2 weeks (Saturday to Saturday).
  * *Shoulder (May–June, September):* Warm weather, far fewer traffic bottlenecks along the Riviera coastal roads, and more flexibility with minimum stays.

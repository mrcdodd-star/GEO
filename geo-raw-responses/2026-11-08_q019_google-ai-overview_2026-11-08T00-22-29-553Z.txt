Choosing between Plum Guide and a specialist villa rental agency (such as CV Villas, Vintage Travel, The Thinking Traveller, or Oliver’s Travels) comes down to whether you want algorithmic/curated scale or deeply localized, hands-on hospitality.
While both promise you won't end up in a sketchy or misrepresented property, they operate with completely different philosophies.
At-a-Glance Comparison

Plum Guide
How it works: It acts as a curated marketplace (similar to a strict, high-end Michelin guide for rentals). They don’t just look at photos; they send "critics" or local testers to measure Wi-Fi speed, test the shower pressure, check the neighborhood noise levels, and evaluate the interior design.

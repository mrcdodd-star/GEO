Here are some UK-friendly alternatives to The Thinking Traveller. The company is known for 
curated, design-led villas across Sicily, Puglia, Tuscany, Corsica and the Greek islands, all exclusive to the brand
. I've grouped the alternatives by what they do best.

**Closest in style (curated, Mediterranean)**
- **Oliver's Travels** offers 
quirky, characterful homes, from medieval châteaux and treehouses to sleek beach villas and family farmhouses
. Its site says 
only the top 2% of the 100,000+ holiday homes its experts reviewed make it into the collection
. It also says 
Condé Nast Traveller readers have voted it one of the world's best villa rental companies ten years running
.
- **Wimco Villas, CV Villas, Scott Williams and Villatalk** are among 
the competitors usually listed alongside it
. Scott Williams is described as a 
boutique agency specialising in Mediterranean luxury villas
.

**Italy specialists**
- **Tuscany Now & More** has 
a portfolio that has grown since 1990 to cover Tuscany, Umbria, Verona, Venice and Rome
. Travel + Leisure readers rated it above The Thinking Traveller in their 2023 favourites list.
- **Red Savannah** and **Italy Perfect** both 
specialise in luxury Italian villa rentals
. Italy Perfect villas 
might come with a housekeeper, laundry service, grocery shopping or an on-site wine cellar
.
- **SopranoVillas** offers 
fully-staffed villas in Italy
. It's an Italy-only option.

**Higher-end or more service-heavy**
- **Le Collectionist** focuses on 
ultra-luxe stays in glamorous destinations, from clifftop villas in Mykonos to historic Italian palazzos
.
- **Abercrombie & Kent Villas** is 
a curated offering across Tuscany, Provence, the Balearics and more
. It's a good fit if you want tailor-made touches or guided experiences.
- **Onefinestay** is a hotel-style service with a concierge. 
It came third in the Villa Rental category of Condé Nast Traveller's 2025 Readers' Choice Awards.


**Things to consider**
- The Thinking Traveller's strength is its 
local manager who answers questions and organises excursions
. Ask any alternative what on-the-ground support it provides.
- Exclusive properties are a big part of the appeal. Oliver's Travels and others may list villas that are also on other platforms, so you can sometimes compare prices.
- Some reviewers of The Thinking Traveller mention add-on extras that cost more than local rates. Ask for a clear breakdown of extras wherever you book.

### Core specialist electric motorcycle brands

- **Zero Motorcycles (USA)**  
  - Focus: Street and dual‑sport electric motorcycles only.  
  - Reputation: Often cited as the **best all‑rounder street brand**, with models like the **Zero SR/F**, **DSR/X**, **FX/FXE** regularly topping “best of” lists.[1][2][3][4][6]  
  - Why notable: Mature product line, good balance of performance, range, and price; strong aftermarket and community support.[3][5]

- **LiveWire (Harley‑Davidson’s electric brand, USA)**  
  - Focus: Urban sport / street bikes, e.g. **LiveWire S2 Del Mar**.[2][3][4]  
  - Reputation: Frequently listed as **best overall / best tech lovers** electric motorcycle for its design and connected features.[2][3][4]  
  - Caveat: Some reviewers criticize earlier LiveWire models as **overpriced for the performance**.[6]

- **Energica (Italy)**  
  - Focus: High‑end sport‑touring and performance electric bikes.  
  - Key model: **Energica Experia**, often ranked as **overall best electric motorcycle** and “best range” (up to ~420 km / 261 miles city range).[3][4][6]  
  - Why notable: Long‑range capabilities, premium build, strong touring focus.[3][4][6]

- **Cake (Sweden)**  
  - Focus: Lightweight, minimalist **off‑road and urban** electric bikes (Kalk, Osa series).  
  - Reputation: Frequently named **best value**, **most fun**, or **best varied‑terrain** options, especially the **Kalk** and **Osa Flex**.[4][6]  
  - Why notable: Design‑driven, playful, very light bikes suited to mixed on/off‑road use.[4][6]

- **Stark (Spain/Sweden)**  
  - Focus: High‑performance **electric motocross**.  
  - Key bike: **Stark VARG**, often cited as **top performance off‑road** and “most fun,” and ranked among the best electric bikes tested.[1][3][6]  
  - Why notable: Race‑level power, radical MX focus; limited availability.[3][6]

- **Sur‑Ron (China)**  
  - Focus: Affordable **trail / light off‑road** electric bikes and “e‑moto” hybrids.  
  - Reputation: Called out as a key **established off‑road brand**.[3]  
  - Why notable: Very popular with hobby trail riders and modifiers; strong price‑to‑fun ratio.[3]

### Major motorcycle/OEM brands with strong electric offerings

- **Honda (Japan)**  
  - Example: **Honda WN7**, praised as **best for new street riders** thanks to approachable power and Honda’s dealer network.[1][3]  
  - Why notable: Global service network, brand trust; good entry‑level platform.[3]

- **Can‑Am (BRP, Canada)**  
  - Example models: **Can‑Am Pulse** and **Origin**, appearing in top‑10 lists of real‑world tested electric bikes.[1]  
  - Why notable: Re‑entering two‑wheel space with electric street and dual‑sport focus.[1]

- **BMW (Germany)**  
  - Example: **BMW CE 04** electric scooter, often ranked **best premium** urban electric two‑wheeler.[4]  
  - Why notable: Strong design, high quality, aimed at urban commuters.[4]

- **Harley‑Davidson**  
  - Parent company behind **LiveWire**; the original Harley‑branded LiveWire is still referenced but typically considered less competitive on value than newer models.[5][6]

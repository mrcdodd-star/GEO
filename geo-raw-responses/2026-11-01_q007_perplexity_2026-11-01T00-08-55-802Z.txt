--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
The easiest route is to use a UK-based Italy villa specialist and ask for a **staffed villa**, not just a villa with a chef available on request. For example, Tuscany Now & More is London-based and offers chef and concierge options; CV Villas has a UK phone line and villas with cook service across Italy.[2][8]

When you enquire, give them your dates, guest count, preferred region and budget, then confirm these details **in writing** before paying:

- Is the chef included every day? Which meals and how many hours?
- Is daily housekeeping included, and what are the hours and days off?
- Are groceries, chef fees, service charges and taxes extra?
- Can they accommodate dietary needs, children or accessibility requirements?
- What are the deposit, cancellation and payment terms?

Staffing varies significantly between villas: some provide daily housekeeping and a chef for only certain meals or days, and food costs are often separate.[1][3] Ask for a written quote that itemises the rental, staff schedule, food and any optional services.

If your dates are in peak summer, start early: one agency recommends booking nine to twelve months ahead for June–September.[3]

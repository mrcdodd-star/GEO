Finding a private villa for **12 guests in the Algarve during August** requires advance planning, as August is peak summer season and large-capacity properties (typically requiring 6+ bedrooms) sell out quickly.

---

### 1. Key Regions in the Algarve for Large Groups

| Region | Vibe & Ideal For | Highlights |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Golden Triangle** *(Quinta do Lago, Vale do Lobo, Almancil)* | **Luxury & Exclusive** | Ultra-luxury 6- to 8-bedroom villas, manicured golf courses, high-end beach clubs, ~20 mins from Faro Airport. |
| **Vilamoura** | **Golf & Marina Life** | Large modern villas, active nightlife, upscale marina, proximity to watersports and dining. |
| **Lagos & Western Algarve** | **Scenery & History** | Historic town, stunning rock formations (Ponta da Piedade), wide sandy beaches like Meia Praia. Great mix of culture and coast. |
| **Carvoeiro & Ferragudo** | **Coastal Charm** | Dramatic cliff views, family-friendly atmosphere, relaxed seaside dining, secluded headland villas. |
| **Albufeira (Galé / Olhos de Água)** | **Nightlife & Beaches** | Central location, close to major beaches, nightlife, and waterparks. Highly accessible for mixed-age groups. |

---

### 2. Crucial Features to Look For (12-Guest Requirements)

* **Bedroom Configuration:** Look for **6 bedrooms** (or 5 bedrooms with flexible twin/bunk setups). 
* **En-suite Bathrooms:** Aim for at least 4–5 bathrooms to avoid morning bottlenecks.
* **Air Conditioning:** **Essential in August**, when temperatures frequently exceed 30°C–35°C (86°F–95°F). Confirm A/C is in *all* bedrooms, not just the living room.
* **Outdoor Space:** Ensure the villa has shaded outdoor dining (pergola/gazebo) large enough to seat 12 people comfortably, along with enough sun loungers around the pool.
* **Parking:** Space for at least 2 minivans or 3–4 cars, as rental cars or group vans are generally necessary for exploring the Algarve.

### 4. Important August Booking Considerations

1. **Saturday-to-Saturday Rules:** In August, almost all large Algarve villas operate on strict 7-night minimum stays running **Saturday to Saturday** (or occasionally Sunday to Sunday).
2. **Pricing Expectations:** August commands peak rates. High-end 6-bedroom villas generally range between **€6,000 and €25,000+ per week**, depending on the level of luxury, proximity to the beach, and amenities.
3. **Airport Transfers & Car Hire:** Book cars/minivans early. Faro Airport (FAO) is very busy in August; pre-booking transfers or vehicle rentals for a party of 12 is critical.
4. **Additional Services:** Many large villas offer optional private chefs, daily housekeeping, or pool heating (though pool heating is rarely necessary in peak August).

**Things to check before booking**
- **What's included:** Concierge is often free, but the chef, transfers and activities are usually charged on top. Villas with staff included are a separate category from villas where a chef is available as an extra. WIMCO, for example, 
separates villas with chef services included from those where chef services are available.

- **ATOL and ABTA:** If the company bundles flights, ask about ATOL protection. Ask about ABTA membership too.
- **Food costs:** Ask whether groceries are included in the chef's price or billed separately.

### Headline differences

- **Location & setting**
  - **The Dorchester**: On **Park Lane**, directly overlooking Hyde Park; classic Park Lane landmark with green views and an easy walk to Buckingham Palace and central sights.[1][12][17]
  - **Claridge’s**: On **Brook Street in the heart of Mayfair**, closer to Bond Street/Oxford Street shopping and a more urban, townhouse‑style setting.[1][12][15][17]

- **Atmosphere & design**
  - **Claridge’s**: Famous for **original Art Deco interiors**, glamorous lobby, “grand occasion” feel and strong association with royalty and celebrities.[1][15][17]
  - **The Dorchester**: A **grande dame** with opulent, more traditional decor; some rooms are more classic, others more contemporary.[1][10][17]
  - Travel writers often lean toward **Claridge’s overall**, calling it the more distinctive and stylish choice.[2][17]

- **Dining & bars**
  - **The Dorchester**
    - **Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester** – **3 Michelin stars**, one of London’s top fine‑dining restaurants.[1][15][17]
    - **China Tang** – highly regarded Chinese restaurant.[1]
    - Prominent bar scene (e.g. **Vesper Bar**) and a famed Promenade for tea and drinks.[1][17]
    - Best pick if **top‑tier dining and a serious food program** are priorities.[1][17]
  - **Claridge’s**
    - Dining is good but **less Michelin‑focused**; the signature experience is **afternoon tea in The Foyer** and the bar (e.g. Dante at Claridge’s).[1]
    - If your priority is **fine dining**, The Dorchester has the clear edge.[1][17]

- **Afternoon tea**
  - Both are iconic for tea.
  - Claridge’s is often mentioned as a classic “occasion” tea, with live pianist and strong tradition.[1][17][20]
  - The Dorchester’s tea on **The Promenade** has won **UK Tea Council awards** and has been rated above Claridge’s in some rankings.[7][17]
  - For **pure tea quality**, some awards tilt toward **The Dorchester**; for **atmosphere and glamour**, many people prefer **Claridge’s**.[1][3][7]

- **Spa & pool**
  - **Claridge’s**: Has a **subterranean spa with a pool**; good if a proper on‑site pool matters to you.[1][5][15]
  - **The Dorchester**: **No on‑site pool**; has a refreshed spa with nine treatment rooms and steam room. Guests can use the pool at 45 Park Lane across the street.[1][5]
  - If you want **spa + pool in the same building**, Claridge’s is better.[1][5]

- **Clientele & vibe**
  - Commentary from frequent travellers suggests:

- **Choose Claridge’s if**:
  - You want **Art Deco style**, a glamorous lobby and that *“this is the event”* feeling.[1][15][17]
  - A **spa with pool** on site matters.[1][5]
  - You care about being in **central Mayfair** for shopping and restaurants, more than having park views.[12][15]
  - You prefer a slightly **younger, buzzy luxury vibe**.[14][17]

- **Choose The Dorchester if**:

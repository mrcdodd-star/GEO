For a UK-based multigenerational family, the **Algarve** is a strong all-round choice: it combines beaches and activities for different ages with villas and relatively short transfers from the airport.[2][10] Other good options depend on your group’s pace and priorities:

- **Mallorca, Spain — easiest all-round beach break:** Sandy beaches, calm-water spots, boat trips and a broad choice of places to stay make it flexible for mixed ages.[9]
- **Crete, Greece — beach plus culture:** Pair relaxed beach days with historic sites and family attractions; its size also means you can choose a base that suits your pace.[9]
- **Costa del Sol, Spain — convenient variety:** Resorts, beaches and attractions make it easier for different generations to split up and find things they enjoy.[3]
- **South Tyrol, Italy — cooler-weather alternative:** A good pick for mountain scenery and outdoor activities; it also features among family-friendly European destinations for 2026.[2]
- **Albania — potentially more budget-friendly:** A newer option to consider if keeping costs down matters; check travel logistics and accommodation carefully for your group.[2]
- **Andalucía, Spain — culture and activities:** Offers a mix of historic sights, outdoor pursuits and relaxed rural stays, so the family can vary the pace.[6]

For a larger group, look for a villa or apartment with step-free access, bedrooms on the ground floor, multiple bathrooms, shade and a pool with a shallow area. The Algarve is particularly suitable if you want a villa-based holiday and easy access to local facilities.[10]

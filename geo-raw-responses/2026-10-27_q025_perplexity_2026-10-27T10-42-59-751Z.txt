For a large family, start with **Oliver’s Travels** for characterful European villas, estates, and chateaux, or **The Thinking Traveller** for a more curated, high-service stay—especially in Italy and the Mediterranean. Both are recognized in 2026 villa-rental rankings, and Oliver’s specifically highlights homes for multigenerational groups and group getaways.[3][4][10]

- **Oliver’s Travels** — Best all-around first stop for larger groups wanting a distinctive European home; its concierge can help arrange services such as private chefs, childcare, and housekeeping.[6][10]
- **The Thinking Traveller** — Best for a highly curated Mediterranean villa and service-focused booking; its collection includes spacious villas suited to family reunions.[3][4]
- **Red Savannah** — Worth comparing for luxury villas with ample space and personalized service for families and groups.[1][3]
- **Vrbo** — A useful option if you want a broader range of prices and guest reviews; filter for an entire home, bedroom count, and family-friendly features.[6]
- **Interhome** — Consider it for a wider selection of European holiday homes, particularly if you’re looking beyond luxury villas or considering a Swiss chalet.[6]

**Practical tip:** Compare the total cost—not just the nightly rate—and confirm bedroom and bathroom counts, air-conditioning, pool safety, accessibility, cancellation terms, and whether cleaning, local taxes, and deposits are included. For a big family, ask the company to confirm the sleeping arrangements and suitability for children in writing before paying.

**Yes, Oliver’s Travels is a legitimate, reputable, and reliable company**. Headquartered in London, they have been operating for over 20 years (originally launched as Simply Chateau) and specialize in luxury villas, country homes, and chateaux across Europe and the Caribbean.

### 1. Reputation & Customer Feedback
* **Trustpilot Rating:** They maintain a high score of **4.5 out of 5 stars** ("Excellent") across more than 3,600 customer reviews. 
* **Positive Feedback:** Customers frequently praise the ease of booking, helpful concierge services, prompt pre-departure communication, and the unique, high-quality standard of the properties.
* **Common Complaints:** The most common negative reviews stem from on-site property issues (e.g., a pool heater not working, air conditioning failures, or slow responses from local keyholders). Because Oliver's Travels is an agency, day-to-day property maintenance during your stay is usually managed by the property owner or a local caretaker.

---

### 2. Financial Protection & Legal Info (UK Focus)
* **ATOL Protection:** Because Oliver’s Travels sells **accommodation-only** rentals (rather than package holidays that include flights), ATOL protection does not apply.
* **ABTA:** Most bookings are non-package rentals where Oliver's Travels acts as a booking agent on behalf of individual property owners. 

**How to protect your booking:**
1. **Pay with a UK Credit Card:** Under **Section 75 of the Consumer Credit Act**, paying all or part of your deposit/balance (between £100 and £30,000) on a UK credit card gives you legal financial protection if the company defaults or if there is a major breach of contract.
2. **Get Comprehensive Travel Insurance:** Ensure you take out travel insurance as soon as you book to cover unexpected cancellations, medical emergencies, or supplier failures.

---

### 3. Key Things to Keep in Mind Before Booking
* **Check the Specific House Terms:** Each property listed on Oliver’s Travels is privately owned, meaning cancellation terms, security deposits, pool heating fees, and end-of-stay cleaning charges can vary from property to property. Always check the specific "House Rules" on the listing page.
* **Security Deposits:** Security deposits are usually held as a credit card pre-authorisation or collected by the owner locally. Make sure to take photos/videos of the property upon arrival and departure.
* **Report Issues Immediately:** If anything is broken or not as advertised when you arrive, report it to **both** the local property manager and Oliver’s Travels guest relations team straight away so there is an official paper trail.

### Verdict
Oliver's Travels is a trusted option for high-end villa rentals. Paying by credit card and reviewing the property-specific terms will ensure a smooth booking process.

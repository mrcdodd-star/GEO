When looking to rent a **large party house for 20 guests**, the approach depends on whether you are planning a relaxed family gathering, a milestone birthday, a hen/stag weekend, or a lively celebration. 

Because standard rental platforms (like Airbnb) enforce strict party bans, booking through **specialist group accommodation sites** or directly contacting hosts is usually the best approach.

---

### 1. Best Platforms to Find Large Party Houses (20+ Guests)

#### **Specialist Group & Party Rental Sites**
* **[GroupAccommodation.com](https://www.groupaccommodation.com):** Excellent for large properties across Europe and the UK that cater specifically to groups of 20 to 50+ guests.
* **[Party Houses UK](https://partyhouses.co.uk):** Specializes in properties designed for events, birthdays, and celebrations equipped with bars, hot tubs, and large dining spaces.
* **[Kate & Tom's](https://kateandtoms.com):** Focuses on luxury countryside manors, estates, and houses designed for large celebrations.
* **[Coolstays](https://www.coolstays.com):** Features unique large party venues, converted barns, mansions, and historic houses built for big groups.
* **[Oliver’s Travels](https://www.oliverstravels.com) / [The Thinking Traveller](https://www.thethinkingtraveller.com):** Premium choice for luxury group villas in Europe (Spain, Italy, France, Greece).

#### **Mainstream Platforms (with caveats)**
* **Vrbo:** Allows filtering for properties sleeping 20+ guests. Many rural property owners permit events if arranged beforehand.
* **Airbnb:** Great for large houses, but **note:** Airbnb has a strict global party policy. If you book via Airbnb, ensure the gathering fits within standard house rules, or message the host before booking to confirm event permissions.

---

### 2. Essential Features to Look For (for 20 Guests)

* **Bedrooms & Bathrooms:** Aim for at least **8 to 10 bedrooms** and **6+ bathrooms** so everyone has space and privacy.
* **Dining Capacity:** Check that the dining room table seats **20 people in one space**.
* **Entertainment & Amenities:** Look for homes with hot tubs, heated pools, games rooms (pool/ping pong), cinema rooms, and dedicated outdoor seating/BBQ areas.
* **Secluded/Rural Location:** If your party involves music or late-night social activity, look for detached, rural estates with no immediate neighbours to avoid noise complaints.

---

### 3. Key Tips for Booking

1. **Be Transparent About the Event:** Always inform the host in advance if it’s a birthday, hen/stag party, or wedding celebration. Hosts appreciate honesty, and unapproved parties can lead to lost security deposits or mid-stay evictions.
2. **Check Noise Curfews:** Many properties allow loud celebrations inside, but require outdoor quiet hours after 10:00 PM / 11:00 PM.
3. **Security Deposits:** Large group properties usually require a refundable security deposit to cover potential damage. 
4. **Book Early:** Large houses accommodating 20+ guests with high-end amenities often book out **6 to 12 months in advance**, especially for summer weekends and holidays.

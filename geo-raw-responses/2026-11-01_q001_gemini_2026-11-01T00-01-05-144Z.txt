--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
Planning a holiday for 16 people spanning three generations—especially with three toddlers—requires balancing a few crucial factors: **short travel times, easy logistics, gentle/shallow beaches, flat walking areas for buggies and grandparents, and suitable accommodation.**

When traveling with this age mix, standard hotels can feel disjointed. **Renting a large private villa (or a cluster of gîtes/apartments on one estate)** is almost always the best option, giving the group communal spaces, kitchen facilities for toddler meals, and flexibility for early bedtimes.

Here are the top European destinations that suit this exact group dynamic best from the UK:

---

### 1. Menorca, Spain
* **Flight time:** ~2.5 hours from most UK airports.
* **Why it’s ideal:** Menorca is widely regarded as the ultimate family-friendly Balearic island. It’s quiet, flat, extremely safe, and has a much slower pace than Mallorca or Ibiza.
* **For Toddlers:** Clear, calm, crystal-clear bays with exceptionally shallow, warm water that shelves very gently (e.g., **Son Bou**, **Cala Galdana**, or **Punta Prima**).
* **For Grandparents:** Gentle walking paths, authentic harbour towns like Ciutadella and Mahón for relaxed lunches, and low-key evenings.
* **Accommodation:** Plenty of large rustic *fincas* and modern villas spread across the island, many within a 20-minute drive of the airport.

---

### 2. Northern Mallorca (Puerto Pollença / Alcúdia)
* **Flight time:** ~2.5 hours from the UK.
* **Why it’s ideal:** Mallorca has the best infrastructure and flight availability from regional UK airports, making travel painless. The northern coast around Puerto Pollença and Alcúdia is tailor-made for multi-generational trips.
* **For Toddlers:** Puerto Pollença has a horseshoe bay with very shallow water and a wide, pine-shaded, car-free promenade ("Pine Walk") that’s perfect for buggy walks.
* **For Grandparents:** Vibrant dining scene, beautiful mountain backdrops without needing to hike them, easy accessibility, and good local amenities.
* **Accommodation:** Huge selection of 7–9 bedroom villas with private pools, often with large grounds.

---

### 3. Central Algarve, Portugal (e.g., Praia da Luz, Carvoeiro, or Quinta do Lago)
* **Flight time:** ~2.5–3 hours to Faro.
* **Why it’s ideal:** Portugal is incredibly welcoming to children and older travellers alike. The central/western Algarve offers stunning coastlines, short airport transfers, and reliable summer weather with a nice Atlantic breeze (which stops it from feeling oppressively hot).
* **For Toddlers:** Sheltered cove beaches (like Carvoeiro or Praia da Luz) protect against larger waves, and many beachside towns have soft sand with calm spots.
* **For Grandparents:** Excellent seafood, flat boardwalks (like Quinta do Lago / Vale do Lobo), golf courses, and lovely coastal boat tours.
* **Accommodation:** Some of Europe’s best villa resorts with high-standard amenities, gated grounds, and concierge services.

---

### 4. Dordogne or Southwest France (Drive or Fly)
* **Travel:** Fly to Bergerac/Bordeaux/Toulouse (~1.5–2 hours) OR drive via Eurotunnel/Ferry with an overnight stop.
* **Why it’s ideal:** If the idea of taking three toddlers through airport security sounds daunting, driving from the UK in two cars/minibuses gives you unlimited luggage space for pushchairs, travel cots, and toddler gear.
* **For Toddlers:** Grassy grounds to run around in, river beaches with gentle water (like canoeing spots along the Dordogne), and minimal noise.
* **For Grandparents:** World-class food and wine, historic châteaux, bustling morning markets, and stunning countryside scenery.
* **Accommodation:** You can hire an entire small château or a complex of gîtes with shared gardens and a private pool.

---

### Key Tips for Booking this Trip

1. **Prioritise a Gated/Fenced Pool:** 
   * If renting a villa, **this is non-negotiable with three toddlers**. Having 13 adults around often creates a false sense of security where everyone assumes someone else is watching the pool. A lockable pool fence provides complete peace of mind.
2. **Consider Airport Transfers vs. Car Hire:** 
   * Hiring 3 to 4 separate cars at the airport with 16 people and child seats can take hours at the rental desk. Consider booking a private minibus transfer for the whole group to the villa, and hiring just one small car locally for grocery runs.
3. **Pacing and Timing:**
   * If you aren’t bound by school holidays, aim for **June or September** for lower temperatures and quieter beaches. If tied to July/August (school holidays), ensure the accommodation has air conditioning in all bedrooms—crucial for toddler day-naps and grandparents sleeping comfortably.
4. **Layout Matters:**
   * Look for a property with at least one or two ground-floor bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms for the grandparents, avoiding steep stairs where possible.

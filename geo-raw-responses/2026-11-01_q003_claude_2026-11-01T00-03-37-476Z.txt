For 20 people, I'd book one big villa or a small boutique hotel rather than separate rooms. Here are some options for a UK group.

## Best all-rounders (short flights, big villas)
- **Mallorca or Catalonia, Spain.** 
Mallorca and the Catalan coast are among one travel writer's first choices for a villa holiday.
 Mallorca has lots of estates that sleep 16–20+. Flights are short and frequent from most UK airports.
- **Tuscany, Italy.** This is good for a food-and-wine celebration. 
One example is a private chef cooking a long Friday dinner, a wine tasting on Saturday, and a pool day before you leave.
 Large farmhouses with several wings are common.
- **Andalucía, Spain.** 
It offers year-round sun, good food and beautiful landscapes, and it's popular for 50th gatherings.
 It works well if you're going outside summer.
- **Corfu or Paxos, Greece.** 
There are some gorgeous villas in the quiet north-east of Corfu.
 It suits a more relaxed, scenic trip.
- **Provence, the Côte d'Azur or the Dordogne, France.** 
Rented villas with private pools are a popular choice for friends and family.


## If you want something more special
- **Bali, Phuket or Koh Samui.** 
Villas there can host a group in one place, with private chefs, pool parties and concierge-arranged experiences.
 The flights are long and expensive, so this suits a group with more time and budget.
- **Caribbean.** 
A fully staffed luxury villa with a private pool is a good way to host your own 50th party.

- **Yacht charter in Greece or Croatia.** 
Chartering a yacht is a great way to celebrate a milestone birthday.
 Most boats only sleep 8–12, so for 20 you'd probably need a small flotilla.
- **Ski chalet.** If the birthday falls in winter, 
a chalet gives you the benefits of a holiday cottage, plus snow, log fires and staff to cook and bring the bubbles.


## Practical tips for 20 people
- **Check the sleeping capacity.** Few villas sleep 20 comfortably. Two or three villas on the same estate or street can work better, or a villa plus a nearby hotel.
- **Pick the season to suit the group.** May, June and September are cooler and cheaper than July and August. 
The Mediterranean in May has long sunny days, low prices and uncrowded resorts.

- **Look at the full cost.** 
That means the villa price, flights, transfers, catering and any extra services.
 Splitting the cost evenly and agreeing a budget early avoids awkwardness.
- **Consider a specialist agent.** For big groups, the saved time and stress is often worth it. Companies like Tuscany Now & More (Italy) and Trusted Villas are options.
- **Use a shared way of paying.** Use a group chat and a shared payment pot, and agree a cancellation policy at the start.

Plum Guide and specialist villa agencies do different jobs, so the better choice depends on the kind of trip.

## Plum Guide
- **Curation:** 
It uses AI screening, expert review and an in-person Home Critic visit, and rejects roughly 95% of the properties it considers.
 Reviewers generally find it 
higher quality on average than Airbnb or Vrbo.

- **Reliability:** Guests often say listings match the photos and descriptions. 
Trustpilot reviews are generally positive, at 4.6 stars.

- **Price:** One comparison puts it at 
roughly 10–25% above Airbnb.

- **Coverage:** 
It covers the UK, Europe, the US and a growing list of international destinations,
 but 
it doesn't list properties in every location
 because it is so selective.
- **Best for:** UK staycations, city breaks, long weekends and families or groups who want a good self-catering home without paying for staff.

## Specialist villa agencies
Examples are Oliver's Travels, Villa Plus, Think Villas, Simpson Travel and Powder Byrne. They are best for destination-heavy holidays, such as a villa in Tuscany, Provence, Mallorca or the Greek islands.
- **Service:** 
If you want a chef, daily housekeeping and a property manager who meets you on arrival, you need a fully managed villa company, not Plum Guide.
 The same comparison says these 
run 2–4x the cost of an equivalent Plum Guide property.
 Treat that multiple as a rough guide, because it comes from one source and varies a lot by agency.
- **Large properties:** 
The very largest villas, with 10 bedrooms, multi-acre estates and staff houses, tend to be sold through specialist agencies
 rather than curated marketplaces.
- **Local knowledge:** Agencies usually have people on the ground, such as a local rep, a key-holder and a pool or maintenance team. They can also arrange extras like transfers, car hire, flights and private chefs.
- **UK protection (check this yourself):** If an agency sells villa and flights together, the booking may count as a package. It would then usually carry ATOL or ABTA protection and package travel rights. A standalone accommodation booking on a marketplace generally doesn't get this. Confirm the exact protection before you pay.

## Quick guide
| If you want... | Choose |
|---|---|
| UK or city stay with consistent quality | Plum Guide |
| Villa abroad with pool, staff or concierge | Specialist agency |
| Villa plus flights with package protection | Specialist agency |
| Very large group or estate | Specialist agency |
| Lowest price | Neither; compare Airbnb or Vrbo, and vet carefully |

## Practical tips
1. Compare the same villa across both channels. Many agencies and marketplaces list the same owner's property at different prices.
2. Check the cancellation terms, deposit schedule and what happens if the host cancels. An old Tripadvisor thread has a guest whose 
booking was cancelled by the host two days before arrival.
 That story is from 2018, so don't treat it as typical.
3. Look at the extras: cleaning, pool heating, damage deposits and tourist tax.

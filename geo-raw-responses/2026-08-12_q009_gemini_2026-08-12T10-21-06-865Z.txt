--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
While **The Dorchester** and **45 Park Lane** are sister properties under the elite Dorchester Collection, located directly across the street from one another in London’s ultra-exclusive Mayfair district, they offer vastly different interpretations of five-star luxury. 

If **The Dorchester** is the grand, historic matriarch of British high society, **45 Park Lane** is her sleek, modern, art-loving younger sibling. 

---

### 1. Atmosphere, Style, and Design (The Core Difference)
* **The Dorchester:** Known as London's "Grand Dame", this iconic hotel originally opened in 1931. It represents historic, opulent, and grand British luxury. While it underwent a massive, multi-million-pound renovation (completed in 2023 by Pierre-Yves Rochon), it retains its majestic, old-school glamour. The lobby features soaring, gold-lacquered Corinthian columns, vibrant floral carpets, massive fresh flower arrangements, and a bustling, high-society atmosphere.
* **45 Park Lane:** Opened in 2011, this hotel has a distinctly contemporary, masculine, and intimate aesthetic. Designed by renowned French architect Thierry Despont, the interiors feature rich warm woods, dark leather, clean lines, and a sleek Art Deco-inspired style. It is home to a rotating gallery of bold, contemporary British art (featuring pieces by artists like Damien Hirst). The vibe is highly relaxed and exclusive, closely resembling a chic, private gentlemen's club rather than a bustling hotel lobby.

### 2. Size and Scale
* **The Dorchester:** A large-scale luxury hotel featuring **241 rooms and suites**. It is almost constantly humming with international travelers, locals, and dignitaries.
* **45 Park Lane:** An ultra-boutique, highly exclusive property with just **45 rooms and suites** (plus one massive penthouse suite). Service here is deeply personalized and discreet. 

### 3. Rooms and Views
* **The Dorchester:** The newly designed rooms are styled to evoke a high-end "English garden," using soft pastel tones, floral prints, and custom-made fabrics. The bathrooms are legendary—lined in classic Italian marble with some of the deepest soaking tubs in London. Views vary; some overlook Hyde Park, while others look out over Mayfair or the hotel's interior courtyards.
* **45 Park Lane:** The rooms are modern, high-tech (outfitted with Bang & Olufsen sound systems), and feature leather accents and geometric Art Deco lines. Crucially, because of the building's narrow, linear design, **every single one of the 45 rooms and suites has a direct, panoramic view of Hyde Park**. 

### 4. Dining and Drinking
Both hotels are culinary powerhouses, but they offer very different dining experiences:
* **The Dorchester:**
  * **Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester:** A world-renowned, 3-Michelin-starred temple of contemporary French haute cuisine.
  * **The Grill by Tom Booton:** A modern, playful, and highly acclaimed take on classic British dining.
  * **The Promenade:** Famous for hosting one of London's most iconic, traditional afternoon teas accompanied by live piano music.
  * **Vesper Bar & Artists' Bar:** Highly glamorous spots for classic, theater-inspired cocktails.
  * **China Tang:** A lavish, subterranean hotspot serving upscale Cantonese cuisine.
* **45 Park Lane:**
  * **CUT at 45 Park Lane:** Wolfgang Puck's only European restaurant—a vibrant, high-end, American-style steakhouse.
  * **Sushi Kanesaka:** An incredibly exclusive, Michelin-starred, 9-to-13-seat traditional Japanese *omakase* experience. 
  * **BAR45:** A stylish mezzanine bar famous for its sophisticated, local crowd and its signature Negronis.

### 5. Spa, Wellness, and Swimming Pools
* **The Dorchester:** Features a beautiful, classic subterranean spa (The Dorchester Spa) offering top-tier treatments, including facials by La Prairie. However, **The Dorchester does not have a swimming pool**. 
* **45 Park Lane:** Features a spectacular, ultra-modern 10,000 sq. ft. wellness space (The Spa at 45 Park Lane). It boasts a state-of-the-art gym, sauna, steam room, and **a stunning 20-meter heated indoor pool**—the longest swimming pool on Park Lane. 
* *Note on cross-access:* Because they are sister properties, guests staying at The Dorchester have full access to cross over and use the 20-meter swimming pool and fitness facilities at 45 Park Lane.

---

### Which one should you choose?

* **Choose The Dorchester if:** You want a grand, classic "London" experience. It is perfect if you love historic pageantry, buzzing hotel lobbies with great people-watching, traditional afternoon tea, and having access to a 3-Michelin-star French restaurant under the same roof. 
* **Choose 45 Park Lane if:** You prefer a modern, contemporary, and intimate aesthetic. It is ideal for travelers who want the ultimate in privacy, guaranteed Hyde Park views, a "private club" atmosphere, and exceptional modern dining like high-end steak or elite Japanese sushi.

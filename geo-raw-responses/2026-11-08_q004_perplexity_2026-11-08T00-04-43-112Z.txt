For a **week-long family reunion somewhere warm**, I’d shortlist **Mallorca, the Algarve, Andalusia, or Crete**. They’re good options for a mixed-age group: you can share a villa or large rental, enjoy time together by the pool or beach, and still find outings for different interests.

- **Mallorca, Spain — easiest all-rounder:** Look for a large villa near a town, so the group can split between pool days, beaches, and meals out. It’s also possible to combine Mallorca with Menorca for an eight-day island-hopping trip.[1]
- **Algarve, Portugal — relaxed beach-and-villa week:** A strong pick if you want a spacious rental, beach time, and easygoing coastal towns. The eastern Algarve around Tavira is noted for shallower, warmer water; a pool is handy if ocean swimming is a priority.[7]
- **Andalusia, Spain — culture plus coast:** Base near Málaga or Marbella for seaside downtime, with day trips for historic sights, white villages, or a flamenco evening. It’s a varied option for a group with different tastes.[10]
- **Crete, Greece — island variety:** Choose a base near a larger town or family-friendly bay, then keep most days local and plan one or two excursions. Larger Greek islands such as Crete offer more family-holiday infrastructure than smaller islands.[7]
- **Tuscany, Italy — best for a villa gathering:** A countryside villa with a pool gives everyone a shared home base, with hill towns and Florence for day trips. It’s less of a beach-focused choice, but can make a memorable reunion.[10]

For a reunion, I’d prioritise **one roomy property, a pool, air conditioning, and a short transfer from the airport** over trying to tour several places. If dates are flexible, late August or early September may mean less intense heat and lower prices than peak August in southern Europe.[7]

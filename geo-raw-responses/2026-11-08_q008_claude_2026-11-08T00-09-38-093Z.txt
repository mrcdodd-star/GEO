**Tips**
- **Check the sleeping numbers.** Many listings show a range, such as "sleeps 10–20". Confirm that the top figure includes sofa beds or extra beds, and whether the pool and living spaces suit that many people.
- **Consider linked villas.** Two adjoining villas can be easier to find and cheaper than one very large house.
- **Look at ABTA/ATOL protection.** 
Solmar is ABTA & ATOL protected
, but its villas only go up to about 20 guests. Protection varies between agencies, so ask about it when you book.
- **Book early.** Large villas for peak summer weeks go quickly.

## ✅ Tips for Booking Smart

1. **Clarify exactly what “staffed” means.** Is it a chef all meals? Just housekeeping? How many staff?  
2. **Check what’s included vs extra fees.** Think food, transport, boat trips, beach clubs, alcohol.  
3. **Fly-in options.** Some operators bundle flights from the UK; others expect you to book separately.  
4. **Travel protection.** Make sure the agency is ATOL and/or ABTA protected—important for peace of mind.  
5. **Book early** for top villas, especially for seasonal spots like Santorini or Mykonos.

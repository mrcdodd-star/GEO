**Sometimes—but not automatically.** Paying extra to a specialist is most worthwhile when the villa is central to an important trip and you want someone to vet the property, arrange practical details, and help if something goes wrong. For a straightforward stay, Airbnb may be better value if you’re comfortable checking the listing and dealing mainly with the host. Specialist agencies often offer more curation and booking support, while marketplace quality and support can vary by host.[4]

## When the premium may be worth it

- **Big group, family holiday or celebration:** Coordinating bedrooms, child-friendly features, arrivals, transport or services is harder to improvise. Specialist support can reduce that hassle.[4]
- **You want more confidence in what you’re booking:** Some specialists describe their properties as handpicked or inspected, though standards differ by company—check exactly what “vetted” means.[2][5]
- **You’re booking a package:** UK-based operators such as Simpson Travel offer package holidays with financial protection; compare the protection and terms for the specific booking rather than assuming every villa-only booking has the same cover.[2]

## When Airbnb may be enough

Choose Airbnb if the trip is simple, you’re happy to assess the host and reviews yourself, and the total price is meaningfully lower. Which?’s comparison found Airbnb had a 77% customer score, versus 79% for some recommended package providers; it also found package providers could cost more, so higher price alone doesn’t guarantee a better experience.[5]

Before paying, compare the **full total**—including cleaning, service charges, deposits, cancellation terms and any extras—and search the property name or images elsewhere to see whether it’s listed directly or through another agency. Confirm whether the pool is private, who handles problems locally, and what happens if the property is unavailable or materially different from its description.

**Rule of thumb:** pay the premium for dependable support and reduced uncertainty, not just the “specialist” label. If the villa is ordinary, the booking is low-stakes, and the listing checks out, Airbnb can be perfectly reasonable.

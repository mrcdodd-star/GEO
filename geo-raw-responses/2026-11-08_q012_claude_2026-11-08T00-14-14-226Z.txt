## Questions to ask before booking
1. **Cost:** find out whether the chef, transfers and activities are included or billed on top. Many companies quote the chef and transfers as extras.
2. **Protection:** ATOL only applies if flights are part of the package. 
Without flights, bookings are not ATOL protected.
 If you book villa-only, ask what financial protection you have.
3. **Staffing:** a "private chef" can mean daily meals or a few set dinners. Confirm which, and whether food costs are included.

If you tell me where you're going, how many of you there are, your budget and whether you want flights included, I can narrow this down.

The easiest route is usually a specialist villa agency rather than a general booking site. Agencies can tell you exactly what staff each villa includes.

**1. Understand the service levels**
"Staffed" covers a range of arrangements:
- 
Some villas include a chef or cook in the stay, while many include breakfast and housekeeping and add a chef on request.

- Chef hours differ. At one Positano property, for example, 
a chef is on hand eight hours a day, with housekeeping through the day and breakfast each morning.
 At another, 
a chef cooks breakfast and one other meal six days a week.

- Expect days off. 
At some villas the chef takes Wednesday off
, and housekeeping may skip Sundays.

**2. Use a specialist agency**
These agencies have listings that match your criteria:
- **Tuscany Now & More** is 
a London-based, family-run villa rental company specialising in Italian villa holidays.
 It has a dedicated 
page of villas with private chefs, housekeeping and concierge teams.

- **Haute Retreats** lists 
more than 270 luxury villas in Italy
. It has a Tuscany page with 
11 villas where the chef is part of the rent.

- **Invitation to Tuscany** has a 
catered villas section, with breakfast, chefs or daily maid service depending on the villa.

- **SopranoVillas** offers 
a private chef, butler, maid, and gardener
 at its fully staffed villas.
- **Rental Escapes** is another option. Its listings say staffing 
may include daily housekeeping, private chefs, butlers, villa managers, gardeners, and pool attendants.


I haven't compared these agencies on price or quality, so get quotes from two or three.

**3. Questions to ask before booking**
- Is the chef **included in the rent or charged extra**? Even when included, 
food is often charged at cost
, and 
the cost of ingredients and wine is not generally included in the villa rental price.

- How many hours per day does the housekeeper work, and which days? Is there a day off?
- Which meals does the chef cover? Is table service included?
- Is the staff's gratuity expected or included?
- Are there dietary or allergy limits, and can the chef cater for children?
- What are the **tourist tax, final cleaning, utilities and security deposit** charges?
- What are the payment schedule and cancellation terms? Is the agency ATOL- or otherwise financially protected? Villa-only rentals usually aren't covered by ATOL, so travel insurance matters.

**4. Practical tips**
- **Book early** for June to September. Top staffed villas often go a year ahead.
- Pick the region first. Tuscany and Umbria have the most staffed villas, followed by Lake Como, the Amalfi Coast, Puglia and Sicily.
- If your preferred villa has no chef, most agencies can **arrange a freelance private chef**. That can be cheaper and more flexible, for example for just a few evenings.
- Ask about **airport transfers and car hire**. Many villas are remote.

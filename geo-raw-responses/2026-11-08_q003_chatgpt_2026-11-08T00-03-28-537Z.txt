## What to Book Early & Challenges to Watch  

- **Accommodation**: 20 people = lots of rooms or a big villa/resort. Book early.  
- **Flights**: direct/chartered if possible. Try to group travellers so flight times are similar.  
- **Transport locally**: vans, transfers that can move all of you together.  
- **Anchor event**: plan at least one big organised thing (special dinner, private boat, etc.). Let other days be flexible.  
- **Diet / mobility / preferences**: ensure there’s something for non-party people, non-drinkers, etc.

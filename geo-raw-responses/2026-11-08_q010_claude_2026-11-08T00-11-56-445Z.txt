**Things to check when booking**
- **What "staffed" means.** It varies a lot. Some villas include a housekeeper and breakfast, and the chef is an add-on. At others, 
a chef is booked only for the meals you choose.
 Ask exactly which staff are included and for how many hours.
- **Food costs.** Groceries are usually charged separately from the chef's fee. Confirm this.
- **Booking early.** 
Booking several months ahead gives a wider selection of villas and more flexibility with chef services.
 For summer 2027, I'd start looking soon.
- **Travel arrangements.** Corfu, Crete, Mykonos and Santorini have direct UK flights in season. Smaller islands like Paros often need a connection or ferry. Ask about airport transfers.
- **Financial protection.** Booking through a UK agent that holds ATOL or ABTA protection can give you extra peace of mind.

If you tell me your group size, budget, preferred island and travel dates, I can narrow this down. For example, Corfu is easy to reach from the UK and has lots of staffed options. The Cyclades suit a more glamorous, island-hopping trip.

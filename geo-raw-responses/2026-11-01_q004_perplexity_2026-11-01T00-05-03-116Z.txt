For a week-long reunion, I’d choose **one base with a big shared villa, pool and outdoor dining area** rather than moving between places. These are strong options from the UK:

- **Algarve, Portugal** — A quinta or villa with a pool gives everyone room to gather, with beaches, boat trips and easy-going days out nearby. It’s a reliable all-rounder for different ages and activity levels.[3]
- **Mallorca, Spain** — Rent a rural finca or a cluster of nearby villas; combine pool time with beaches, villages and a day trip by boat. The Balearics are well suited to a week-long family break.[1]
- **Crete or another Greek island** — Choose a villa near a beach and a village, so the group can split up easily and come together for meals. Greece offers warm-water swimming, food, villages and history.[1]
- **Southern Corsica, France** — A good pick for scenery and beaches, with a more adventurous feel; consider a single villa base rather than trying to see the whole island.[7]
- **Croatia’s Split Riviera** — Best if the family wants more than pool-and-beach time: sea kayaking, boat trips and outdoor activities can suit older children and teens.[3]

**Best bets by reunion style:** Algarve for the easiest all-round gathering; Mallorca for a classic island week; Croatia for an active group. For spring or autumn, the Algarve and Greek islands are worth considering; if warm weather is the priority, travel later in the season, as shoulder-season conditions can vary.[9]

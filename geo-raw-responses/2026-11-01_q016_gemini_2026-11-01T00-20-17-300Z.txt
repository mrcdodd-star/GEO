--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
If you love **The Thinking Traveller** for its handpicked Mediterranean villas, exclusive access, design-led properties, and dedicated local concierge service, several top UK-friendly alternatives offer a similar high-end experience:

---

### 1. **Le Collectionist**
* **Best for:** Ultra-luxury, design-forward villas with top-tier bespoke concierge service.
* **Destinations:** French Riviera, Balearic Islands (Ibiza, Mallorca), Greek Islands, Amalfi Coast, Tuscany, French Alps, St Barts.
* **Why it’s a great alternative:** Le Collectionist is arguably the closest European competitor to The Thinking Traveller in terms of aesthetic quality and curated luxury. Each villa is vetted for exceptional architecture and design. Their in-house concierge team handles everything from private chefs and yacht charters to exclusive local experiences.

---

### 2. **CV Villas**
* **Best for:** Classic Mediterranean luxury with optional ATOL-protected package options (flights + car rental).
* **Destinations:** Corfu, Paxos, Crete, Amalfi Coast, Tuscany, Mallorca, Algarve, Caribbean.
* **Why it’s a great alternative:** A long-established British luxury villa specialist with a massive footprint across Greece and Italy. While slightly larger in scale than Thinking Traveller, many of their villas are exclusive to CV Villas ("Above & Beyond" range) and feature private infinity pools, seaside access, and dedicated local reps.

---

### 3. **Scott Williams**
* **Best for:** Off-the-beaten-track, architectural gems and discreet luxury.
* **Destinations:** Greek Islands, Puglia, Sicily, Corsica, South of France.
* **Why it’s a great alternative:** A boutique UK-based agency specializing in understated luxury and characterful homes. They focus on intimate local knowledge rather than mass listings, making them ideal if you want a secluded, high-spec villa in hidden corners of the Mediterranean.

---

### 4. **Oliver’s Travels**
* **Best for:** Characterful properties, châteaux, large group stays, and wider geographic choice.
* **Destinations:** France, Italy, Spain, Greece, UK, Caribbean, Bali.
* **Why it’s a great alternative:** Known for handpicking properties "with character"—from historic Italian estates and French châteaux to sleek coastal villas. They offer a dedicated concierge service to arrange in-villa dining, childcare, and activities.

---

### 5. **Simpson Travel**
* **Best for:** Seamless service, boutique family-friendly villas, and lesser-known Mediterranean spots.
* **Destinations:** Greece (Paxos, Meganisi, Lefkada, Crete), Turkey, Corsica, Mallorca, Italy.
* **Why it’s a great alternative:** Highly acclaimed in the UK for customer service. While their inventory leans slightly more toward boutique family holidays rather than ultra-high-net-worth estates, their handpicked villas, adult-only options, and end-to-end logistics (including regional flights and car hire) make booking completely effortless.

---

### 6. **onefinestay**
* **Best for:** Hotel-standard servicing (24/7 guest support, 200-point property inspections) in private villas.
* **Destinations:** Southern Europe (Ibiza, South of France, Mykonos), Caribbean, Ski Resorts, Major Cities.
* **Why it’s a great alternative:** Part of the Accor Group, onefinestay bridges the gap between boutique hotel service and private villa living. Ideal if you prioritize guaranteed housekeeping standards, high-end linens, and round-the-clock support.

---

### 7. **Red Savannah / Abercrombie & Kent Villas**
* **Best for:** Tailor-made, high-end luxury estates with tailored itineraries.
* **Destinations:** Tuscany, Umbria, Amalfi, Côte d'Azur, Ibiza, Greece, Caribbean.
* **Why it’s a great alternative:** Premium UK travel operators with dedicated villa departments. Their collections feature private historic palazzos, fully staffed estates, and country manors with personal travel designers overseeing every element of the trip.

---

### Quick Recommendation Guide:

* **If you want exact design & luxury parity to Thinking Traveller:** Go with **Le Collectionist** or **Scott Williams**.
* **If you want full flight & villa package protection (ATOL):** Choose **CV Villas** or **Simpson Travel**.
* **If you are booking a large family gathering/château:** Check **Oliver's Travels**.

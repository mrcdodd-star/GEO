When booking a luxury villa in Europe from the UK, the best option depends on whether you prefer **exclusive, highly vetted portfolios**, **fully staffed estates**, or **financial package protection (ABTA/ATOL)**.

The following top luxury villa rental companies cater directly to UK travelers:

---

### 1. The Thinking Traveller
* **Best For:** Mediterranean exclusivity, boutique luxury, and unmatched local knowledge.
* **Destinations:** Sicily, Puglia, Tuscany, Corsica, Greek Islands (Ionian, Cyclades, Sporades), and Menorca.
* **Why Choose Them:** Frequently named *Best Villa Rental Company in the World* in the *Condé Nast Traveller Readers’ Choice Awards*. Every villa is exclusive to their portfolio—you won't find them listed anywhere else. They offer a 24/7 local support team (Think Experiences) to arrange private chefs, boat charters, or wine tastings.

### 2. Le Collectionist
* **Best For:** Ultra-luxury, high-design estates, French Riviera glamour, and Alpine chalets.
* **Destinations:** Côte d'Azur, Provence, Amalfi Coast, Ibiza, Majorca, Mykonos, French & Swiss Alps.
* **Why Choose Them:** Le Collectionist specializes in top-tier luxury. Every booking comes with a dedicated local concierge who can curate fully bespoke stays—arranging private jets, helicopter transfers, Michelin-starred chefs, and daily housekeeping.

### 3. CV Villas
* **Best For:** Classic UK reliability, Greece specialists, and ATOL-protected packages.
* **Destinations:** Corfu, Paxos, Crete, Amalfi Coast, Tuscany, Algarve, Mallorca, Côte d'Azur.
* **Why Choose Them:** Established over 50 years ago, CV Villas is a UK staple. They allow you to book either villa-only or full packages with flights and car hire (offering full **ABTA/ATOL** financial protection). Their "ABOVE" collection represents their highest-tier luxury properties with dedicated concierges.

### 4. Scott Dunn
* **Best For:** Fully-staffed luxury family holidays with private chefs and childcare.
* **Destinations:** France (Dordogne, Provence, Côte d'Azur), Italy, Majorca, Greece.
* **Why Choose Them:** Scott Dunn excels if you want a hotel experience in a private residence. Many of their villas come complete with private chefs, hosts, and Scott Dunn–trained nannies, making them ideal for multi-generational family trips or large groups.

### 5. Oliver’s Travels
* **Best For:** Unique, characterful luxury properties (châteaux, castles, large group rentals).
* **Destinations:** France, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Greece, Croatia, Ireland, and the UK.
* **Why Choose Them:** Known for handpicked properties that range from medieval French châteaux to contemporary beachfront villas. They have a complimentary UK-based concierge team to help organize everything from catering to airport transfers.

### 6. onefinestay
* **Best For:** Hotel-standard servicing, prime coastal estates, and urban luxury homes.
* **Destinations:** Amalfi Coast, Tuscany, French Riviera, St. Tropez, Ibiza, Paris, London.
* **Why Choose Them:** Part of the Accor hospitality group, onefinestay combines private luxury homes with 24/7 guest services, professional cleaning, and luxury toiletries. 

### 7. Abercrombie & Kent (A&K Villas)
* **Best For:** High-end tailor-made trips with seamless logistics.
* **Destinations:** Tuscany, Umbria, Provence, Côte d’Azur, Balearics, Greece.
* **Why Choose Them:** Backed by one of the world's premier luxury travel agencies, A&K inspects all properties to strict luxury standards and seamlessly coordinates flights, transfers, and private excursions.

---

### Tips for UK Renters:

1. **Financial Protection (ABTA / ATOL):** If you book flights and accommodation together through companies like **CV Villas** or **Scott Dunn**, your trip is ATOL protected under UK law. If you book villa-only via platforms like *The Thinking Traveller* or *Le Collectionist*, make sure to purchase comprehensive travel insurance covering luxury holiday rentals.
2. **Staffing Level:** Clarify whether the villa is self-catered with optional extras, or if it includes daily maid service, a full-time butler, or a resident chef.
3. **Peak Season Timing:** High-end European villas (especially in July/August across Sardinia, Ibiza, and the French Riviera) often get booked **8 to 12 months in advance**.

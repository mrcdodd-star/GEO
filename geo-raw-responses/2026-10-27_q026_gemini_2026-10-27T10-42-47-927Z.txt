### Important Tips for Americans Renting Villas in Italy

1. **Saturday-to-Saturday Stays:** Many traditional countryside villas in Italy (especially Tuscany and Umbria) only accept bookings from Saturday to Saturday during peak season (May through October).
2. **Confirm Air Conditioning:** Unlike in the US, AC is not guaranteed in historical Italian villas due to local historic preservation laws. If traveling in July or August, explicitly ask the agency if bedrooms are individually air-conditioned.
3. **Hire an Arrival-Night Chef:** On check-in day, driving from the airport and navigating Italian rental cars or grocery stores can be overwhelming. Asking the villa agency to arrange a private chef for your first evening is a cost-effective way to kick off the trip stress-free.
4. **Be Aware of Local Cash Fees:** Be prepared for local tourist taxes (*tassa di soggiorno*) or heating/electricity surcharges, which are often legally required to be paid in cash (Euros) directly to the key-holder upon check-out.
5. **Driving Constraints:** If renting in countryside regions, ensure you're comfortable with manual vs. automatic rental cars and narrow roads. If staying in historic town centers (Florence, Siena, Lucca), ask your agency about **ZTL zones** (restricted traffic zones) so you don't incur heavy traffic fines.

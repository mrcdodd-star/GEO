Suite booking rates at the iconic **Claridge’s** in Mayfair, London, vary significantly depending on the season, day of the week, and booking demand. However, average starting rates for their luxury suites generally range from **£1,500 to over £21,000+ per night**.

The approximate starting rates by suite category are detailed below:

### 1. Junior Suites & Studios (approx. 650–780 sq ft)
These open-plan suites offer plenty of space, blending Claridge's signature Art Deco elements with classic Victorian elegance.
* **Claridge’s Studio / Deluxe Junior Suite:** Starts around **£1,500 to £1,800** ($1,950 – $2,400 USD) per night.
* **Terrace Junior Suite:** Starts around **£2,000** ($2,600+ USD) per night.

### 2. One-Bedroom Suites (approx. 780–1,000 sq ft)
These suites feature a completely separate, spacious sitting room and typically include Claridge's renowned **24-hour private butler service**.
* **Mayfair Suite:** Starts around **£2,200** ($2,900 USD) per night.
* **Claridge’s Suite:** Starts around **£2,500 to £2,800** ($3,200 – $3,600 USD) per night.
* **Mayfair Terrace Suite:** Starts around **£2,800+** ($3,600+ USD) per night.

### 3. Specialty & Signature Two-Bedroom Suites (approx. 1,200–2,200 sq ft)
Individually designed by premier tastemakers, these highly prestigious residences feature double-height ceilings, hand-picked art, and expansive dining areas perfect for entertaining.
* **Two-Bedroom Mayfair Suite:** Starts around **£3,500 to £4,500** per night.
* **Brook Suite:** Starts around **£4,100** (~$5,400 USD) per night.
* **The Grand Piano Suite** (designed by Diane von Furstenberg, featuring a private bar, marble fireplace, and grand piano): Starts around **£11,000 to £14,000** (~$14,300 – $18,000 USD) per night. 
* **Georgian Suite:** Starts around **£16,000** (~$20,800 USD) per night.

### 4. Penthouses & The Royal Suite (up to 2,400 sq ft)
These represent the absolute height of luxury in London, historically hosting royal families, heads of state, and global celebrities. 
* **The Royal Suite / The Penthouse:** Typically start at **£16,000 to £21,600+** per night depending on how many connecting bedrooms are booked.

---

### Key Inclusions & Perks
When you book a suite at Claridge's, several highly bespoke amenities are typically included in your rate:
* **24-Hour Butler Service:** Available for Mayfair Suites and higher tiers. Your butler will unpack your luggage, prepare in-suite cocktails, and cater to any request.
* **Wellness Access:** Full, complimentary access to the newly renovated, subterranean Claridge’s Spa, heated swimming pool, and gym.
* **In-Suite Amenities:** Complimentary soft drinks and local treats from the mini-bar, high-end Surrenne bath products, and Dyson Supersonic hairdryers.

### Tips for Booking
* **The "Life is Suite" Package:** Claridge's periodically offers this package, which provides a **10% discount** on best available rates for suites, complimentary daily full English breakfast for two (served in your suite or the Foyer), and a chilled welcome bottle of Billecart-Salmon Champagne.
* **Taxes and Service Charges:** Rates quoted by the hotel are usually inclusive of VAT, but look out for a **5% discretionary service charge** added to the room bill at checkout.

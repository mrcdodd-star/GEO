Several UK-based specialists offer fully staffed villas in Greece. Here is what I found.

**UK agencies and operators**
- **CV Villas (London):** 
In 2019 it launched ABOVE, a luxury collection that includes fully staffed properties.
 
Many properties in the collection offer an in-villa chef for the whole stay.
 Its Corfu staffed villas include 
Kalokairi Beach House (7 bedrooms, sleeps 16, from £3,930 a week) and Upper Mantin (4 bedrooms, sleeps 8).
 
It is bonded with the CAA and holds an ATOL, so package holidays are protected.

- **The Thinking Traveller (London and Athens offices):** 
A discreet team keeps the villa spotless, and a private villa cook prepares meals in the local cuisine.
 It is a good choice if you want a chef included.
- **Iconic Villas:** It has 
a dedicated collection of fully staffed villas in Greece
, and it also arranges tailor-made holidays.
- **Group Accommodation:** This suits larger parties. For example, 
Villa Agave in Mykonos sleeps 6–12 and includes a cook, with a fully staffed experience available.


**Practical tips**
- **Book early.** 
School-holiday peak weeks in Greece typically book out 9–12 months in advance.

- **Check what "staffed" means.** It can mean a housekeeper only, a housekeeper plus a cook, or a full team with a chef, butler and concierge. Ask whether food costs, drinks and gratuities are included. Chef services are often charged separately from the villa rental.
- **Consider the island.** 
Paros is suggested as a quieter alternative to Santorini, which is becoming harder for families.
 Corfu has a large choice of staffed villas, and Mykonos, Crete and Kefalonia also have options.
- **Look for financial protection.** Choose ATOL-protected packages if you're booking flights with the villa.

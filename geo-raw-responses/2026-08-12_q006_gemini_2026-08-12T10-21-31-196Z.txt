For a luxurious stay in London combined with world-class dining, several of the city's most iconic 5-star hotels house spectacular, Michelin-starred restaurants. 

---

### **Three Michelin Stars (The Pinnacle of Fine Dining)**

#### **1. The Connaught (Mayfair)**
*   **The Restaurant:** **Hélène Darroze at The Connaught**
*   **The Experience:** Holding the ultimate three-star accolade, Chef Hélène Darroze delivers highly refined, seasonally-led French gastronomy. Each dish is built around exceptional ingredients sourced directly from independent British and French growers and farmers.
*   **The Hotel:** The Connaught is one of Mayfair’s most prestigious and historic hotels, blending classic British heritage with contemporary art, featuring outstanding personal butler service and a tranquil Aman Spa.

#### **2. The Dorchester (Mayfair)**
*   **The Restaurant:** **Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester**
*   **The Experience:** Under the vision of legendary French chef Alain Ducasse, this restaurant serves contemporary French haute cuisine characterized by intense flavors, light sauces, and meticulous presentation.
*   **The Hotel:** Situated on Park Lane, The Dorchester is a legendary London landmark. Having recently undergone a massive top-to-bottom renovation, it offers opulent, fresh interiors overlooking Hyde Park.

---

### **Two Michelin Stars (Exceptional Cuisine)**

#### **3. The Ritz London (Green Park / St. James's)**
*   **The Restaurant:** **The Ritz Restaurant**
*   **The Experience:** Under Executive Chef John Williams MBE, the restaurant holds two Michelin stars. It offers classic French-British cuisine utilizing the finest seasonal British ingredients, served in one of the most breathtaking, sparkling, fresco-ceilinged dining rooms in the world.
*   **The Hotel:** The Ritz is synonymous with high society, neoclassical elegance, and quintessential British luxury. 

#### **4. The Peninsula London (Belgravia)**
*   **The Restaurant:** **Brooklands by Claude Bosi**
*   **The Experience:** Located on the hotel’s eighth-floor rooftop, Brooklands offers a stunning sensory journey. The dining room pays homage to British aviation and motorsport (complete with a scale model of the Concorde on the ceiling). Chef Claude Bosi serves inventive, modern French-British dishes using exceptional ingredients.
*   **The Hotel:** Built overlooking Hyde Park Corner, the Peninsula is one of London's newest ultra-luxury 5-star properties, featuring sleek, expansive rooms, state-of-the-art technology, and an exceptional spa.

#### **5. Hotel Café Royal (Soho / Piccadilly)**
*   **The Restaurant:** **Alex Dilling at Hotel Café Royal**
*   **The Experience:** An intimate, highly exclusive dining room where Chef Alex Dilling showcases his exquisite, modern take on classic French gastronomy. Known for incredible technical precision, beautiful plating, and signature uses of caviar.
*   **The Hotel:** Hotel Café Royal seamlessly marries historic 19th-century architecture with bold, minimalist modern design, right where Mayfair meets Soho.

#### **6. Mandarin Oriental Hyde Park (Knightsbridge)**
*   **The Restaurant:** **Dinner by Heston Blumenthal**
*   **The Experience:** A captivating culinary journey through Britain's gastronomic history, featuring dishes inspired by recipes dating back to the 14th century (such as the famous "Meat Fruit" chicken liver parfait masquerading as a mandarin). 
    *   *Traveler’s Note:* Dinner by Heston Blumenthal is scheduled to close in **January 2027** due to the natural end of its tenancy, so be sure to book soon if you want to experience this iconic institution.
*   **The Hotel:** Located between bustling Knightsbridge and the peaceful lawns of Hyde Park, this historic hotel boasts royal heritage, stunning Edwardian architecture, and a world-class wellness spa.

---

### **One Michelin Star (High-Quality Cooking)**

#### **7. Raffles London at The OWO (Whitehall)**
*   **The Restaurant:** **Mauro Colagreco at Raffles London**
*   **The Experience:** Created by acclaimed chef Mauro Colagreco (of Mirazur fame), this restaurant focuses on "circular cooking" and the rich biodiversity of British terroir, using hyper-seasonal ingredients to construct poetic, vegetable-forward fine dining plates.
*   **The Hotel:** Housed inside the monumental Old War Office (the former office of Winston Churchill), this breathtaking 5-star hotel opened to massive acclaim, offering heritage suites, a Guerlain spa, and grand Edwardian interiors.

#### **8. Four Seasons Hotel London at Park Lane (Mayfair)**
*   **The Restaurant:** **Pavyllon London**
*   **The Experience:** Helmed by multi-Michelin-starred French master Yannick Alléno, Pavyllon offers modern French dining centered around a lively open-counter kitchen. They also serve a unique, Michelin-starred breakfast tasting menu on the weekends.
*   **The Hotel:** A highly polished, modern Mayfair classic. It features private balconies with views of Hyde Park, a glass-walled rooftop spa, and the legendary service standard of the Four Seasons brand.

#### **9. The Goring (Belgravia)**
*   **The Restaurant:** **The Dining Room**
*   **The Experience:** A wonderfully upscale but warm space designed by Russell Sage, serving impeccable, classic British cuisine with modern twists. 
*   **The Hotel:** The Goring is the only hotel with a Royal Warrant from the late Queen Elizabeth II and famously hosted Kate Middleton the night before her wedding. It offers classic English country house charm, a private garden, and exceptional luxury in Belgravia.

#### **10. The Savoy (Covent Garden)**
*   **The Restaurant:** **1890 by Gordon Ramsay**
*   **The Experience:** Located overlooking the hotel’s iconic entrance, this intimate restaurant pays tribute to legendary chef Georges Auguste Escoffier (who worked at the Savoy in 1890). It serves a refined, modern French tasting menu using classic Escoffier techniques.
*   **The Hotel:** Positioned on the Strand, The Savoy is one of London’s most famous hotels, offering Art Deco and Edwardian glamour, a stunning American Bar, and lovely views of the River Thames.

#### **11. The Stafford London (St. James's)**
*   **The Restaurant:** **Michael Caines at The Stafford**
*   **The Experience:** A relatively new addition to London’s starred lineup, bringing Chef Michael Caines' signature style of refined modern British gastronomy to a historic hotel setting.
*   **The Hotel:** Hidden away in a quiet courtyard in historic St. James's, The Stafford is a charming 5-star boutique retreat famous for its 380-year-old wine cellars and the atmospheric American Bar.

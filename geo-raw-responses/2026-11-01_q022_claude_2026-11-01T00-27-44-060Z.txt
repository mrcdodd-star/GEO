--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
Both are UK-based villa specialists, but they work differently. Which suits you depends on where you're going and how much hand-holding you want.

## The Thinking Traveller
- **Focus:** 
It has offered access to villas in the heart of the Mediterranean since 2002.
 The portfolio is smaller and more curated than Oliver's.
- **Local support:** Reviewers often praise the on-the-ground house managers. 
One said the local house managers were lovely and attentive, and that the website deserves five stars for ease of use.
 
There's also a personal "Villa Matchmaker" service.

- **Extras:** Guests mention it arranging chefs, wine, car hire and boat trips. 
One summary says support centres on practical villa management rather than broad concierge services.

- **Reviews:** 
Trustpilot's summary of 160 reviews on one page says reviewers overwhelmingly had a great experience.
 Complaints exist too. 
One reviewer said coordination, accuracy and support fell short when plans went wrong.
 
Another said extras were charged at close to double local rates.


## Oliver's Travels
- **Range:** It's broader, with villas across many European countries and further afield. This comes from my general knowledge, so check the site for the current destinations.
- **Reviews:** The feedback is more mixed. Feefo shows 
a 4.7/5 service rating.
 Many guests say the villa matched the photos. 
One said Oliver's had already done the hard work of searching, with better availability and pricing than a rival site.

- **Main criticism:** Some reviewers say Oliver's acts as an introducer rather than the operator. 
One Trustpilot reviewer said it takes your money and washes its hands of responsibility.
 
Another said it only introduces you to the property owners, which removes its own responsibility.
 
A third said they got no redress over a villa that didn't match its listing.

- **Positive coverage:** 
Rolling Stone's reviewer praised the vetted inventory, concierge service and on-site service.
 That was one stay at a French château, so it's a single data point.

## Quick take
| | Thinking Traveller | Oliver's Travels |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | Mediterranean (Italy, Greece, Corsica) with a managed, personal feel | Wider choice of destinations, group sizes and budgets |
| Service model | Local house managers and a dedicated contact | Mixed. Many happy guests, but some complaints about little help once you've booked |
| Risk | Possibly pricier, with a narrower choice | Quality and accuracy vary more from villa to villa |

If you're heading to Italy, Greece or Corsica and want a smooth, supported stay, the Thinking Traveller looks like the safer bet. If you want a destination it doesn't cover, or more choice on price and size, Oliver's can work well. Ask them what happens if something goes wrong on arrival, and check the cancellation terms.

Where are you thinking of going, how many are you, and what's your rough budget? I can narrow it down from there. Also check that any booking carries ATOL or ABTA protection if flights are included, and look at the deposit and balance terms.

--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
Electric motorcycles have become the ultimate tool for daily city commuting. Unlike traditional gas-powered bikes, electric commuters eliminate the blistering engine heat, heavy clutch work in gridlock, and high maintenance costs of internal combustion engines. Instead, they deliver silent, zero-emission, "twist-and-go" convenience with instant torque that makes navigating urban environments a breeze.

The best electric motorcycles for daily city commuting are categorized below by what they do best, from budget-friendly options to premium highway-capable machines.

---

### 1. Best Overall / Premium Commuter: LiveWire S2 Del Mar
If your commute involves a mix of city streets and occasional highway stretches, the **LiveWire S2 Del Mar** (and its siblings on the S2 platform, like the *S2 Mulholland*) represents the gold standard. Built on LiveWire's lightweight "Arrow" platform, it feels nimble while packing a serious performance punch. 

* **MSRP:** ~$15,500
* **Top Speed:** 103 mph
* **Real-World City Range:** ~110 miles (around 60–70 miles on the highway)
* **Charging:** Level 2 AC fast charging (charges from 20% to 90% in under an hour). 
* **Why it’s great for commuting:** It offers incredible power (84 hp peak) and handling that allows you to easily outpace city traffic and confidently merge onto the freeway. It also supports Level 2 charging, meaning you can easily top it up at public charging stations. 

### 2. Best Value & Portability: Ryvid Anthem
The **Ryvid Anthem** is one of the most innovative and budget-friendly electric motorcycles on the market. It is specifically designed to solve the common issues faced by urban apartment dwellers.

* **MSRP:** ~$6,000
* **Top Speed:** 75+ mph
* **Real-World City Range:** ~70–75 miles
* **Charging:** Removable 4.3 kWh battery (can be rolled indoors like a suitcase to charge via a standard 110V wall outlet).
* **Why it’s great for commuting:** Beyond its highly competitive price, the Anthem features an **electronically adjustable seat height** (from 30 to 34 inches), allowing you to raise it for highway visibility or lower it for confident stop-and-go foot placement. At just 313 lbs, it is exceptionally easy to handle.

### 3. Best Style & Urban Sophistication: Maeving RM1S
For the commuter who wants a head-turning aesthetic without sacrificing practicality, the British-built **Maeving RM1S** is a retro-inspired work of art. Designed by former Triumph engineers, it blends a vintage café racer profile with quiet, modern electric power.

* **MSRP:** ~$9,000 (US) / £7,495 (UK)
* **Top Speed:** 70 mph
* **Real-World City Range:** ~80 miles (with dual battery setup)
* **Charging:** Twin removable batteries that can be charged from any standard home or office outlet.
* **Why it’s great for commuting:** The RM1S is incredibly lightweight (~310 lbs) and perfectly balanced. Because the motor is housed in the rear hub, the "tank" area is actually a 10-liter storage compartment. It's a low-maintenance, high-style commuter ideal for strictly urban and suburban limits.

### 4. Best American-Built Custom: LAND Moto District (Street or ADV)
Hand-built in Cleveland, Ohio, the **LAND Moto District** is a highly customizable, lightweight street-legal electric motorcycle that bridges the gap between high-end e-bikes and true motorcycles. 

* **MSRP:** Starts at ~$6,995 (up to $11,200 for the long-range/ADV spec)
* **Top Speed:** 70+ mph
* **Real-World City Range:** 40 to 120 miles (depending on the battery capacity chosen)
* **Charging:** Removable 72V battery packs.
* **Why it’s great for commuting:** The District weighs an ultra-light 240 lbs and feels like a beefed-up, incredibly agile mountain bike. It features 4 distinct ride modes (Mode 1 behaves like an e-bike, while Mode 4 unlocks full motorcycle power and speed), making it an excellent, approachable "on-ramp" for beginner riders transitioning to motorcycles.

### 5. Best Supermoto Carver: Zero FXE
The **Zero FXE** is a futuristic, stripped-down supermoto designed specifically for carving up tight city streets. 

* **MSRP:** ~$12,195
* **Top Speed:** 85 mph
* **Real-World City Range:** ~100 miles (drops to ~40-50 miles with heavy highway use)
* **Charging:** Standard J1772 or 110V wall outlet (takes about 9 hours to full, though an optional accessory quick-charger can cut this to ~4 hours).
* **Why it’s great for commuting:** Weighing only 301 lbs, the FXE features a tall seat height (32.9 inches) and wide bars, giving you an upright, commanding view over traffic. Its quick, punchy acceleration and high-quality Showa suspension make it incredibly fun for urban environments.

### 6. Best Playful Mini-Commuter: BMW CE 02
BMW calls the **CE 02** an "e-parkourer." Falling somewhere between a scooter and a motorcycle, it is targeted directly at younger urban riders and strictly inner-city commutes.

* **MSRP:** ~$7,599
* **Top Speed:** 59 mph
* **Real-World City Range:** ~56 miles
* **Charging:** 110V wall outlet (approx. 3–4 hours).
* **Why it’s great for commuting:** It is compact, incredibly easy to park, and has an ultra-low seat height. If your daily commute consists entirely of surface streets (no freeways) and you want a premium, high-quality build with BMW reliability, this is an excellent, low-stress daily runabout.

---

### Key Factors to Keep in Mind Before Buying:
1. **Your Charging Setup:** If you live in an apartment without a garage, look *only* at bikes with **removable batteries** (Ryvid, Maeving, LAND). Hauling a 300-lb bike into your living room to plug it in is impossible, but carrying a 35-lb battery to your desk or kitchen is highly practical.
2. **Highway vs. City Streets:** Electric bikes lose battery much faster at sustained highway speeds (70+ mph) due to wind resistance. If your daily route is strictly local streets, your range will stretch significantly; if you have a 20-mile highway stretch, buy a bike with a larger battery and highway capacity (like the LiveWire S2 or Zero FXE).
3. **Gear and Cargo:** Urban commuting usually requires carrying a backpack, laptop, or lunch. Check what factory or aftermarket luggage options exist for your choice (the Maeving has integrated storage, while the LiveWire and Ryvid have accessory racks).

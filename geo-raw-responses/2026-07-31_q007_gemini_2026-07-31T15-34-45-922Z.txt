--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
Claridge’s and The Dorchester represent the absolute pinnacle of luxury afternoon tea in London. Located just a ten-minute walk apart in Mayfair, they both sit at the top tier of the market. 

The cost for the traditional experience is nearly identical (starting at **£95 per person**, with Champagne options starting at **£110–£125+**). However, the two experiences offer fundamentally different atmospheres, culinary styles, and physical environments. 

---

### 1. The Setting & Vibe: Art Deco Glamour vs. Plush Opulence

#### **Claridge’s (The Foyer & Reading Room)**
* **The Aesthetic:** Pure, sleek 1920s Art Deco elegance. The room features mirrored pillars, a striking Dale Chihuly hand-blown glass chandelier in the center, and the hotel's iconic jade-and-white striped tableware. 
* **The Atmosphere:** Bustling, theatrical, and high-fashion. It is very much a "see-and-be-seen" venue. The soundtrack is a sophisticated blend of classical and contemporary music played by a live pianist and cellist.
* **Best For:** Guests who want a highly polished, legendary London "event" and appreciate historic design.

#### **The Dorchester (The Promenade)**
* **The Aesthetic:** Grand, palatial, and deeply warm. The Promenade was fully transformed under a major Pierre-Yves Rochon redesign. It features massive Corinthian marble columns, gold-leaf accents, and towering, dramatic floral arrangements created by in-house designer florist Philip Hammond. 
* **The Atmosphere:** Sumptuous and incredibly relaxing. The deep, overstuffed sofas and plush armchairs are legendary for being so comfortable that guests never want to leave. It is accompanied by a live pianist playing on a grand piano. 
* **Best For:** Guests looking for ultimate physical comfort, breathtaking floral displays, and a slightly more intimate, cozy-yet-grand vibe.

---

### 2. The Food & Pastries: French Precision vs. Bold Flavors

#### **Claridge’s**
* **The Pastries:** Led by Executive Pastry Chef **Thibault Hauchard** (formerly of Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester and Le Meurice). The pastries are masterpieces of French precision—visually delicate, perfectly structured, and incredibly refined. 
* **The Scones:** Claridge’s puts a unique spin on tradition by serving a mix of classic raisin and seasonal apple-infused scones, accompanied by clotted cream and their distinctively rich, fragrant *Marco Polo* tea gelée.
* **The Tea:** An extraordinary, highly curated list of rare teas sourced directly from family-run estates, served in Claridge’s signature bespoke china.

#### **The Dorchester**
* **The Pastries:** Led by Executive Pastry Chef **Michael Kwan** (UK Pastry Team President and World Pastry Cup champion). The desserts are highly imaginative, bold, and often floral- or fruit-forward, perfectly matching the hotel’s garden themes.
* **The Sandwiches:** Frequently praised by food critics as some of the finest finger sandwiches in London. Standard menus often feature exceptional pairings like champagne-infused cucumber or aged beef sirloin.
* **The Scones:** Exceptionally soft, light, and served warm straight from the oven with homemade seasonal preserves and Cornish clotted cream.

---

### 3. Service & Table Setup: Highly Choreographed vs. Warmly Attentive

* **Claridge’s (Clever Design):** Claridge’s uses a brilliantly practical table setup: rather than crowding your table with the tiered cake stand, they place the tiers on a **tall, elegant side stand** next to your table. This leaves you with plenty of room to relax with your teacups and champagne flutes. Service here is highly choreographed, swift, and highly professional.
* **The Dorchester (Generous Hospitality):** The Dorchester’s tailcoat-wearing staff are famous for being incredibly warm, approachable, and highly attentive without a hint of stuffiness. They are exceptionally generous with refills; you will repeatedly be offered fresh plates of your favorite sandwiches and warm scones without having to ask.

### The Verdict: Which should you choose?

* **Choose Claridge’s if:** You want the ultimate bucket-list London tea ritual. It is the correct choice if you love Art Deco aesthetic, want to dress up to the nines, prefer a lively, chic atmosphere, and want to experience the flawless choreography of London’s most famous hotel tea.
* **Choose The Dorchester if:** You are a major foodie who prioritizes having the absolute best-tasting sandwiches and scones. It is also the ideal choice if you want to sink into incredibly comfortable seating, prefer towering, gorgeous floral backdrops, and want a warm, slightly more relaxed luxury service.

## What to Think About First

When considering châteaux, it helps to have a few key preferences in mind so your search is more focused:

- **Region**: Loire Valley (classic & romantic), Normandy, Provence, Dordogne, Occitanie, etc. Transportation/logistics differ a lot depending on where you go.
- **Guest numbers**: Some châteaux accommodate large receptions (250+), others are better for smaller, intimate weddings.
- **Overnight accommodation**: Want everyone to stay on-site, some local guesthouses, or a mix?
- **Budget**: Prices differ greatly based on size, location, prestige, and what's included. A château in the Paris region typically costs more than in rural areas.
- **Dates & rental length**: Many châteaux offer weekend packages (Fri–Sun); securing the right date often requires booking well in advance.
- **Included services**: Will the château provide a coordinator, tables/chairs, catering? Are you allowed to bring your own vendors?

## Budgeting: What to Expect

- **Venue hire only**: Front entrance fees vary widely. For example, using *The Venue Advisors* listings, many châteaux cost between **€5,000 and €20,000** for a weekend hire, depending heavily on size, location and what’s included. ([marketplace.thevenueadvisors.com](https://marketplace.thevenueadvisors.com/en/availability/2026/?utm_source=openai))  
- **All-inclusive weekend stays** often cost more, especially when you include accommodation, staff, catering, rentals (tables/chairs), etc. Packages could come in above €20K, depending on scale.  
- **Hidden costs**: Transport for guests, legal requirements (especially if you want a legal ceremony vs symbolic), suppliers (caterers, DJs, photographers), decorations, site visit costs.

---

## Legal & Practical Things (from the UK Perspective)

- **Marriage laws**: If you want a civil ceremony in France, there are legal residency requirements (in many cases, one of you must live in the commune or the town hall). Many UK couples instead do a civil ceremony in the UK, then have a symbolic or religious ceremony in France.  
- **Travel & logistics**: Transport for guests, luggage, and décor items—these all add complexity. Choose châteaux with good access (airports, good roads).  
- **Language**: While many venues speak English, having someone local—planner or bilingual speaking staff—will help.  
- **Contracts & deposits**: A deposit will be required. Make sure everything is spelled out in English & French (if needed), especially what’s included, the cancellation policy, and extra fees (cleaning, overtime for music, etc.).  
- **Suppliers**: Some châteaux will have preferred suppliers; others allow you to bring your own. Clarify this early.

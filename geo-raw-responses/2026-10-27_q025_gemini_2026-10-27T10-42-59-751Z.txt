### Key Tips for US Travelers Renting European Villas

1. **Air Conditioning (AC):** AC is not standard in older European homes, even luxury ones in Northern or Central Europe (and parts of Tuscany/Provence). If traveling in summer, **explicitly check if bedrooms AND living areas have AC**, not just "fans."
2. **Bed Configurations:** In Europe, "double beds" can sometimes be two single mattresses pushed together. Confirm bed sizes (King vs. Queen vs. Twin) with the agent if you have couples or kids sharing rooms.
3. **Accessibility:** Many historic European villas (especially in Italy and Greece) have steep stairs, stone pathways, or hillside access. Mention any mobility constraints or stroller needs to the agency beforehand.
4. **Car Rentals & Access:** Large family villas are often situated in rural countryside. Make sure to check road conditions—some winding, narrow estate drives in Europe cannot accommodate large 12-passenger vans.

For a UK-based traveller, these are strong starting points, depending on the style of villa holiday you want:

- **CV Villas** — A good all-rounder for European destinations, with a broad selection across Greece, Italy, Spain, France, Portugal and Croatia. It offers UK-based support and says package holidays booked with it are protected; its **ABOVE** collection focuses on especially luxurious, staffed villas.[7]
- **The Thinking Traveller** — Best for a carefully curated Mediterranean villa, particularly in destinations such as Italy and Greece. It has a London office and offers serviced villas with options such as housekeeping, private chefs and local concierge support.[1][8]
- **Firefly Collection** — Worth considering for a wide choice: it lists more than 1,000 properties and works with multiple villa operators. It has a London office, so ask who will handle your booking and on-the-ground support for the specific villa.[2]
- **The Villa Agency** — A UK-based option with villas across Europe, including Portugal. It says holidays booked through it are ATOL- or ABTOT-bonded; check which protection applies to your particular booking.[4]
- **Abercrombie & Kent Villas** — A higher-end choice if you want concierge help and tailored experiences; its European portfolio includes Italy, France, Greece, Spain and Portugal.[10]
- **AMA Selections** — A boutique-style option for distinctive luxury homes, with European properties and a bespoke booking approach.[3]

**Quick shortlist:** CV Villas for breadth and booking protection; The Thinking Traveller for a Mediterranean specialist; A&K for concierge-led service. Before paying, confirm what’s included (staff, cleaning, utilities and local taxes), the cancellation terms, and whether the booking is financially protected—especially if it’s accommodation-only.

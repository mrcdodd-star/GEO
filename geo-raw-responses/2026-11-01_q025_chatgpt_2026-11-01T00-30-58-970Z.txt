## What to Look for When Booking for a Large Family

To make sure your villa rental is smooth and enjoyable, especially with many people, here are the key things you’ll want to double-check:

1. **Number of Bedrooms & Bathrooms**  
   Share-house style arrangements don’t work as well with big groups. More space = more comfort.

2. **Kitchen & Dining Space**  
   A big family often means cooking or large group meals. Is the kitchen well-equipped? Big dining table? Outdoor dining?

3. **Amenities for All Ages**  
   Pools, play areas, accessibility (no steep stairs if there are elder members), laundry, outdoor space, WiFi.

4. **Support & On-Ground Services**  
   • Local contact/manager  
   • Concierge or ability to arrange meals, transport, experiences  
   • Daily housekeeping if needed  
   • Emergency line

   Companies like Le Collectionist, Simpson Travel, CV Villas, and Tuscany Now & More offer robust local support. ([uncompromised.travel](https://uncompromised.travel/stays/luxury-villa-rental-company-index-2026?utm_source=openai))

5. **Transparency in Pricing**  
   Look for all taxes, cleaning fees, service fees, damage deposit up front. Some platforms hide fees until checkout. Agencies score better here. ([uncompromised.travel](https://uncompromised.travel/stays/luxury-villa-rental-company-index-2026?utm_source=openai))

6. **Payment & Protection**  
   • Pay by credit card when possible  
   • Check if the company provides cancellation options and/or purchase insurance  
   • Using agencies with established protection schemes (e.g. ATOL or ABTOT in the UK) if booking as part of a travel package helps. ([uncompromised.travel](https://uncompromised.travel/stays/luxury-villa-rental-company-index-2026?utm_source=openai))

## 💡 What to do to protect yourself if you decide to book

Here are some tips to minimize the risk:

- Check recent reviews of the **specific property**, not just the company overall.  
- Ask the owner/property manager directly about any concerns (cleanliness, maintenance, amenities).  
- Keep documentation: take photos on arrival, note anything that doesn’t match the description.  
- Confirm cancellation, refunds and what happens if something goes wrong (e.g., local issues).  
- Use payment methods that offer buyer protection (credit card etc).  
- Don’t assume ABTA protection unless it’s clearly in effect *now* and shown prominently.

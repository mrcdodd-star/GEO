The **best 5-star hotels in London** are consistently cited as icons like **Claridge’s, The Ritz, The Dorchester, The Savoy, 1 Hotel Mayfair, Landmark London, and The Connaught**, alongside newer luxury names such as **The Trafalgar St. James** and **Great Scotland Yard Hotel**.[3][5][7][9][10]

Below is a curated shortlist combining expert reviews (Time Out, Luxury London, Five Star Alliance) with major booking/review platforms:

---

### Classic, world-famous grand hotels

- **Claridge’s (Mayfair)**  
  - Frequently named **London’s most historic luxury hotel**.[3]  
  - Art Deco glamour, renowned afternoon tea, and Michelin-level dining.[3][9][10]  
  - Excellent for Mayfair shopping and high‑end dining nearby.[3]

- **The Ritz London (Piccadilly)**  
  - Described as **“most downright iconic”** among London five‑stars.[3]  
  - Famous Palm Court afternoon tea, classic Louis XVI décor, dress code in main restaurant.[3][9]  
  - Ideal if you want very traditional, old-school luxury.

- **The Dorchester (Park Lane)**  
  - Highlighted for **top Michelin-starred restaurants**, including Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester.[3][9]  
  - Overlooks Hyde Park, strong reputation for service and fine dining.[3][9]  
  - Good mix of classic glamour and modern renovations.

- **The Savoy (Strand / Covent Garden)**  
  - River Thames views and “**riverside luxury and timeless British style**.”[7]  
  - Historic bars and restaurants (including the American Bar and Savoy Grill).[7][9]  
  - Great location for theatre in Covent Garden and the West End.

---

### Luxury boutique & modern style

- **The Connaught (Mayfair)**  
  - Frequently listed among the **top luxury hotels in London**.[9][10]  
  - Michelin-starred restaurant (Hélène Darroze at The Connaught) and a celebrated bar.[9][10]  
  - Intimate, discreet, ideal for a quieter high-end stay.

- **1 Hotel Mayfair (Mayfair)**  
  - Picked as **“best modern”** five‑star hotel in London.[3]  
  - Strong sustainability focus, biophilic design and contemporary rooms.[3]  
  - Good if you prefer eco-conscious but high-luxury accommodation.

- **Great Scotland Yard Hotel (near Trafalgar Square)**  
  - Recommended as one of the **best 5‑star hotels in London** on Expedia.[5]  
  - Housed in the former Metropolitan Police HQ; stylish, characterful interior.[5]  
  - Very central for major sights (Trafalgar Square, Covent Garden, Westminster).

- **The Trafalgar St. James, Curio Collection by Hilton (Trafalgar Square)**  
  - Featured by Expedia as a top 5‑star choice, with a popular rooftop bar.[5]  
  - Strong option if you want modern design in a super‑central location.[5]

---

### Spa-focused and relaxation

- **Landmark London (Marylebone)**  
  - Highlighted as **“best spa hotel”** among five‑stars.[3]  
  - Impressive atrium, indoor pool, and spacious rooms.[3][9]  
  - Good for guests prioritising wellness and larger room sizes.

---

### Other highly regarded five-star options

These frequently appear in expert and luxury-hotel listings:[9][10]

- **The Berkeley (Knightsbridge)** – fashion‑forward styling, rooftop pool, and Marcus restaurant.[9][10]  
- **Mandarin Oriental Hyde Park (Knightsbridge)** – Asian‑influenced luxury, spa, and direct Hyde Park access.[9][10]  
- **The Langham (Marylebone)** – one of London’s grand old hotels, strong food & beverage offering.[9][10]  
- **Shangri‑La The Shard (London Bridge)** – spectacular views from within The Shard; contemporary luxury.[9][10]

### How to choose the “best” for you

- **For classic British glamour:** Claridge’s, The Ritz, The Dorchester, The Savoy.[3][7][9]  
- **For modern, design-led stays:** 1 Hotel Mayfair, Great Scotland Yard, The Trafalgar St. James, Shangri‑La.[3][5][9]  
- **For food lovers:** The Dorchester, The Connaught, The Berkeley, Mandarin Oriental (multiple Michelin‑starred venues).[3][9][10]  
- **For spa and wellness:** Landmark London, Mandarin Oriental, The Berkeley.[3][9][10]  
- **For central sightseeing:** Great Scotland Yard, The Trafalgar St. James, The Savoy, Shangri‑La.[5][7][9]

To refine your choice, check recent guest scores and prices on large platforms like **Tripadvisor** (5-star filter for London)[1][2], **Booking.com**[4][6], **Expedia**[5], or **Trip.com**[8], focusing on the latest reviews and your preferred neighbourhood.

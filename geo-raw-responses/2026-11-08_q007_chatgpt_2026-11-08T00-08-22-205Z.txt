## ✔️ Steps to Find and Book

1. **Set your budget**  
   Fully staffed villas with private chef & daily housekeeping are luxury—expect to pay considerably more than self-catered villas.

2. **Choose the region**  
   Region affects style, food, travel time, and cost (Tuscany, Amalfi Coast, Puglia, Sicily, Lakes etc.). Decide what's more important: scenery, distance from nearest airport, beach vs countryside.

3. **Search with services filters**  
   Use keywords like “staffed”, “chef included”, “daily housekeeping”, “full-service villa”, “private chef service”. Many sites have checkboxes or filters for “staff included”. ([tuscanynowandmore.com](https://www.tuscanynowandmore.com/villa-collections/fully-staffed-villas-in-italy-list?utm_source=openai))

4. **Enquire and read every detail**  
   When you find a villa you like:
   - Ask exactly which meals are included and who pays for groceries.
   - Check housekeeping hours (full-day? few hours? every day?).  
   - What extra services cost (laundry, pool heating, transfers etc.).

5. **Plan ahead**  
   In high season, charters and booking chef services fill up early. Book your villa and chef 2-4 months in advance for shoulder season; 6-12 months for summer. ([chefondemand.it](https://www.chefondemand.it/blog/en/tuscany-villa-rentals-with-chef-guide-2026/?utm_source=openai))

6. **Legal/licensing checks**  
   Ensure the villa has legal accommodation status (in Italy, you might check for a CIN code). Read the contract & cancellation policy carefully.

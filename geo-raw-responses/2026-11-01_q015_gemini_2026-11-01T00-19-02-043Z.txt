### 1. Best for Mediterranean & Overseas Villa Holidays (from the UK)

* **The Thinking Traveller**
  * **Best For:** Unmatched quality across Italy (Sicily, Puglia, Tuscany), Greece, Corsica, and Mallorca.
  * **Why them:** Frequently voted the *Best Villa Company in the World* by *Condé Nast Traveller* readers. Their local insider team, 24/7 support, and curated experiences (like private chefs, wine tastings, and boat trips) are exceptional.

* **CV Villas**
  * **Best For:** Classic European luxury (Greece, Italy, France, Spain) and the Caribbean.
  * **Why them:** A British favourite for over 50 years. They cater to everything from romantic cliffside hideaways to sprawling beachfront estates. As a UK-based operator, they offer full ATOL-protected packages (flights, car hire, and villa) or property-only bookings.

* **Oliver’s Travels**
  * **Best For:** Standout châteaux, grand villas, and unique luxury homes across Europe, Asia, and the Caribbean.
  * **Why them:** UK-headquartered with an impressive portfolio of over 5,000 handpicked properties. They excel at matching unique properties to your style (châteaux in France, cliffside villas in Ibiza) and provide a free concierge team to arrange private chefs, spa treatments, and yacht charters.

* **Simpson Travel**
  * **Best For:** Hassle-free luxury family holidays in Greece, Turkey, France, Italy, and Spain.
  * **Why them:** Highly regarded by UK travellers for their seamless service. They offer flight-inclusive luxury packages with private transfers, boutique villas with private pools, and optional high-end childcare or nanny services directly at the villa.

* **Crabtree & Crabtree**

* **Kate & Tom’s**
  * **Best For:** Large luxury gatherings, milestone birthdays, and family reunions in the UK.
  * **Why them:** Specialises in impressive, large-scale British holiday homes, complete with indoor swimming pools, tennis courts, and party barns.

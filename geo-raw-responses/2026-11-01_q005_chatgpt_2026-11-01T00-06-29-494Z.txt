## What to look for

Before booking, make sure you check:

- Do meals come **fully board** (breakfast, lunch, dinner) or **all-inclusive**?
- Are there **kids’ clubs/crèches**? What ages do they cater for? Are they supervised?
- Is there **babysitting**, or even nanny services?
- How many hours per day is childcare included, and is there an extra charge?
- Is food kid-friendly or does the hotel offer children’s menus?
- What additional amenities are family-friendly (baby gear, pool safety, indoor/outdoor play, etc.)?

## What You Probably Won’t Find Easily

- Free full-day nanny service included in basic price (this is usually premium/extra).
- Resorts that cover childcare *and* kitchen in private villa-style stays at low cost (tends to go up quickly).
- Kids’ clubs for very young babies (under 6 months) are more rare and often have extra fees.

---

## Tips to Maximize Relaxation

- Book in **school holiday in advance** — these fill first. If outside those times, often fewer people, more availability, sometimes lower rates.
- Ask directly whether meals are all included and whether child meals are part of it.
- Ask about **babysitting/nanny-services** available in-resort if you want evenings off too.
- Check the childcare ratio and whether staff are certified (especially for under 3s).
- If self-catering or fully inclusive aren’t an option, villas with chefs or private staff rental could work — though more costly.

## What to Look for When Choosing

To get the best bang for your money and avoid disappointment, keep an eye on:

1. **Property Inspection / Vetting**  
   Make sure the company physically inspects its villas. Top agents (Le Collectionist, The Thinking Traveller, CV Villas) all have in-person inspections. ([uncompromised.travel](https://uncompromised.travel/stays/luxury-villa-rental-company-index-2026?utm_source=openai))

2. **Financial Protection / Legal Cover**  
   UK customers benefit when companies are members of protection schemes like ABTA, ATOL, or ABTOT especially if flights are involved. Simpson Travel, CV Villas name specific protections. ([uncompromised.travel](https://uncompromised.travel/stays/luxury-villa-rental-company-index-2026?utm_source=openai))

3. **Extras & Services**  
   Think about whether you want housekeeping, private chefs, concierge, transport, etc. Onefinestay & The Thinking Traveller are strong here. ([dailystoke.com](https://dailystoke.com/luxury/luxury-villa-rental-agencies/?utm_source=openai))

4. **Transparent Pricing**  
   Good companies show full costs early (weekly rates, taxes, cleaning fees, etc.). Some villas are “price on request”, which can hide extra costs. ([dailystoke.com](https://dailystoke.com/luxury/luxury-villa-rental-agencies/?utm_source=openai))

5. **Location & Scope**  
   Do you want UK staycations or overseas escape (e.g. Mediterranean, Caribbean)? Some companies focus more abroad, others have a strong domestic offering. Choose accordingly.

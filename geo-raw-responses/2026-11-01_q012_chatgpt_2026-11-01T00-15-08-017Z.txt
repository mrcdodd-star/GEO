## What to Consider

- **Destination Vibe**: Some companies (like Mr. Villa & GoToBeach) are more regional (e.g. Morocco or Turkey), others are global (like Villanovo, Firefly).
- **Service Level**: Fully staffed villas cost more, but you're often closer to “turn up and enjoy” experience. Others give more options ala carte (e.g., choose which services you want).
- **Pricing Transparency**: Ask what’s included—chef, transfers and excursions can be hefty extras.
- **Protection & Booking Terms**: Look for ATOL / ABTA protection (for overseas travel), and consider cancellation policies given potential travel disruptions.

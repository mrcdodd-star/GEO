## What to Prioritise When Booking a Multigenerational Holiday

Here's what many families find makes or breaks a trip:

- **Accommodation layout:** Multiple bedrooms, interconnecting rooms, separate living areas so grandparents and kids each get privacy. ([whichfamilyvacation.com](https://whichfamilyvacation.com/answers/best-family-resorts-in/europe/for/multi-gen?utm_source=openai))

- **Ease of travel + minimal transfers:** Short flights, direct connections. Big plus if airport/hotel pick-ups are easy.

- **Flexible dining times & variety:** Some want early dinner, others late; resorts or towns with options and multiple restaurants help. ([europeandestinations.co.uk](https://www.europeandestinations.co.uk/holidays/family-holidays/mixed-age-families/?utm_source=openai))

- **Activity mix:** Not everyone wants the same pace every day. Alternate busy and lazy days. Consider activities ages can share (boat trips, cooking, nature walks, gentle hikes). ([europeandestinations.co.uk](https://www.europeandestinations.co.uk/holidays/family-holidays/mixed-age-families/?utm_source=openai))

- **Mobility & accessibility:** If seniors are involved, avoid steep hills, cobbled streets without smooth paths; property with lift access, easy pool entry.  

- **Length of stay:** 10-14 nights often strikes a good balance. Enough time to settle in, pace things out without feeling rushed. ([europeandestinations.co.uk](https://www.europeandestinations.co.uk/holidays/family-holidays/mixed-age-families/?utm_source=openai))

## Tips
- **Check the sleeping numbers.** "Sleeps 20" sometimes includes sofa beds, extra beds or an annexe. Ask how many real bedrooms and bathrooms there are.
- **Look at clusters or adjoining villas** if you can't find a single house. This is common in Croatia and gives you more choice and often better value.
- **Ask about events and noise rules.** Many villas limit parties and have a maximum number of day guests.
- **Prices are usually quoted per week in euros.** Ask what's included, such as cleaning, pool heating, staff and security deposits.
- **Book early for summer.** The largest villas are few and often go a year or more ahead.

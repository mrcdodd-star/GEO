## Costs and planning
- 
One planner estimates that a French destination wedding for 80–150 guests costs €40,000–€120,000 all in.
 Venue hire is only part of that.
- Check whether you're getting **dry hire** (venue only) or an **all-inclusive package**. 
A package replaces dozens of separate supplier conversations, contracts and payment schedules.

- Many châteaux require a minimum stay of 2–3 nights in peak season.
- Ask whether the venue allows outside caterers.
- Check the sleeping capacity against your guest list.

## Choosing a region
- **Northern France (Normandy, Hauts-de-France):** Easy to reach by car, Eurotunnel or ferry.
- **Loire Valley:** 
It is one of the best-served regions for English-speaking couples who want a multi-day château wedding.

- **Dordogne and Bordeaux:** Direct flights from the UK, and a relaxed feel.
- **South of France:** Vineyard châteaux, often with estate wine.

## Legal ceremony
A civil ceremony in France has residency requirements. Many UK couples have a legal wedding in the UK and a symbolic ceremony at the château. Check the current rules with the *mairie* or a planner before you book.

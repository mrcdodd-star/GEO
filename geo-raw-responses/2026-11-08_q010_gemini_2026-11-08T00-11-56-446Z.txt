If you are based in the UK and looking for a **fully staffed luxury villa holiday in Greece**, you have access to some of the world’s best specialist tour operators and villa agencies. Fully staffed villas offer total relaxation: a private chef to handle dining, daily housekeeping, a villa manager/concierge, and often dedicated hosts or drivers.

---

### Top UK Specialists for Staffed Greek Villas

1. **CV Villas (*ABOVE* Collection)**
   * **Best For:** Classic luxury, multi-generational families, and established island estates (especially Corfu and Paxos).
   * **What to expect:** Their *ABOVE* collection features top-tier properties that come with a dedicated private chef, host/waitstaff, and housekeeper. 
   * **UK Advantage:** Full ABTA/ATOL package protection available (flights + transfers + villa).

2. **The Thinking Traveller**
   * **Best For:** Exclusive, architecturally impressive villas across the Ionian Islands, Cyclades, and Saronic Gulf.
   * **What to expect:** Serviced and fully staffed options with private chefs, daily maid service, and 24/7 local experience managers.

3. **Scott Dunn**
   * **Best For:** Bespoke luxury packages tailored for UK guests wanting seamless travel, private aviation, or Scott Dunn nanny services on-site.
   * **What to expect:** Fully catered and staffed properties in Crete, Corfu, and Mykonos with dedicated hosts, private chefs, and childcare options.

4. **The Luxury Travel Book / Firefly Collection**
   * **Best For:** Ultra-high-end party or milestone celebration villas.
   * **What to expect:** Estate-level properties in places like Paros, Mykonos, and Porto Heli, complete with multi-person teams (chef, sous chef, butler, housekeeper, boat master).

5. **Oliver’s Travels (*Luxe Collection*)**
   * **Best For:** Showpiece design villas with extensive concierge add-ons.
   * **What to expect:** Tailored staffing levels—from daily breakfast chef + maid to full 24-hour estate teams.

---

### Top Regions in Greece for Fully Staffed Villas

* **Corfu (Northeast Coast - Kassiopi, Nisaki, Kalami):** The UK’s flagship luxury villa destination. Direct flights from almost all UK regional airports. Outstanding clifftop estates with private jetties, infinity pools, and full crew teams.
* **Porto Heli (Peloponnese Riviera):** Known as the "Hamptons of Greece." Very discreet, grand waterfront mansions favoured by royals and ultra-high-net-worth travellers looking for complete privacy and dedicated staff.
* **Mykonos & Paros (Cyclades):** Ideal for chic, barefoot-luxury escapes. Villas here often feature full staff (chefs, mixologists, security, chauffeurs) close to cosmopolitan beach clubs.
* **Crete (Elounda & Chania):** Excellent for large families. Large private estates featuring private spa treatments, dedicated cooks, and child-friendly staff.
* **Paxos & Zakynthos:** For smaller groups or intimate retreats with private cooks and secluded sea access.

---

### What "Fully Staffed" Typically Includes

* **Private Chef:** Prepares breakfast plus lunch and/or dinner daily, customized to dietary requirements. *(Note: Food and beverage ingredients are typically billed at cost or paid via an Advance Provisioning Allowance / APA).*
* **Villa Manager / Butler:** Handles day-to-day requests, coordinates excursions, boat charters, and table reservations.
* **Daily Housekeeping:** Discreet, twice-daily maid service, turndown, and laundry.
* **Optional Staff Add-ons:** Dedicated driver with luxury V-Class/SUV, private boat captain, UK-qualified nanny, or wellness staff (yoga instructors/massage therapists).

### Advice for Booking from the UK

1. **Flight Accessibility:** 
   * **Direct routes:** Corfu (CFU), Mykonos (JMK), Santorini (JTR), Chania/Heraklion (CHQ/HER), and Kalamata (KLX) have direct flights from London (LHR, LGW, LCY, STN) as well as Manchester, Birmingham, Bristol, and Edinburgh.
   * **Helicopter/Private Boat Transfers:** For island villas (e.g., Paxos or private islands), UK operators can arrange direct helicopter charters from Athens or Corfu airport straight to your villa helipad.
2. **Booking Window:** Staffed villas for peak UK school holidays (July–August) book out **6 to 12 months in advance**. May, June, and September offer superb weather, lower heat, and better availability.
3. **ATOL Protection:** If you book flights and the villa together through a UK-licensed luxury agent (like CV Villas or Scott Dunn), your entire holiday is ATOL protected.

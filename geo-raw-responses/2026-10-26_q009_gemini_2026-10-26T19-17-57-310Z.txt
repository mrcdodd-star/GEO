Renting a **fully catered luxury villa in the Mediterranean** combines the privacy and space of a personal estate with the 5-star service of a luxury resort. Whether you are planning a multi-generational family vacation, a celebration with friends, or a quiet coastal retreat, catered villas eliminate the stress of cooking, shopping, and housekeeping.

### What a "Fully Catered" Villa Experience Includes

While level of service varies by property, a fully catered Mediterranean villa standardly includes:

1. **Private Chef & Kitchen Team:** 
   * Pre-arrival menu planning tailored to your dietary preferences.
   * Daily breakfast, lunch, and multi-course dinners using local, seasonal Mediterranean ingredients.
   * Afternoon teas, pool-side snacks, and wine pairings.
2. **Housekeeping & Butler Service:**
   * Daily housekeeping, turndown service, and linen changes.
   * Dedicated villa hosts/butlers to serve meals, mix cocktails, and manage daily requests.
3. **Dedicated Local Concierge:**
   * Airport transfers, private yacht charters, helicopter transfers, winery tours, and spa treatments organized on-demand.
4. **Pre-Arrival Provisioning:**
   * The fridge and bar are fully stocked before you arrive with your favorite wines, spirits, soft drinks, and snacks.

### Top Mediterranean Destinations for Catered Villas

#### 1. Spain: Ibiza & Mallorca (Balearic Islands)
* **Vibe:** Chic, design-forward estates, ocean views, and farm-to-table Balearic gastronomy.
* **Best For:** Sunset dinners, poolside lounge vibes, and proximity to private beach clubs.
* **Highlight:** Hillside fincas or clifftop contemporary villas with expansive outdoor dining terraces.

#### 2. Italy: Amalfi Coast, Tuscany, & Sardinia
* **Vibe:** Historic coastal palazzos, cliffside infinity pools, and authentic regional Italian cooking.
* **Best For:** Food and wine lovers who want authentic homemade pastas, fresh seafood, and local wine pairings prepared in-house.
* **Highlight:** Terraced lemon grove estates on the Amalfi Coast or grand beachfront compounds in Costa Smeralda (Sardinia).

#### 3. Greece: Mykonos, Santorini, Corfu, & Crete
* **Vibe:** White-washed Cycladic architecture, turquoise sea views, and fresh Ionian/Aegean seafood banquets.
* **Best For:** Island hopping, cliffside views, and relaxed outdoor dining setups under the stars.
* **Highlight:** Ultra-private cliffside compounds with private jetties or helipads.

#### 4. France: Côte d’Azur & Provence
* **Vibe:** High-end French Riviera elegance, Michelin-level private chefs, and historic châteaux.
* **Best For:** Discerning guests seeking top-tier culinary standards, vintage champagne cellars, and proximity to Saint-Tropez or Cannes.

#### 5. Croatia: Dalmatian Coast (Hvar, Dubrovnik, Korčula)
* **Vibe:** Secluded waterfront stone villas, private boat access, and Mediterranean-Adriatic fusion dining.
* **Best For:** Travelers wanting crystal-clear water, privacy away from larger crowds, and easy access to private yacht excursions.

### Key Questions to Ask Before Booking

1. **How is food billing structured?** 
   * *Full Board / Inclusive:* Chef labor, groceries, and beverages are bundled into the rental cost.
   * *Chef-Only + Cost of Ingredients (APA):* The chef’s service fee is included in the rent, but food/beverages are billed at cost based on local supermarket receipts or an Advance Provisioning Allowance (APA).
2. **What are the chef's working hours?** 
   * Clarify if the chef prepares 3 meals a day, or 2 meals (e.g., breakfast + lunch or dinner) to give guests flexibility to dine out on occasion.
3. **Is staff accommodation on-site?**
   * Some large estates have separate staff quarters, while others have live-in staff or staff that commute daily. Check which setup fits your privacy preference.

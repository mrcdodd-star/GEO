**Oliver’s Travels** offers a complimentary pre-arrival concierge service for guests booking luxury villas, châteaux, or holiday homes. 

---

### Key Services Offered
The concierge team helps customize your trip before you arrive:

* **Private Chefs & Catering:** Arrange local private chefs, caterers, or special event meals tailored to dietary requirements.
* **Fridge Stocking:** Have groceries, fresh local produce, and essential food/drinks delivered before you arrive.
* **Tours & Experiences:** Book local wine tastings, cooking classes, guided tours, or custom event packages.
* **Transport & Travel Extras:** Coordinate airport transfers, car rentals, taxi bookings, or ski passes/ski hire.
* **Housekeeping:** Schedule extra mid-week cleans, towel/linen changes, or daily housekeeping services.

---

### How to Use the Concierge Service
1. **Timing:** Requests should ideally be submitted **at least 6 weeks prior** to your holiday start date.
2. **Submit a Request Form:** Once a booking is confirmed, complete the **Concierge Request Form** on the Oliver’s Travels website using your **6 or 7-digit booking number** and lead booker email address.
3. **Costs:** The concierge planning service itself is included with your stay, but the underlying third-party services (e.g., groceries, private chef fees, car rentals) incur separate charges.

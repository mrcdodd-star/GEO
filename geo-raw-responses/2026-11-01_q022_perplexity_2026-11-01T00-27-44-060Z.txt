**UK-specific point:** both are UK-based, but the comparison describes Oliver’s Travels as acting as an **agent for the property owner**, while The Thinking Traveller contracts as **principal**. The cited source says neither names a financial-protection scheme, so check the contract and payment protection for your specific booking before paying.[1]

**Rule of thumb:** if you already know you want a villa in the Mediterranean and value local help, lean **The Thinking Traveller**. If destination flexibility, a larger range of styles, or a more unusual property matters more, lean **Oliver’s Travels**. Compare the exact villa, dates, total price and cancellation terms—those may matter more than the brand.

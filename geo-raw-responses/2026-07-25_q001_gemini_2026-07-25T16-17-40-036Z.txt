--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
The electric motorcycle market is more diverse and advanced than ever, with better battery ranges, fast-charging capabilities, and legacy motorcycle manufacturers finally entering the space alongside innovative startups. 

Depending on your riding style—whether you want a daily commuter, a high-performance track weapon, a rugged off-road machine, or an urban runabout—the best electric motorbike brands are categorized below by their strengths:

---

### 1. The Industry Heavyweights (Best All-Rounders)

These brands have robust dealer networks, proven reliability, and a wide range of models.

#### **Zero Motorcycles** (USA)
Often referred to as the "Tesla of motorcycles," Zero is the longest-running and most established electric motorcycle brand. They offer everything from lightweight urban commuters to high-performance street fighters and heavy adventure bikes.
*   **Best Known For:** Excellent software, highly customizable riding modes, and a massive dealer network for easy servicing.
*   **Key Models:** 
    *   **Zero SR/F:** The gold-standard electric naked streetfighter, putting out 110 hp.
    *   **Zero DSR/X:** A heavyweight electric adventure (ADV) bike designed for touring and light off-roading.
    *   **Zero FXE:** A lightweight, nimble supermoto that is arguably the ultimate urban commuter.

#### **LiveWire** (USA)
Spun off from Harley-Davidson, LiveWire combines Harley's manufacturing muscle and premium build quality with cutting-edge EV technology. LiveWire bikes are praised for having some of the best handling and "soul" in the electric market.
*   **Best Known For:** Premium fit and finish, excellent fast charging on their flagship model, and aggressive tracker styling.
*   **Key Models:**
    *   **LiveWire S2 Del Mar:** A highly agile, flat-tracker-inspired middleweight bike that serves as a perfect everyday urban performer.
    *   **LiveWire ONE:** The premium flagship cruiser/streetfighter with fast-charging capability. 

---

### 2. High-Performance & Premium Brands

For riders who want blistering speed, massive range, and premium, exotic components.

#### **Energica** (Italy)
If Zero is Tesla, Energica is the Ferrari of electric motorcycles. Hailing from Italy's motor valley, Energica builds heavy, high-voltage, incredibly powerful sportbikes and tourers that come standard with DC Fast Charging (CCS).
*   **Best Known For:** Brutal acceleration, race-grade Brembo and Öhlins components, and the ability to charge from 0% to 80% in about 40 minutes.
*   **Key Models:**
    *   **Energica Experia:** The market's first purpose-built electric sport-touring/adventure motorcycle, built for long-distance comfort.
    *   **Energica Ego+:** A fully-faired electric superbike with a top speed of 150 mph.

#### **Verge Motorcycles** (Finland)
Verge is a boutique brand making waves with incredibly futuristic designs and boundary-pushing engineering. Their bikes feature a hubless rear wheel where the electric motor is actually integrated inside the rim.
*   **Best Known For:** Jaw-dropping design, massive torque, and leading-edge technology.
*   **Key Models:** 
    *   **Verge TS Pro / TS Ultra:** High-end cruisers boasting up to 370 miles of range and extremely rapid charge times.

---

### 3. Legacy Brands & New Entrants

Traditional powersports giants are now producing highly polished, mass-market electric bikes.

#### **Can-Am (BRP)** (Canada)
After decades away from the two-wheel market, Can-Am returned with a splash by launching a highly-praised electric lineup. They utilize liquid-cooled Rotax E-Power motors and an enclosed, maintenance-free chain case.
*   **Best Known For:** Beginner-friendliness, high build quality, liquid-cooled battery longevity, and built-in Apple CarPlay.
*   **Key Models:**
    *   **Can-Am Pulse:** A smooth, accessible naked street bike for urban commuting.
    *   **Can-Am Origin:** A dual-sport bike capable of both highway cruising and dirt-trail riding.

#### **BMW Motorrad** (Germany)
BMW has focused its electric efforts entirely on premium urban mobility. Rather than traditional motorcycles, they have redefined the "scooter" and urban "motobike" segments.
*   **Best Known For:** Futuristic, sci-fi aesthetics and highly refined German engineering.
*   **Key Models:**
    *   **BMW CE 04:** A large, highway-capable electric maxi-scooter with striking architectural styling.
    *   **BMW CE 02:** A smaller, playful electric "e-parkourer" designed for Gen-Z city riders.

---

### 4. Off-Road, Dirt, & Lightweight Playbikes

If you want to tear up trails or MX tracks without waking the neighbors, these brands dominate the dirt.

#### **Stark Future** (Spain)
Stark completely disrupted the motocross industry with the **Stark VARG**. It is widely considered by professional riders to be the most powerful and customizable production dirt bike on the planet, gas or electric.
*   **Best Known For:** Near-silent operation, a phone-based dashboard that lets you tune the engine from 10 hp to 80 hp, and matching or beating 450cc gas dirt bikes in every metric.

#### **Sur-Ron & Talaria** (China)
These brands occupy the wildly popular "lightweight trail bike" (or "e-moto") category. Sitting somewhere between a heavy mountain bike and a light dirt bike, they are incredibly popular for trail riding, backyard tracks, and urban stunt riding.
*   **Key Models:** **Sur-Ron Light Bee X** (or the heavier **Ultra Bee**), and the **Talaria Sting R**. 

---

### Summary: Which Brand is Right for You?
*   Choose **Zero** if you want a reliable, daily-use street bike with great dealer support.
*   Choose **LiveWire** if you want a premium, highly stylized urban cruiser with incredible handling.
*   Choose **Energica** if you want to go fast, go far, and require fast highway charging.
*   Choose **Can-Am** if you are a beginner or commuter looking for a highly polished, low-maintenance daily rider.
*   Choose **Stark** or **Sur-Ron** if you want to ride off-road, hit the trails, or ride motocross.

### ✅ Tips to Help You Choose & Plan

Here are some finer points to help decide and make the booking go well:

1. **Pick the travel time carefully:** For 16 people, early booking saves cost and ensures better room selection. Fly early morning if possible so toddlers nap on the plane.

2. **Accommodation layout:** Villas or multi-room family suites are more restful. Ensure grandparents have accessible rooms. If staying in a resort, find one with elevators, ground-floor rooms, etc.

3. **Meals:** All-inclusive or partially so? Often easier when groups have toddlers—buffets or resorts with flexible dining is helpful. If self-catering, getting groceries locally should be accessible.

4. **Backup plans for bad weather:** Museums, indoor play areas, shorter drives. Destinations like northern Italy or Portugal generally give better options for mixed weather days.

5. **Pace yourselves:** Even just having a “rest day” mid-trip, especially with toddlers who often need downtime.

## Tips for August
- **Prices:** The "from" prices above are usually low-season rates. In August, expect roughly double or more for the same villa.
- **Bedrooms and beds:** Check the bed configuration. "Sleeps 12" sometimes includes sofa beds or children's beds. If you're 12 adults, look for 6 bedrooms with en-suites.
- **Air conditioning:** Make sure it covers the bedrooms. Some villas only have it in part of the house.
- **Pool heating:** Heating isn't needed in August, but it's useful if you have small children. Check the pool depth and fencing too.
- **Location:** The Golden Triangle (Quinta do Lago, Vale do Lobo, Vilamoura) is polished and close to Faro airport. The western Algarve (Salema, Alvor, Lagos) is quieter and more scenic. Albufeira is best if you want nightlife and walkable restaurants.
- **Car hire:** Many villas are rural, so you'll probably need 2–3 cars or a minibus. Some listings say car hire is "essential".
- **Flights:** Faro is well served from UK airports, but August flights for a group of 12 sell out, so book flights and villa together.
- **Payment:** Pay by credit card or through an ATOL- or ABTA-protected agent where possible. Be cautious about paying direct by bank transfer to unknown owners.

When looking for the best electric motorcycle for daily city commuting, the ideal choice depends on your specific living situation (whether you have a garage or live in an apartment), your budget, and whether your route includes highway stretches. 

The top electric motorcycles for urban commuting are categorized below by what they do best:

---

### 1. Best for Apartment Dwellers & Retro Styling: **Maeving RM2 / RM1S**
If you don't have a garage with a charging port, you need a bike with **removable batteries** that you can carry inside to charge. The British-built Maeving line has perfected this.
* **Why it’s great for city commuting:** It looks like a stunning, high-quality classic café racer rather than a futuristic appliance. The **RM2** is built for two-up riding (allowing you to carry a passenger), while the **RM1S** is a sporty single-seater. Both use dual removable "suitcase" batteries that you can slip out and plug into any standard wall outlet.
* **Top Speed:** 70 mph
* **Real-World City Range:** ~80 miles (combined city/suburb riding)
* **Charging:** ~3–4 hours (removable batteries)
* **Approx. Price:** ~$10,995 / £7,995

### 2. Best Budget-Friendly & Adjustable: **Ryvid Anthem**
The Ryvid Anthem is widely considered the best-value lightweight electric motorcycle on the market. 
* **Why it’s great for city commuting:** It features a highly innovative, aerospace-inspired folded stainless steel frame and an **electronically adjustable seat height** (ranging from 30 to 34 inches), allowing you to raise or lower the bike on the fly. It also has a removable 4.3 kWh battery with wheels and a fold-out handle, making it highly portable for apartment dwellers.
* **Top Speed:** 75+ mph
* **Real-World City Range:** ~75 miles
* **Charging:** Built-in Level 2 charger (charges from 20% to 80% in about 30 minutes on a public J1772 plug) or 110V wall outlet.
* **Approx. Price:** ~$6,495

### 3. Best Urban Agility & Fun Factor: **Zero FXE**
If you want something light, aggressive, and highly maneuverable to flick through tight city traffic, the Zero FXE is the gold standard.
* **Why it’s great for city commuting:** It rides like a lightweight supermoto (a dirt bike on street tires). It is incredibly narrow, weighs only 298 lbs, and delivers instant, silent torque that makes zip-and-go city riding an absolute blast. 
* **Top Speed:** 85 mph
* **Real-World City Range:** ~100 miles (drops to ~50–60 miles if you hit sustained highway speeds).
* **Charging:** Fixed battery. Standard overnight charge (~9 hours) on a 110V outlet, though fast-charging accessories can cut this down to under 2 hours.
* **Approx. Price:** ~$11,000–$12,495

### 4. Best "Normal" Motorcycle Feel: **Can-Am Pulse**
For riders transitioning from traditional gas-powered bikes who want a premium product that feels familiar, BRP’s Can-Am Pulse is a highly refined newcomer.
* **Why it’s great for city commuting:** Its proportions, weight distribution, and riding posture match a naked middleweight gas bike (like a Yamaha MT-07). It has a liquid-cooled Rotax electric motor and a fully enclosed chain-drive swingarm, meaning next-to-zero drivetrain maintenance. Its active regenerative braking is so effective that you barely have to touch the physical brakes in stop-and-go traffic.
* **Top Speed:** 80 mph
* **Real-World City Range:** ~50 to 70 miles
* **Charging:** Fixed battery. Level 2 charging takes about 1.5 hours to 100% (or 5 hours on Level 1).
* **Approx. Price:** ~$13,999 (though frequently found at dealership discounts closer to $9,000)

### 5. Best High-Performance / Sport Commuter: **LiveWire S2 Del Mar**
Built by Harley-Davidson's electric sub-brand, the S2 Del Mar is for commuters who need to mix city street riding with fast highway stretches.
* **Why it’s great for city commuting:** With 84 horsepower and 194 lb-ft of torque, it does 0–60 mph in just 3 seconds. It features flat-track styling, top-tier Brembo brakes, and a highly advanced software suite with over-the-air updates. 
* **Top Speed:** 103 mph
* **Real-World City Range:** ~110 miles (~60 miles highway)
* **Charging:** Fixed battery. Supports fast Level 2 charging (0% to 80% in about 75 minutes).
* **Approx. Price:** ~$15,499 (promotional pricing sometimes dips to $9,999)

### 6. Best Urban Scooter/Mini-Bike Hybrid: **BMW CE 02**
BMW refers to this quirky machine as an "eParkourer," aimed squarely at younger, fashion-forward urban commuters.
* **Why it’s great for city commuting:** It bridges the gap between a step-through scooter and a mini-motorcycle (like a Honda Grom). It features a low 29.5-inch seat height, minimalist panels, BMW’s premium build quality, and a highly responsive twist-and-go throttle with no clutch. It is exceptionally easy to park and filter through dense urban environments.
* **Top Speed:** 59 mph
* **Real-World City Range:** ~55 miles
* **Charging:** 0–80% in about 1.5 hours with the Highline quick-charger package.
* **Approx. Price:** Starting at ~$7,599

---

### Key Takeaways for Buying an Electric Commuter:
1. **The "Apartment Rule":** If you cannot park your bike next to an electrical outlet in a garage or driveway, **only** look at bikes with removable batteries (Maeving or Ryvid). Dragging a heavy extension cord across a city sidewalk is rarely practical.
2. **The Highway Penalty:** Electric motorcycles are highly efficient in the city due to regenerative braking, but sustained speeds over 60 mph deplete batteries rapidly. If your daily commute involves more than 10 miles of highway, ensure you buy a bike with a larger battery (like the LiveWire S2 Del Mar or Zero FXE).

--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
Renting a large private property for a group of 20 or more guests from the UK offers fantastic options across Europe. Whether you’re planning a milestone birthday, a family reunion, or a getaway with friends, continental Europe and the British Isles have incredible châteaux, fincas, and estates tailored for large groups.

Here is a guide to the **best regions, property types, specialist booking agencies, and key planning tips**.

---

### 1. Popular Destinations & Property Styles for 20+ Guests

#### **France: Classic Châteaux & Country Estates**
* **Regions:** Dordogne, Loire Valley, Provence, Gascony, French Alps.
* **Why it works:** France has an abundance of authentic châteaux and grand country manors built to host large groups. Many come with extensive grounds, private pools, tennis courts, and even private chapels or grand dining halls.
* **Travel from UK:** Easy drive via Eurotunnel/ferries for Northern/Central France, or short flights to Bordeaux, Toulouse, Marseille, or Nice.

#### **Italy: Tuscan Estates & Puglian Masserias**
* **Regions:** Tuscany, Umbria, Puglia, Lake Como, Sicily.
* **Why it works:** Italian *agriturismos* (estate compounds) or renovated *masserias* (fortified farmhouses in Puglia) often feature multiple cottages centered around a main courtyard and pool. This offers a balance of communal gathering spaces and private sleeping quarters.
* **Travel from UK:** Frequent direct flights to Pisa, Florence, Bari, Brindisi, Naples, or Milan.

#### **Spain & Balearics: Fincas & Coastal Villas**
* **Regions:** Andalusia (Ronda/Marbella hinterland), Mallorca, Ibiza, Costa Brava.
* **Why it works:** Traditional Spanish *fincas* (rural estates) and large modern Balearic villas cater very well to indoor-outdoor living, with expansive terrace dining areas and outdoor kitchens.
* **Travel from UK:** Direct budget and flag-carrier flights from almost every major UK airport to Malaga, Palma, Alicante, or Barcelona.

#### **Portugal: Quintas & Douro Valley Manors**
* **Regions:** The Algarve, Douro Valley, Sintra/Lisbon Coast.
* **Why it works:** Portuguese *quintas* (historic countryside estates) offer great value, excellent wine/food options, and stunning vineyard or coastal views.
* **Travel from UK:** Direct flights to Faro, Porto, or Lisbon.

#### **UK & Ireland: Manor Houses & Castles (No Flying Needed)**
* **Regions:** Cotswolds, Scottish Highlands, Cornwall, Lake District, Southwest Ireland.
* **Why it works:** If you want to avoid flights, renting a historic UK manor house or Scottish castle (e.g., in Perthshire or Argyll) provides a magnificent experience with grand reception rooms and open fires.

### 3. Key Planning Tips for 20+ Guests

* **Bedroom/Bathroom Ratio:** Make sure the listing specifies individual beds vs. sofa beds, and check how many bedrooms are en-suite. For 20 guests, aim for at least 8 to 10 bedrooms.
* **Layout & Privacy:** Look for properties structured as a "main house + guest annexes" if you have guests who appreciate quiet (e.g., families with infants or elderly relatives).
* **Dining Facilities:** Confirm the property has a dining table (or outdoor terrace table) that can seat **all 20+ people together at once**.
* **Catering Options:** Cooking for 20 people every day can feel like work. Look for properties that offer options for a local private chef to cater arrival dinners or daily breakfasts.
* **Noise Regulations:** If you plan on playing music outdoors past 10 PM, check local regulations. Some regions (especially in Mallorca, Ibiza, and urban France) have strict noise ordinances.
* **Booking Lead Time:** Estates accommodating 20+ guests are limited. For summer trips, it’s best to book **10 to 14 months in advance**.

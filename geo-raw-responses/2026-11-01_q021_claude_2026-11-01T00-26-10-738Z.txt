--- RAW RESPONSE TEXT ---
Oliver's Travels looks like a legitimate, established company, and most customers are happy. The main complaints are about villa quality and how much help you get when something goes wrong.

**Positives**
- Trustpilot shows a high score. 
One page lists 3,432 reviews with a 4.8 out of 5 rating
, and 
another shows 3,608 reviews
.
- Many reviewers praise the booking process and the staff. 
One describes a third booking with them as excellent, and says cancelling an earlier one was easy.

- Many customers come back. 
One reviewer was so happy with last year's holiday that they went straight back to the website this year.

- Staff can sort out pricing problems. 
In one case the owners wanted more than the advertised price, and the Oliver's rep got the villa at the advertised price.


**Complaints**
- **Villa quality doesn't always match the marketing.** 
The company says its villas are the "top 2%" and are thoroughly inspected.
 Some guests disagree. 
One reviewer called their villa a "House of Horrors".
 
A Feefo reviewer said their villa was "over marketed" given how basic it was.

- **Limited help once you've booked.** 
One reviewer says Oliver's wash their hands of customer concerns and leave you to sort it out with the owners.
 
Another says they got no redress and the same listing was still being used.

- **Several parties involved.** 
One reviewer dealt with three different agencies, which they found confusing.

- **Pricing.** Some older Tripadvisor posts, roughly a decade old by the search metadata, complain of 
20–29% markups
 and 
hidden fees in the small print
. These are old, so treat them with caution. It's still worth comparing prices elsewhere.

**Tips before booking**
- Read the **recent 1–3 star reviews** for the specific villa, not just the overall score. Several complaints are about individual properties.
- Check the **cancellation and deposit terms** before you pay. Older complaints about lockdown refunds show why this matters.
- Check what's **included** (cleaning, linen, pool heating) and the **total price**.
- If you can, **pay by credit card** for Section 75 protection on purchases over £100. One older review said cards weren't accepted, so check the current payment options.
- Search for the villa's name and address. Some owners also list on other sites, which lets you compare prices and find extra reviews.
- Ask about **local support** on arrival and what happens if the villa isn't as described.
